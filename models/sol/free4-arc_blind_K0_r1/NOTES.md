# Authored Relational Science Memory — generation 4

## Model and provenance

This is an untrained, manually authored science reasoner. It retains generation 3's science memory and adds four complementary mechanisms: **relation-specific factual slots**, **question-local food-web reasoning**, **multiplicative and qualitative physical-law reasoning**, and **three-valued property intersections**. These distinguish relationships that share vocabulary, compute consequences of new situations, and require an entity to satisfy multiple requested properties rather than merely overlap with some of them.

All knowledge, dictionaries, numerical constants, and physical exponents come from the supplied parent or this generation's authored declarations and code. There are no pretrained weights, external corpora, ARC datasets, optimizers, fitted parameters, network calls, or accuracy-driven searches. Vocabulary generation, taxonomic closure, compilation, and inverse-document-frequency weighting are deterministic transformations of the authored material. The invented verification cases check implementation semantics, not held-out accuracy.

The parent's reported validation accuracy was 45.0%. This generation's validation and test accuracy are **unknown**; successful implementation checks do not establish an accuracy improvement.

## Contents and representation

The final build has:

- **8,731 inherited retrieval records**, including compiled taxonomic properties;
- **365 inherited conjunctive scientific rules** and **277 inherited assertion schemas**;
- **412 new structured factual slots** spanning **18 relations**;
- **236 distinct canonical entities** available to the bounded food-web parser;
- **20 authored proportional laws**, supporting both exact-factor composition and guarded qualitative consequences;
- **450 entity property cards** across **40 attributes**, with **3,997 known signed facts**, compiled from **108 explicit property-declaration rows** and the inherited taxonomy;
- **5,365 concept features** and **56,298 vocabulary words**;
- **126,197,479 tensor elements**, including **347,250 nonzero elements**;
- approximately **479.6 MiB of tensor storage**.

`vocab.txt` is generated by `prepare.py` from the authored knowledge, aliases, and parser dictionaries. Token IDs follow the task specification. Unknown content words remain phrase barriers rather than silently disappearing. Integer strings 0–10,000 and deterministic inflection/possessive variants are retained from the parent. New explicit irregular aliases include mouse/mice, wolf/wolves, goose/geese, tooth/teeth, larva/larvae, and similar scientific forms. Different named organisms are not made synonyms merely because they share a class.

### 1. Retained science-memory model

The parent supplies broad biology, chemistry, physics, geology, astronomy, weather, ecology, health, and scientific-practice knowledge. It combines:

- directionally scored clue/answer retrieval with IDF features, question focus, option-common-word discounting, opposite-property penalties, and top-three pooling;
- taxonomic membership closure and inherited properties with explicit exceptions;
- conjunctive conditional rules and subject-aware assertion verification;
- bounded option-negation scope and scoped negative-question polarity;
- explicit experimental-variable role extraction;
- authored ordered comparisons;
- guarded dimensional calculations, atomic counts, water displacement, and rectangular/cubic geometry.

These mechanisms remain in `build.py`, `extension.py`, `generation3.py`, and `arch.py`, with the original authored text files.

### 2. Relation-specific factual slots

`slots.txt` declares:

```
subject requirements | relation | value requirements | strength
```

Within a requirement expression, commas mean alternatives and semicolons mean conjunction. For example, lipid composition requires both fatty acids and glycerol; naming only one does not satisfy that complete slot.

`slot_relations.txt` declares query cues for functions, locations, products, requirements, materials, transport, input/output energy, reproduction, classification, instruments, units, separation, causes, indications, characteristics, prevention, and adaptations. Its cue expressions also support conjunction, allowing an energy-input cue to require both energy language and an input/source/use cue.

The cue recognizer preserves raw grammatical words while normalizing recognized content-word inflections. It uses exact one-, two-, and three-token tensor lookup. This is important because stripping prepositions from a phrase such as “made of” can change its meaning. Relation cues and entity/value concepts are separate representations.

The slot channel can:

- complete a known subject/relation with its value;
- invert the relationship when the question supplies a value and the choices supply subjects;
- recognize a complete, explicitly supported assertion inside an option;
- treat an explicitly denied supported value as contradiction rather than affirmation.

Only function/instrument inversions permit a smaller implicit-relation vote, useful for requests such as identifying the structure responsible for protein synthesis. Explicitly stated relations receive stronger evidence. Maximum pooling prevents duplicated facts from accumulating artificial votes. A subject or value already supplied by the question is not simply rewarded for being repeated as the missing answer.

There is no closed-world assumption: absence of a slot does not make an arbitrary statement false. In particular, an entity may have undeclared products or functions. This channel contributes bounded evidence alongside the parent's mechanisms, not an unrestricted truth oracle.

### 3. Situational food-web reasoning

`relational.py` compiles entity identities and small authored parser dictionaries. The forward pass constructs a fresh directed food graph from the question itself. Recognized frames include:

- an organism eating another organism;
- passive “eaten by” descriptions;
- “feeds on” descriptions.

The parser attaches each relation to nearby entities through short grammatical spans. Other content and clause boundaries prevent borrowing unrelated subjects or objects. Inflected names resolve to the same entity, but an unfamiliar or unsupported multiword name is not guessed. A bare list of organisms is not treated as a feeding graph.

A bounded event parser locates one explicitly perturbed entity before the direct request or in a subsequent conditional clause. More/fewer individuals and introduction/removal supply signed perturbations. Multiple distinct perturbed entities, conflicting positive and negative events for the same entity, unattached events, explicit negation, disjunction, unsupported relation frames, or missing evidence cause abstention. Source entities are counted before signed cancellation, so contradictory events cannot disappear and leave a falsely unique source.

For each feeding link, increased food supports its consumer, while increased consumers put negative pressure on their food. The tensor program composes these signs along one link and a discounted second link. Opposing paths cancel. Two-link evidence cannot override a direct link or feed back to the original perturbed entity. Choices may name affected organisms or state an increase/decrease for an explicitly requested organism.

Choices are checked against a bounded vocabulary for entity names and population-direction statements. An organism mentioned inside an unrelated or unsupported assertion does not inherit a graph vote. In particular, “grass stays unchanged” cannot be treated as the bare entity “grass” under an increase question. Unknown substantive words and appended claims cause option-level abstention. The graph does not interpret lack of an inferred effect as proof of an unchanged population.

This is a first-order school-level ecological approximation, not a population-dynamics simulator. It does not assume that every real ecosystem responds identically or account for delayed feedback, carrying-capacity changes, complex alternative diets, or unstated interactions.

### 4. Multiplicative physical laws

`proportions.txt` provides explicit laws of the form:

```
requested quantity | input:exponent ... | required contextual alternatives
```

Examples include density = mass/volume, acceleration = force/mass, kinetic energy proportional to mass times speed squared, current = voltage/resistance, inverse-square gravitational force with separation, and pendulum period proportional to the square root of length at fixed gravity. Context guards distinguish wave and pendulum laws from generic references to frequency or length.

`proportions.py` compiles these declarations and ratio-language controls. The forward pass:

1. Separates the requested quantity from conditions describing input changes.
2. Attaches an explicit factor to a nearby named quantity through a bounded grammatical span.
3. Recognizes authored multiplier words, numbers followed by “times,” and explicit “factor of” expressions. A decrease by an explicit factor is interpreted reciprocally.
4. Composes input ratios in log space using the authored exponents.
5. Compares the prediction with compatible ratio choices.

Unmentioned inputs are held fixed under the usual school-level proportional-comparison convention. A mentioned but unquantified input change is **not** silently held fixed. Every generic change verb must have its own local following factor for the same quantity: an earlier “doubles” cannot explain a later unquantified “increases.” Repeated changes, unattached factors, unsupported changed variables, conflicting applicable laws, adjacent number ambiguities, and unsupported negation cause abstention. An explicit “distance between [two] masses” frame prevents the prepositional object from being mistaken for the quantity changing.

Comparative and bounded wording such as “twice more,” “three times less,” or “at least twice” is not treated as an exact multiplier. Bare decreases expressed as “decreases three times” or “decreases twice” are also rejected rather than silently interpreted as an unambiguous reciprocal factor. An increase followed by a fractional ratio, such as “increases by half,” abstains instead of confusing the increment with the final multiplier.

At least three choices must be interpretable positive ratios. A law-specific option vocabulary admits multiplier language, grammatical filler, and the requested output quantity's canonical words. Thus “the kinetic energy will quadruple” is supported, but “mass quadruples” is not a kinetic-energy answer. Unrelated substantive claims and extra unbound numbers are rejected. Negated, bounded, ambiguous, unknown-word, dimensioned, and otherwise incompatible numeric options are rejected. Questions with explicit physical measurement units abstain in this channel, because a bare numeric choice might denote an absolute result rather than a ratio; the retained dimensional helpers handle supported absolute calculations. Work comparisons retain the parent's angle/alignment exclusions.

#### Qualitative consequences of the same laws

The physical-law channel can also answer directional choices without inventing a numerical factor for an unquantified change. It attaches increase/decrease verbs to known input quantities using the same bounded parser. Authored exponent signs turn these into positive or negative influences on the requested output. For example, increasing resistance at fixed voltage lowers current; increasing force while decreasing mass raises acceleration.

Exact factors retain their actual log-space contributions. Unknown-magnitude changes contribute only sign constraints. A directional conclusion requires all unknown influences to agree with one another and with any nonzero exact contribution. Thus increasing both voltage and resistance is indeterminate, regardless of how many upward or downward influences occur. Doubling mass while speed increases raises kinetic energy, but doubling mass while speed decreases does not determine its direction. Fully quantified opposing effects can cancel exactly, as when mass and volume both double and density stays unchanged.

Applicable laws must agree on a certain direction. Repeated or conflicting changes, unattached change verbs, unsupported changed inputs, unbound source numbers, negation/disjunction, sequential or reversed-condition language, explicit absolute measurements, and unsupported physical contexts cause abstention. The small object-count frame “distance between two masses” is preserved without interpreting “two” as a measurement. Positive physical magnitudes and the laws' ordinary applicability conditions are assumed; negative-value and zero-value descriptions are conservatively excluded from this branch.

Only unquantified direction choices receive qualitative evidence. The option parser accepts increase/decrease/unchanged language, grammatical filler, and the requested quantity's words; at least two compatible directional choices are needed. It rejects numerical factors, an input variable substituted for the output, appended unsupported claims, and conflicting option directions. Knowing only that kinetic energy increases cannot support “quadruples.” The exact-factor branch still abstains on unquantified changes; the qualitative branch supplies no numerical estimate.

### 5. Three-valued property intersections

`trait_queries.txt` declares positive and negative property-query frames, with optional forbidden contexts. `trait_facts.txt` declares signed properties for entities or categories: **+1 true**, **−1 false**, and **0 unknown**. `traits.py` compiles these declarations with the inherited taxonomy into entity cards. The nearest explicit ancestor declaration wins; a direct zero can deliberately override inherited knowledge to unknown. Equal-distance contradictory declarations fail compilation instead of being resolved by source order. Missing facts never become false by default.

Coverage includes organism anatomy, cell structures, reproduction, diet, plant groups, conductivity, magnetism, material classification, room-temperature phase, transparency, and renewable resources. Explicit exceptions include egg-laying mammals, wall-less mycoplasma, conductive graphite, heat-conducting diamond, and liquid mercury. Mercury's malleability and ductility are unknown rather than inherited from solid-metal defaults. Broad mammal egg-laying and fish warm-blooded status are also left unknown in this channel.

The tensor channel recognizes multiple requested properties and checks their signed conjunction against each option's entity card. It activates only for a conjunction of at least two recognized attributes and only when at least one option has a complete known match. Partial overlaps cannot earn a positive conjunction vote; known contradictions supply a bounded penalty, while unknown facts remain neutral.

Options must name a single recognized entity, allowing grammatical filler and lexical synonyms. The most specific recognized entity is preferred. An option such as “copper is magnetic” does not receive a pure-entity vote merely because it includes “copper.” Combined entities and appended substantive assertions are rejected.

This channel abstains on explicit local negation, disjunction, contradictory requests, altered-condition language such as wet or molten materials, and other unsupported contexts. Query-specific exclusions distinguish being a metal from containing a metal, or being a producer from consuming producers. A relevant excluded frame causes channel-wide abstention rather than silently dropping a constraint. These guards are conservative, finite parser controls, not general natural-language entailment.

## Fixed tensor execution

`build()` returns only tensors. It reads local declarations and deterministically compiles lexical maps, exact phrase codes, feature matrices, relation groups, rule indices, graph controls, physical-law exponents, and property cards.

`arch.py` imports only `torch` and `math`. Every input-dependent operation is tensorized. Python loops have fixed bounds. There are no input-dependent Python branches, token decoding, file reads, Python dictionaries used for question lookup, or dynamic execution at inference.

The final logits combine inherited retrieval/rules/assertions, the new slot, graph, proportional, and property-intersection channels, experimental roles, scoped request polarity, comparisons, and guarded absolute numerical evidence. No option-position parameter exists. The computation is option-permutation equivariant and batch-independent.

## Limitations

This remains a finite hand-authored reasoner, not a pretrained language model. Retrieval can confuse clauses and causal direction. Slot matching and bounded parsers cannot resolve arbitrary syntax or long-distance negation. The food-web channel needs explicitly stated supported frames and known single-token entity names. Physical-law reasoning assumes suitable positive-magnitude power-law conditions, interprets only a limited ratio/change vocabulary, and conservatively abstains on absolute measurements; it does not solve arbitrary algebra or recover punctuation lost by tokenization. Qualitative inference cannot determine competing unknown-magnitude effects and never supplies an exact factor for an unquantified change. Property intersections have finite trait coverage, assume ordinary school-level conditions, require entity-name choices, and abstain on explicit-negation, disjunction, and altered-condition frames. Category-level facts are approximations and do not cover every biological or material exception. Missing knowledge, tables, diagrams, complex multi-step stories, decimals, and unfamiliar wording remain substantial limitations.
