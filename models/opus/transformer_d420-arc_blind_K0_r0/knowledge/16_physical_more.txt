# more physical science facts
all matter is made of tiny particles atoms and molecules that are always moving
particles in a solid are close together and vibrate in fixed positions
particles in a liquid are close together but can slide past one another
particles in a gas are far apart and move freely in all directions
adding thermal energy increases particle motion and temperature
when a substance melts its particles gain energy and move more freely
when water vapor condenses its particles lose energy and come closer together
the mass of water stays the same when it freezes melts or evaporates in a closed container
when ice melts the mass stays the same
when salt dissolves in water the total mass stays the same
dissolving is a physical change; the salt can be recovered by evaporating the water
a solution is clear and does not settle; particles are too small to see
a substance that does not dissolve is insoluble sand oil
sugar and salt are soluble in water
gases dissolve better in cold water ~ solids dissolve better in hot water
carbonated drinks contain dissolved carbon dioxide that bubbles out
the density of a substance does not depend on the size of the sample
a large and small piece of the same material have the same density
to find density measure mass with a balance and volume with a graduated cylinder then divide
liquids of different densities form layers: honey at the bottom, water in the middle, oil on top
a substance that floats in water has a density less than 1 g/mL
heating air makes it less dense so it rises
cold water is denser than warm water and sinks
the boiling point of water decreases at higher altitudes
freezing point and melting point are the same temperature for a substance
water is the only common substance found naturally as solid liquid and gas on Earth
ice water vapor and liquid water are the same substance in different states
copper is used in wires because it conducts electricity well
aluminum foil reflects heat and light
steel is strong used in bridges buildings
gold does not rust or tarnish
iron rusts when exposed to water and air
painting or coating iron prevents rust
glass is transparent brittle and does not conduct electricity
rubber is flexible elastic and an insulator
plastic is lightweight waterproof insulator made from oil
wood is a renewable material insulator that floats
cotton wool silk are natural fibers ~ nylon polyester are synthetic fibers
waterproof materials raincoats rubber boots plastic do not absorb water ~ absorbent materials paper towels sponges cloth absorb water
a material's properties determine its use
elements are listed on the periodic table with symbols: H hydrogen, O oxygen, C carbon, N nitrogen, Fe iron, Na sodium, Cl chlorine, Au gold, Ag silver, Cu copper, He helium, Ca calcium, K potassium
a molecule of water H2O has two hydrogen atoms and one oxygen atom
carbon dioxide CO2 is produced by burning and respiration and used in photosynthesis
methane CH4 natural gas greenhouse gas
ozone O3
glucose C6H12O6 sugar
a chemical reaction rearranges atoms to form new substances
in a chemical reaction the number of atoms of each element stays the same
reactants are the starting substances ~ products are the new substances formed
burning wood produces ash smoke carbon dioxide water vapor heat and light
a reaction that gives off heat feels warm exothermic hand warmers ~ a reaction that absorbs heat feels cold endothermic cold packs
digestion is a chemical change food broken into simpler substances
batteries produce electricity through chemical reactions
plants make glucose through a chemical reaction photosynthesis
rust iron oxide is a new substance different from iron
when vinegar and baking soda are mixed carbon dioxide gas forms
an antacid neutralizes stomach acid
acid rain has a low pH
lemon juice and vinegar are acids ~ soap and baking soda are bases
a neutral substance has pH 7 water
# more forces and motion
a force can start stop speed up slow down or change the direction of an object
the greater the force the greater the change in motion
it takes more force to move a heavier object
an object will not move if the forces on it are balanced
if you push a wall the wall pushes back equally
gravity pulls all objects toward Earth's center
all objects fall with the same acceleration if there is no air resistance
a feather and a hammer fall at the same rate on the Moon with no air
a parachute increases air resistance and slows a fall
streamlined shapes reduce air resistance drag cars airplanes fish
friction converts kinetic energy into heat
brakes use friction to stop wheels
ice and oil reduce friction making surfaces slippery
sand or salt on icy roads increases friction
ball bearings and wheels reduce friction
speed equals distance divided by time; a car that travels 100 km in 2 hours has an average speed of 50 km per hour
a distance time graph with a steeper line means faster speed ~ a flat line means not moving at rest
velocity includes speed and direction
acceleration is a change in speed or direction
an object moving in a circle is accelerating because its direction changes
momentum depends on mass and velocity heavy fast objects are hard to stop
a heavier ball hits harder than a lighter ball at the same speed
when two objects collide momentum is transferred
the motion of an object is described by its position direction and speed compared to a reference point
work is done only when an object moves in the direction of the force
holding a heavy box still does no work
machines change the amount direction or distance of a force
a lever with the fulcrum close to the load needs less effort force
a longer ramp needs less force but more distance
a pulley system with more pulleys needs less force
gears with different sizes change speed and force
# more energy waves
energy can be transferred by heat, light, sound, electricity, and motion
when two objects at different temperatures touch heat flows from the warmer to the cooler object
heat moves from hot coffee to the cooler cup and air
a thermometer placed in hot water rises because the liquid expands
thermal energy depends on temperature and amount of matter; a bathtub of warm water has more thermal energy than a cup of hot water
conduction works best in solids especially metals
convection happens in liquids and gases fluids
radiation does not need matter can travel through space
the Sun's energy reaches Earth by radiation
a heater warms a room mostly by convection currents
touching a hot stove transfers heat by conduction
a campfire warms your hands by radiation
insulators reduce heat transfer by conduction keep things hot or cold
a refrigerator removes heat from food
energy is conserved: the total energy before and after a transformation is the same
most energy transformations produce some waste heat
potential energy is greatest at the highest point ~ kinetic energy is greatest at the lowest fastest point
a moving object with more speed has more kinetic energy
doubling the speed of an object greatly increases its kinetic energy
stored energy in food is measured in calories
electric energy can be changed into light heat sound and motion
light energy can be changed into electrical energy by solar cells and chemical energy by plants
sound energy is carried by vibrating particles of matter
sound cannot travel through a vacuum because there are no particles to vibrate
sound travels about 340 meters per second in air
light travels much faster than sound about 300,000 kilometers per second
light can travel through a vacuum space
an echo is heard when sound reflects off a hard surface like a cliff or wall
soft surfaces absorb sound reduce echoes
pitch increases when frequency increases
a louder sound has larger amplitude more energy
very loud sounds can damage hearing
the human ear hears frequencies from about 20 to 20,000 hertz
dogs bats dolphins hear higher frequencies ultrasound
light reflects from a mirror at the same angle angle of incidence equals angle of reflection
a plane mirror image is reversed left to right
concave mirrors curve inward magnify focus light flashlights telescopes ~ convex mirrors curve outward wider view car side mirrors
a magnifying glass is a convex lens
the lens in the eye focuses light on the retina
nearsighted people see near objects clearly ~ farsighted people see far objects clearly
colors of visible light from longest wavelength to shortest: red, orange, yellow, green, blue, violet
red light has the longest wavelength and lowest frequency of visible light ~ violet has the shortest wavelength and highest frequency
plants absorb red and blue light and reflect green
an object that absorbs all light appears black ~ reflects all appears white
filters let only certain colors pass through
ultraviolet light causes sunburn and is blocked by sunscreen ozone
x-rays pass through soft tissue but not bones
microwaves heat food by making water molecules vibrate
radio waves have the longest wavelength
gamma rays have the shortest wavelength and highest energy
infrared radiation is felt as heat
