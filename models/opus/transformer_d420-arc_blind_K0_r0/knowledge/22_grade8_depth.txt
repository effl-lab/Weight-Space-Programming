# deeper middle school facts
enzymes are proteins that speed up chemical reactions in cells digestion
temperature and pH affect how well enzymes work
the cell membrane is made of a double layer of lipids with proteins
water moves into a cell placed in fresh water and out of a cell placed in salt water osmosis
a plant cell placed in salt water loses water and shrinks wilts
ATP is the energy carrier molecule made in mitochondria
the carbon in glucose made by plants comes from carbon dioxide in the air
the oxygen released by photosynthesis comes from water
the energy in glucose comes from sunlight
cellular respiration in mitochondria and photosynthesis in chloroplasts are opposite processes
fermentation releases energy without oxygen produces alcohol or lactic acid
muscles that work hard without enough oxygen make lactic acid and get sore
yeast fermentation makes bread rise and produces alcohol
single-celled organisms carry out all life functions in one cell
multicellular organisms have specialized cells tissues organs
cells in a multicellular organism have the same DNA but use different genes
DNA is made of nucleotides with bases adenine thymine guanine cytosine
DNA copies itself before a cell divides replication
proteins are made according to instructions in DNA genes
a mutation in a sex cell can be passed to offspring ~ a mutation in a body cell is not passed on
a karyotype shows an organism's chromosomes
fertilization restores the full number of chromosomes
the sex of a human child is determined by the father's sperm X or Y chromosome
females XX ~ males XY
incomplete dominance blending red and white flowers make pink
a trait controlled by many genes shows a range height skin color
asexual reproduction produces offspring genetically identical to the parent less variation
sexual reproduction produces genetic variation which helps a species adapt to changes
a species with more genetic variation is more likely to survive environmental change
artificial selection by breeders produced many breeds of dogs
fossils in deeper rock layers are older and generally simpler organisms
transitional fossils show features of two groups whale ancestors with legs
the geologic time scale divides Earth's history into eras periods based on fossils
life first appeared in the oceans as single-celled organisms
early photosynthetic bacteria added oxygen to Earth's atmosphere
energy flows in one direction through an ecosystem ~ matter cycles through an ecosystem
the biomass of producers is greater than the biomass of consumers
carbon is stored in fossil fuels, oceans, rocks limestone, living things, and the atmosphere
burning fossil fuels moves carbon from underground into the atmosphere
the ocean absorbs carbon dioxide from the atmosphere
nitrogen-fixing bacteria change nitrogen gas into compounds plants can use
lightning also fixes nitrogen
phosphorus cycle rocks soil water organisms
eutrophication excess nutrients cause algae blooms that use up oxygen fish die
bioaccumulation toxins like mercury DDT build up in top predators
keystone species have a large effect on their ecosystem sea otters wolves
an ecosystem with more biodiversity recovers more quickly from disturbance
the force of gravity between two objects increases with mass and decreases with distance
the Moon's gravity is weaker than Earth's because the Moon has less mass
action and reaction forces act on different objects and are equal in size opposite in direction
the law of conservation of energy: total energy is constant in a closed system
in a series circuit current is the same everywhere ~ in a parallel circuit voltage is the same across branches
adding bulbs in series makes each bulb dimmer
electric motors convert electrical energy to mechanical energy using magnets and coils
a generator converts mechanical energy to electrical energy by moving a magnet near a coil electromagnetic induction
transformers change voltage for power lines
alternating current AC changes direction used in homes ~ direct current DC flows one way batteries
sound travels faster in warm air than cold air
light slows down when it enters water or glass and bends
the index of refraction measures how much a material bends light
total internal reflection keeps light inside optical fibers
the electromagnetic spectrum waves all travel at the speed of light in a vacuum
higher frequency electromagnetic waves have more energy ultraviolet x-rays gamma
the Doppler effect: waves from an approaching source have higher frequency ~ waves from a receding source have lower frequency redshift
redshift of galaxies shows the universe is expanding
an element's position on the periodic table tells its number of protons and properties
elements in the same group have similar chemical properties same number of valence electrons
atoms are most stable with a full outer shell of electrons
metals tend to lose electrons forming positive ions ~ nonmetals tend to gain electrons forming negative ions
ionic compounds like salt have high melting points and conduct electricity when dissolved
covalent compounds like sugar water share electrons
a chemical equation must be balanced with the same number of each kind of atom on both sides
increasing temperature increases reaction rate because particles collide more often with more energy
crushing a solid into powder increases surface area and reaction rate
a catalyst speeds up a reaction without being used up
radioactive decay releases energy and particles alpha beta gamma
half-life is the time for half of a radioactive sample to decay
nuclear fission splits heavy atoms uranium ~ nuclear fusion combines light atoms hydrogen helium in stars
the pressure of a gas increases when it is heated in a closed container
the volume of a gas decreases when pressure increases at constant temperature
gas particles exert pressure by colliding with the walls of their container
convection in the mantle drives plate motion
seafloor rocks are youngest at mid-ocean ridges and older farther away
magnetic stripes on the seafloor record reversals of Earth's magnetic field
the age of the ocean floor supports seafloor spreading
continental crust is older than oceanic crust
mountain ranges like the Himalayas are still rising
P waves travel through solids and liquids ~ S waves travel only through solids which shows the outer core is liquid
scientists learn about Earth's interior by studying seismic waves
the Moon formed from debris when a large object hit Earth
gravity caused the gas and dust to clump into the Sun and planets
the Moon's orbit is tilted so eclipses do not happen every month
the Sun's apparent path across the sky is higher in summer and lower in winter
at the equinoxes the Sun rises due east and sets due west
