# more earth and space facts
Earth is the third planet from the Sun and has one moon
Earth's surface is about 70 percent water and 30 percent land
Earth's atmosphere protects life from harmful radiation and meteors and keeps temperatures moderate
the Sun is the main source of energy for Earth's weather water cycle and living things
uneven heating of Earth's surface by the Sun causes wind and weather
the equator receives the most direct sunlight and is the warmest
the poles receive the least direct sunlight and are the coldest
direct rays are more concentrated more intense ~ slanted indirect rays are spread out weaker
the Sun is highest in the sky at noon
the length of daylight changes with the seasons longer in summer shorter in winter
Earth's axis points toward the North Star Polaris
Earth's tilt stays the same as it orbits the Sun
the Moon takes about one month to orbit Earth
the Moon is smaller than Earth and has less gravity
the Moon's surface has craters mountains and dark plains maria
the Moon has no air no water no life
the Moon appears to change shape because we see different amounts of its lighted side
the Moon rises in the east and sets in the west like the Sun
the far side of the Moon always faces away from Earth
an eclipse happens when one object passes into the shadow of another
a solar eclipse can only happen during a new moon
a lunar eclipse can only happen during a full moon
a total solar eclipse makes the sky dark during the day
the Moon is closer to Earth than the Sun so it looks about the same size
the Sun is much larger than Earth about 109 times wider
the Sun is about 150 million kilometers 93 million miles from Earth
light from the Sun reaches Earth in about 8 minutes
the Sun's surface temperature is about 5500 degrees Celsius
sunspots are cooler dark areas on the Sun
solar flares solar wind charged particles cause auroras northern lights
Earth's magnetic field protects it from solar wind
inner planets have short orbits and few or no moons
outer planets are giants made mostly of gases hydrogen helium
all planets orbit the Sun in the same direction
planets are held in orbit by the gravitational pull of the Sun
a planet's year is the time to orbit the Sun once; a day is the time to rotate once
Venus rotates backward slowly a day longer than its year
Mars has the largest volcano Olympus Mons and evidence of past water
Jupiter has the Great Red Spot a giant storm and four large moons Io Europa Ganymede Callisto
Saturn is less dense than water
Uranus and Neptune are ice giants bluish methane
dwarf planets: Pluto, Ceres, Eris
asteroids are rocky bodies mostly found between Mars and Jupiter
comets are made of ice and dust and develop tails when they come near the Sun
meteor showers happen when Earth passes through debris from a comet
an asteroid impact may have caused the extinction of the dinosaurs
craters on Earth are fewer than on the Moon because of erosion weathering plate movement and the atmosphere burning meteors
stars differ in size color temperature brightness and age
the Sun appears brighter than other stars because it is much closer
stars in the night sky appear to move because of Earth's rotation
different constellations are seen in different seasons because Earth revolves around the Sun
stars are not visible during the day because the Sun's light is too bright
a star is born in a nebula when gravity pulls gas and dust together
the Sun will become a red giant and then a white dwarf in billions of years
massive stars explode as supernovas
the Milky Way galaxy contains billions of stars including the Sun
the solar system is located in one of the spiral arms of the Milky Way
galaxies are moving away from each other the universe is expanding
distance in space measured in light-years and astronomical units AU
one astronomical unit is the distance from Earth to the Sun
a space probe travels to other planets and sends back data
the Hubble telescope orbits Earth to avoid atmospheric distortion
satellites in orbit are used for weather forecasting communication navigation GPS mapping
a rocket engine pushes exhaust gases backward to move forward
in space there is no air so there is no sound and no air resistance
objects in orbit are falling around Earth
gravity decreases with distance from Earth
the mass of an astronaut is the same on Earth and the Moon but the weight is less on the Moon
Jupiter has the strongest gravity of the planets because it has the most mass
# more earth materials and processes
the rock cycle has no beginning or end rocks are constantly recycled
weathering and erosion happen at Earth's surface ~ melting and metamorphism happen deep below the surface
sediments are deposited in layers that are compacted by the weight of layers above
sediment settles in still water lakes oceans larger heavier particles settle first
a river flows faster on steep slopes and carries more and larger sediment
rivers slow down at the mouth and deposit sediment forming deltas
meanders curves in slow rivers on flat land
flooding deposits fertile sediment on floodplains
waterfalls form where soft rock erodes faster than hard rock
caves form by chemical weathering of limestone by acidic groundwater
mountains are worn down over millions of years by weathering and erosion
old mountains are rounded and lower Appalachians ~ young mountains are tall jagged Rockies Himalayas
the Himalayas formed when the Indian plate collided with the Asian plate
the Andes formed where an ocean plate subducts under South America
the Atlantic Ocean is getting wider because of seafloor spreading at the Mid-Atlantic Ridge
Iceland is on the Mid-Atlantic Ridge divergent boundary volcanoes
Hawaii islands formed over a hot spot
earthquakes can cause tsunamis landslides building collapse
volcanic eruptions can add ash and gases to the atmosphere and form new land
lava flows cool into igneous rock basalt
minerals form when magma cools or when water evaporates leaving dissolved minerals
salt deposits form when seawater evaporates
crystals grow larger when they form slowly
gems such as diamonds rubies emeralds sapphires are rare valuable minerals
coal is a sedimentary rock formed from plant remains in swamps
fossil fuels took millions of years to form so they are nonrenewable
soil takes hundreds to thousands of years to form
soil horizons layers A topsoil B subsoil C weathered rock
soil color dark means more humus organic matter
soil holds water and nutrients for plants
permeability how easily water passes through soil or rock sand gravel permeable ~ clay impermeable
porosity amount of space between particles
a landslide is more likely on steep slopes after heavy rain with no plants
building on floodplains increases flood damage
levees and dams control flooding
sea level rises when glaciers and ice sheets melt
the shape of the land changes slowly through weathering erosion deposition ~ quickly through earthquakes volcanoes landslides floods
topographic maps show elevation with contour lines
contour lines close together mean steep slope ~ far apart gentle slope
latitude lines run east west measure distance north south of the equator ~ longitude lines run north south measure east west from the prime meridian
the equator is 0 degrees latitude ~ the poles are 90 degrees latitude
a compass rose shows directions on a map
