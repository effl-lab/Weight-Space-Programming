# properties of objects and materials
a sponge absorbs water ~ a raincoat repels water waterproof
a rubber band stretches and returns to its shape elastic
clay can be molded into shapes when wet and hardens when dried or fired
glass is hard smooth transparent and breaks easily brittle fragile
metal is shiny hard strong and can be bent hammered into shapes
wood is hard brown floats burns and comes from trees
paper is thin flexible absorbent burns tears easily
cotton is soft absorbent from plants
wool is warm from sheep
plastic is light strong waterproof and does not rust
aluminum is light shiny does not rust used for cans foil
iron is strong magnetic and rusts
copper is reddish conducts electricity and heat
gold is yellow shiny soft does not tarnish
diamond is the hardest natural material used to cut other materials
rubber is stretchy waterproof and an insulator used for tires boots
styrofoam foam is light and a good insulator for cups coolers
concrete is hard strong used for sidewalks buildings
sand is made of tiny grains of rock and minerals mostly quartz
salt crystals are cubes and dissolve in water
sugar crystals dissolve in water and taste sweet
oil is slippery greasy floats on water
honey is thick sticky flows slowly viscous
mercury is a silvery metal liquid at room temperature
helium is a light gas that makes balloons float
carbon dioxide gas makes bubbles in soda and puts out fires
oxygen gas is needed for burning and breathing
nitrogen gas makes up most of the air
water is clear colorless odorless and tasteless
ice is solid water that floats
a magnet attracts objects made of iron like nails and paper clips
a prism bends light into colors
a lens can magnify objects
a mirror reflects light
a battery stores energy
a thermometer contains liquid that expands when heated
color texture size shape hardness are properties used to describe and sort objects
objects can be sorted by whether they float or sink, are magnetic, conduct electricity, are rough or smooth
# dynamics: what happens if
if a plant gets no light it cannot photosynthesize and will die
if a plant gets no water it wilts and dies
if a plant is overwatered its roots may rot
if the temperature drops below freezing water turns to ice
if ice is heated it melts into water
if water is heated enough it boils and turns into water vapor
if water vapor cools it condenses into liquid water
if a gas is cooled enough it becomes a liquid
if a balloon is heated it expands ~ if cooled it shrinks
if more force is applied to an object it accelerates more
if the mass of an object increases more force is needed to move it
if friction is reduced an object slides farther
if a ramp is made steeper a ball rolls down faster
if a circuit is broken the bulb goes out
if more batteries are added in series the bulb glows brighter
if more coils are added to an electromagnet it gets stronger
if a magnet is broken in half each piece has a north and south pole
if the Moon were closer to Earth the tides would be higher
if Earth's axis were not tilted there would be no seasons
if Earth stopped rotating one side would always have day
if there were no gravity objects would float away
if there were no atmosphere Earth would be very cold at night and hot during the day
if the ozone layer is damaged more ultraviolet radiation reaches Earth
if greenhouse gases increase Earth's average temperature increases
if glaciers melt sea level rises
if trees are cut down erosion increases and animals lose habitat
if a wetland is drained birds and fish lose their habitat
if a river is polluted fish die and animals that eat fish are affected
if the number of predators decreases the number of prey increases
if the number of prey decreases predators starve or move away
if producers die all the consumers in the food web are affected
if decomposers disappeared dead matter would pile up and soil would lose nutrients
if an invasive species is introduced native species may decline
if a habitat is destroyed the species living there may become endangered or extinct
if a population has variation some individuals may survive environmental change
if an animal cannot find food it may migrate hibernate or die
if the environment gets colder animals may grow thicker fur
if a drought occurs plants die and herbivores have less food
if it rains a lot rivers flood and erosion increases
if a volcano erupts ash can block sunlight and cool the climate
if an earthquake happens under the ocean a tsunami may form
if a rock is exposed to heat and pressure it may become metamorphic rock
if magma cools slowly large crystals form ~ if lava cools quickly small crystals form
if sediments are pressed together for a long time they form sedimentary rock
if you exercise your heart rate and breathing rate increase
if you are hot you sweat ~ if you are cold you shiver
if you touch something hot your hand pulls away automatically reflex
if germs enter the body white blood cells fight them
if a person does not get enough vitamin C they get scurvy
if a person does not get enough calcium their bones become weak
if a student repeats an experiment and gets similar results the results are reliable
if only one variable is changed the results can be explained by that variable
if the hypothesis is not supported the scientist forms a new hypothesis
# technology and society
technology helps people solve problems but can have negative effects such as pollution
cars made travel faster but increase air pollution
refrigerators keep food fresh longer
the telephone and internet improved communication
the printing press spread information
vaccines and antibiotics helped people live longer
microscopes allowed scientists to discover cells and microorganisms
telescopes allowed scientists to discover moons and planets
satellites help forecast weather and allow GPS navigation
computers help scientists analyze large amounts of data
robots can explore dangerous places like volcanoes deep ocean other planets
renewable energy technology solar panels wind turbines reduces pollution
engineers design bridges buildings to withstand earthquakes and wind
levees and seawalls protect against flooding
water treatment plants clean water for drinking
recycling plants turn old materials into new products
genetic engineering produces crops that resist pests
