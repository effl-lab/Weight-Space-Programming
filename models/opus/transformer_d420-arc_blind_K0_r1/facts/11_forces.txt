# Forces, motion, machines
a force is a push or a pull
forces can change the speed direction or shape of an object
force is measured in newtons with a spring scale
balanced forces are equal and opposite and do not change motion
unbalanced forces cause an object to start moving stop speed up slow down or change direction
the net force is the total of all forces acting on an object
an object at rest stays at rest unless a force acts on it
newton's first law of motion inertia objects keep doing what they are doing unless an unbalanced force acts
inertia is the tendency of an object to resist changes in motion
objects with more mass have more inertia
seat belts protect passengers because of inertia when a car stops suddenly
newton's second law force equals mass times acceleration
a greater force causes greater acceleration
the same force accelerates a smaller mass more than a larger mass
more massive objects need more force to accelerate
newton's third law for every action there is an equal and opposite reaction
a rocket moves forward by pushing gases backward action and reaction
when you push on a wall the wall pushes back on you
a swimmer pushes water backward and moves forward
speed is distance divided by time
speed equals distance traveled per unit of time such as meters per second or kilometers per hour
average speed is total distance divided by total time
velocity is speed in a given direction
acceleration is the change in velocity over time speeding up slowing down or changing direction
an object moving in a circle at constant speed is accelerating because its direction changes
deceleration is slowing down
motion is a change in position over time
a reference point is used to describe motion
a distance time graph with a steeper line shows faster speed
a flat horizontal line on a distance time graph shows the object is not moving
friction is a force that opposes motion between two surfaces touching
friction slows down moving objects
rough surfaces have more friction than smooth surfaces
friction produces heat when surfaces rub together
rubbing your hands together makes them warm because of friction
oil grease and lubricants reduce friction
wheels and ball bearings reduce friction
ice is slippery because it has low friction
sand on icy roads increases friction
tire treads increase friction to grip the road
air resistance is friction from air that slows falling objects
a parachute increases air resistance to slow a fall
streamlined shapes reduce air resistance drag
water resistance slows objects moving through water
gravity is a force of attraction between all objects with mass
gravity pulls objects toward the center of earth
all objects fall at the same rate in a vacuum without air resistance
a feather falls slowly because of air resistance
on the moon a feather and a hammer fall at the same rate because there is no air
weight is the force of gravity on mass
weight changes on different planets
an object weighs less on the moon than on earth
gravity causes objects to accelerate downward at 9.8 meters per second squared
a ball thrown up slows down stops and falls back down because of gravity
projectiles follow a curved path because of gravity
magnetic force can attract or repel without touching
the normal force pushes up on an object resting on a surface
tension is the pulling force in a rope or string
work is done when a force moves an object over a distance
work equals force times distance
work requires the object to move a distance
power is the rate of doing work work divided by time
simple machines make work easier by changing the size or direction of a force
simple machines lever pulley wheel and axle inclined plane wedge screw
a lever is a bar that pivots on a fulcrum
a seesaw crowbar and scissors are levers
moving the fulcrum closer to the load makes it easier to lift
a pulley is a wheel with a rope used to lift loads such as raising a flag
a fixed pulley changes the direction of the force
using more pulleys reduces the force needed to lift a load
a ramp inclined plane makes it easier to move objects up to a higher place
a longer ramp needs less force but more distance
a wedge is two inclined planes used to split or cut such as an axe or knife
a screw is an inclined plane wrapped around a cylinder
a wheel and axle such as a doorknob or steering wheel
a compound machine is made of two or more simple machines such as a bicycle
machines make work easier by reducing the force needed
mechanical advantage tells how much a machine multiplies force
gears are wheels with teeth that transfer motion
momentum is mass times velocity
a heavier moving object has more momentum and is harder to stop
in a collision momentum is transferred between objects
a moving object has kinetic energy
a faster moving object has more kinetic energy
a car moving faster takes a longer distance to stop
pushing a heavier object requires more force
when two objects collide they push on each other with equal force
pendulum swings back and forth and its period depends on its length
a longer pendulum swings more slowly
centripetal force keeps an object moving in a circle
buoyancy is the upward force of a fluid on an object
an object floats when the buoyant force equals its weight
ships float because they displace a lot of water
pressure is force divided by area
a sharp knife cuts easily because it puts force on a small area
snowshoes spread weight over a large area to reduce pressure
water pressure increases with depth
air pressure pushes on everything
speed can be measured with a stopwatch and a meter stick
a ball rolls farther on a smooth floor than on carpet because carpet has more friction
a ball rolls faster down a steeper ramp
the higher a ball starts on a ramp the faster it moves at the bottom
the mass of an object does not change its falling acceleration without air resistance
a magnet can push or pull without touching
springs stretch more when more force is applied
elastic objects return to their shape after being stretched
an engine changes fuel energy into motion
brakes use friction to stop a car or bicycle
skating on smooth ice has less friction than walking on a rough sidewalk
dragging a box across the floor produces friction and heat
the force of gravity between earth and moon keeps the moon in orbit
a heavy truck needs more force to start moving than a small car
a wheelbarrow is a lever
a ramp is used to load heavy objects into a truck
throwing a ball harder makes it go faster and farther
a kicked ball stops rolling because of friction
an object in motion stays in motion unless a force like friction stops it
astronauts float in the space station because they are in free fall
