# Animals: adaptations, behavior, life cycles
an adaptation is a trait that helps an organism survive and reproduce in its environment
camouflage helps an animal blend in with its surroundings to hide from predators
camouflage coloring helps prey hide from predators and predators sneak up on prey
animals that change color can hide from predators
a chameleon changes color to blend in with its surroundings
the white fur of a polar bear helps it blend in with snow
thick fur and a layer of fat blubber keep animals warm in cold climates
polar bears have thick fur and blubber to stay warm in the arctic
whales and seals have blubber fat to keep warm in cold water
desert animals are active at night nocturnal to avoid the heat
camels store fat in their humps and can go a long time without water
large ears help desert foxes and rabbits release heat to stay cool
mimicry is when an animal looks like a more dangerous animal for protection
warning coloring bright colors warns predators that an animal is poisonous
skunks spray a bad smell to defend themselves
porcupines have sharp quills for protection
turtles have hard shells for protection
some animals play dead to avoid predators
birds have hollow light bones to help them fly
birds' beak shapes are adapted to the food they eat
a hummingbird has a long thin beak to drink nectar from flowers
a hawk eagle or owl has a sharp hooked beak and talons to catch and tear prey
a duck has webbed feet for swimming and a flat bill
a woodpecker has a strong pointed beak to drill into wood for insects
finches with thick strong beaks crack seeds
a pelican has a pouch beak to scoop fish
owls have large eyes to see at night and hunt nocturnal
predators often have eyes in front for depth perception
prey animals often have eyes on the sides of their heads to see predators
sharp teeth canines help carnivores tear meat
flat teeth molars help herbivores grind plants
fish use gills to get oxygen from water
whales and dolphins breathe air with lungs through a blowhole
fins and a streamlined shape help fish swim
webbed feet help animals swim such as ducks frogs and otters
long legs help animals run fast
a giraffe's long neck helps it reach leaves high in trees
an elephant uses its trunk to grab food and drink water
claws help animals dig climb and catch prey
moles have strong claws for digging underground tunnels
bats use echolocation sound echoes to find food and navigate in the dark
dolphins also use echolocation
snakes use their tongues to smell
some snakes have heat sensing pits to find warm blooded prey
spiders spin webs to catch insects
insects have antennae to sense touch and smell
a thick coat of fur grows in winter and sheds in summer
hibernation is a deep sleep during winter when animals save energy because food is scarce
bears groundhogs and some bats hibernate in winter
during hibernation an animal's heart rate breathing and body temperature drop
migration is when animals move to a different place seasonally to find food or warmer weather
birds migrate south in the fall to find food and warmer climate
monarch butterflies whales geese and caribou migrate
salmon migrate upstream to lay eggs where they were born
squirrels store gather nuts in the fall for winter food
instincts are inherited behaviors animals are born knowing how to do
spinning a web building a nest and migration are instinctive behaviors
learned behaviors are learned through experience or from parents
a dog learning to sit or a bird learning a song are learned behaviors
animals respond to stimuli in their environment
animals communicate with sounds smells movements and colors
bees dance to communicate the location of food flowers to other bees
ants leave chemical scent trails pheromones to communicate
wolves live in packs and howl to communicate
animals live in groups herds flocks schools for protection
courtship behaviors help animals find mates
territorial behavior protects an animal's space and resources
animals need food water oxygen shelter and space
shelter protects animals from weather and predators
animals get energy from the food they eat
herbivores eat only plants such as rabbits deer cows and horses
carnivores eat only other animals meat such as lions wolves sharks and hawks
omnivores eat both plants and animals such as bears raccoons and humans
scavengers eat dead animals such as vultures and hyenas
predators hunt kill and eat other animals called prey
prey are animals that are hunted and eaten by predators
a lion is a predator and a zebra is its prey
owls are predators of mice
the life cycle of an animal includes birth growth reproduction and death

butterflies and moths go through complete metamorphosis egg caterpillar larva chrysalis pupa adult
a caterpillar is the larva of a butterfly
a caterpillar forms a chrysalis cocoon in the pupa stage
incomplete metamorphosis has three stages egg nymph adult
grasshoppers dragonflies and crickets go through incomplete metamorphosis with nymphs
a nymph looks like a small adult without wings
frogs go through metamorphosis egg tadpole froglet adult frog
a tadpole lives in water and breathes with gills
tadpoles lose their tails and grow legs and lungs as they become frogs
a young frog is called a tadpole
birds hatch from eggs
chicks hatch from eggs and parents care for them
the mother bird sits on eggs to keep them warm incubate
mammals feed their young milk

the platypus is a mammal that lays eggs
animals reproduce to make offspring

fish lay many eggs in water

bees make honey from nectar and pollinate flowers
earthworms eat soil and dead plants and improve soil
animals help plants by pollinating flowers and spreading seeds
beavers build dams that change their environment and create ponds
coral reefs are built by tiny animals called coral polyps

dogs have a keen sense of smell
cats have whiskers that help them sense their surroundings
the heart rate of small animals is faster than large animals

diurnal animals are active during the day
