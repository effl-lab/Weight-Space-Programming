# Matter, properties, states, atoms, elements, chemical reactions
matter is anything that has mass and takes up space volume
the three common states of matter are solid liquid and gas
a solid has a definite shape and a definite volume
a liquid has a definite volume but takes the shape of its container
a gas spreads out to fill the whole container
particles in a solid are tightly packed and vibrate in place
particles in a liquid are close together but slide past each other
particles in a gas are far apart and move quickly and freely
gas particles move faster than liquid particles
plasma is a state of matter found in stars lightning and neon signs
heating matter makes its particles move faster and spread apart
cooling matter makes particles move slower and closer together
adding heat energy can change a solid to a liquid and a liquid to a gas
melting is the change from solid to liquid when heat is added
ice melts into liquid water when heated above 0 degrees celsius
freezing is the change from liquid to solid when heat is removed
water freezes into ice when cooled to 0 degrees celsius
boiling is rapid vaporization when a liquid changes to gas at its boiling point
evaporation is when a liquid changes to a gas at the surface
condensation is the change from gas to liquid when cooled
sublimation is when a solid changes directly into a gas such as dry ice
dry ice is solid carbon dioxide that sublimates
deposition is when a gas changes directly into a solid such as frost
the melting point is the temperature at which a solid melts
the boiling point is the temperature at which a liquid boils
the temperature of water stays the same while it is boiling or melting
water expands when it freezes so ice takes up more space than liquid water
ice is less dense than liquid water so ice floats
mass is the amount of matter in an object measured in grams or kilograms with a balance
volume is the amount of space an object takes up measured in liters milliliters or cubic centimeters
weight is the force of gravity on an object measured in newtons with a spring scale
density is mass divided by volume
density equals mass per unit volume grams per cubic centimeter
objects less dense than water float and objects denser than water sink
an object floats if its density is less than the density of the liquid
oil floats on water because oil is less dense than water
the density of water is 1 gram per milliliter
the volume of an irregular object can be measured by water displacement in a graduated cylinder
physical properties can be observed without changing the substance such as color shape size texture mass
physical properties include color odor hardness density melting point boiling point and magnetism
chemical properties describe how a substance reacts to form new substances such as flammability
flammability is the ability to burn
the ability to rust is a chemical property of iron
a physical change changes the size shape or state of matter
cutting tearing folding bending crushing and breaking are physical changes
melting freezing boiling and evaporating are physical changes changes of state
dissolving sugar or salt in water is a physical change
a chemical change produces one or more new substances with different properties
burning wood is a chemical change
rusting iron is a chemical change
cooking an egg or baking a cake or toasting bread is a chemical change
digesting food is a chemical change
fruit rotting or milk souring is a chemical change
fireworks exploding is a chemical change
signs of a chemical change include gas bubbles color change light heat odor and formation of a precipitate
a precipitate is a solid that forms when two liquids are mixed in a chemical reaction
mixing baking soda and vinegar produces bubbles of carbon dioxide gas a chemical reaction
a chemical reaction rearranges atoms to form new substances
reactants are the starting substances and products are the new substances made in a chemical reaction
in a chemical reaction mass is conserved
the law of conservation of mass says matter is not created or destroyed
the total mass of the products equals the total mass of the reactants
in a closed container the mass stays the same after a reaction
when ice melts the mass of the water stays the same
exothermic reactions release heat energy and get warmer
endothermic reactions absorb heat energy and get colder
burning combustion is a reaction with oxygen that releases heat and light
fire needs fuel oxygen and heat
a catalyst speeds up a chemical reaction without being used up
increasing temperature speeds up chemical reactions
crushing a solid into smaller pieces increases surface area and speeds up reactions
an atom is the smallest particle of an element
atoms are made of protons neutrons and electrons
the nucleus of an atom contains protons and neutrons
protons have a positive charge
neutrons have no charge they are neutral
electrons have a negative charge and move around the nucleus in energy levels shells
electrons have much less mass than protons and neutrons
the atomic number is the number of protons in an atom
the number of protons determines the element
the mass number is the number of protons plus neutrons
isotopes are atoms of the same element with different numbers of neutrons
an ion is an atom that has gained or lost electrons and has a charge
an element is a pure substance made of only one kind of atom
elements cannot be broken down into simpler substances by chemical means
there are more than 100 elements
the periodic table organizes elements by atomic number
elements in the same group column of the periodic table have similar properties
rows of the periodic table are called periods
metals are on the left side of the periodic table
nonmetals are on the right side of the periodic table
noble gases such as helium neon and argon are unreactive and in the last column
metals are shiny good conductors of heat and electricity malleable and ductile
malleable means it can be hammered into thin sheets
ductile means it can be pulled into wires
nonmetals are dull brittle and poor conductors insulators
metalloids such as silicon have properties of metals and nonmetals
hydrogen is the lightest and most common element in the universe
helium is a light gas used in balloons because it is less dense than air
oxygen is needed for burning and for breathing
carbon is found in all living things organic compounds
nitrogen makes up most of the air
gold silver and copper are metals
copper is used in electrical wires because it conducts electricity well
iron is a magnetic metal used to make steel
aluminum is a light metal used in cans and foil
mercury is a metal that is liquid at room temperature
sodium is a reactive metal
chlorine is a poisonous gas
the chemical symbol for oxygen is o hydrogen h carbon c nitrogen n iron fe gold au sodium na
a compound is a substance made of two or more elements chemically combined
water h2o is a compound of hydrogen and oxygen
carbon dioxide co2 is a compound of carbon and oxygen
table salt sodium chloride nacl is a compound of sodium and chlorine
a molecule is two or more atoms bonded together
a water molecule has two hydrogen atoms and one oxygen atom
an oxygen molecule o2 has two oxygen atoms
the atoms in oxygen gas share electrons in covalent bonds
covalent bonds form when atoms share pairs of electrons
ionic bonds form when electrons are transferred between atoms
a chemical formula shows the elements and number of atoms in a compound
a chemical equation shows reactants and products of a reaction
compounds have different properties than the elements that make them
a mixture is two or more substances physically combined that keep their own properties
mixtures can be separated by physical means such as filtering evaporation magnets and sorting
a magnet can separate iron filings from sand
filtering separates sand or solid particles from water
evaporation can separate salt from salt water
a solution is a mixture where one substance dissolves evenly in another
salt water and sugar water are solutions
the solute is the substance dissolved and the solvent is the substance doing the dissolving
water is called the universal solvent because it dissolves many substances
sugar dissolves faster in hot water than in cold water
stirring and heating make a solute dissolve faster
crushing sugar into smaller pieces makes it dissolve faster
a saturated solution cannot dissolve any more solute
soluble substances such as salt and sugar dissolve in water
sand and water is a mixture that can be separated by filtering
salad trail mix and sand and iron filings are mixtures
a suspension is a mixture where particles settle out
acids taste sour and have a ph less than 7
lemon juice and vinegar are acids
bases taste bitter feel slippery and have a ph greater than 7
soap baking soda and ammonia are bases
pure water is neutral with a ph of 7
the ph scale measures how acidic or basic a substance is
litmus paper turns red in acid and blue in base
an indicator changes color in acids and bases
acids and bases react to neutralize each other forming salt and water
organic compounds contain carbon
hydrocarbons are compounds of hydrogen and carbon such as fuels
polymers are long chains of molecules such as plastics
plastics are made from petroleum oil
alloys such as steel brass and bronze are mixtures of metals
steel is made from iron and carbon
rust is iron oxide formed when iron reacts with oxygen and water
painting iron prevents rusting by keeping out water and oxygen
silver tarnishes when it reacts with sulfur
a balance measures mass
the mass of an object does not change when its shape changes
breaking a crayon into pieces does not change its total mass
temperature measures the average kinetic energy of particles
particles of matter are always moving
gases can be compressed because their particles are far apart
liquids and solids cannot easily be compressed
a balloon expands when heated and shrinks when cooled
air has mass and takes up space
substances can be identified by their properties such as density boiling point and melting point
salt and sugar look alike but can be told apart by taste or by chemical tests
conductors allow heat and electricity to flow easily
insulators do not allow heat or electricity to flow easily
wood plastic rubber glass and air are insulators
metals are good conductors
magnetic materials include iron nickel and cobalt
the properties of a substance depend on its atoms and how they are arranged
diamond and graphite are both made of carbon atoms arranged differently
graphite in pencils is soft and diamond is hard
water can be a solid liquid or gas
water vapor steam is water in the gas state
steam is invisible water vapor and the cloud seen above a kettle is condensed droplets
the particles of a substance have the same mass in any state
evaporation cools a surface
salt lowers the freezing point of water and melts ice on roads
adding salt to water raises its boiling point
pressure affects the boiling point of water
water boils at a lower temperature on a mountain
a closed system does not let matter in or out
an open container lets gas escape so mass seems to decrease
when a candle burns wax reacts with oxygen to form carbon dioxide water heat and light
when wood burns it becomes ash smoke and gases
burning releases energy stored in chemical bonds
batteries change chemical energy into electrical energy
the elements in the human body are mostly oxygen carbon hydrogen and nitrogen
