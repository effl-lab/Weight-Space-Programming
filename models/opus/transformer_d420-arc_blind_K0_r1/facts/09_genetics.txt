# Heredity, genetics, evolution
heredity is the passing of traits from parents to offspring
inherited traits are passed from parents to offspring through genes
genes are segments of dna on chromosomes that carry instructions for traits
dna is the genetic material found in the nucleus of cells
dna has a double helix shape
dna is made of four bases adenine thymine guanine cytosine
chromosomes are made of dna and proteins
humans have 23 pairs 46 chromosomes in body cells
sex cells egg and sperm have half the number of chromosomes 23
offspring get half their genes from each parent
eye color hair color height and blood type are inherited traits
inherited traits include eye color dimples freckles and attached earlobes
acquired traits are learned or developed during an organism's life
scars calluses learning to ride a bike and speaking a language are acquired traits
traits can be affected by both genes and the environment
a plant's height can be affected by its genes and by the amount of sunlight water and nutrients
an organism's genotype is its genes and phenotype is its physical appearance traits
alleles are different forms of a gene
a dominant allele hides the recessive allele
a recessive trait only shows when an organism has two recessive alleles
dominant alleles are shown with capital letters and recessive alleles with lowercase letters
homozygous means two of the same alleles and heterozygous means two different alleles
a punnett square predicts the probability of offspring traits
a cross between two heterozygous parents gives a 3 to 1 ratio of dominant to recessive traits
gregor mendel is the father of genetics who studied pea plants
mendel crossed pea plants and discovered dominant and recessive traits
sexual reproduction combines genetic material from two parents producing offspring with variation
sexual reproduction produces offspring that are genetically different from parents
asexual reproduction involves one parent and produces genetically identical offspring clones
bacteria reproduce asexually by binary fission splitting in two
budding is asexual reproduction in yeast and hydra
cloning produces a genetically identical copy of an organism
a mutation is a change in the dna of a gene
mutations can be harmful helpful or have no effect
mutations in sex cells can be passed to offspring
radiation and some chemicals can cause mutations
genetic engineering changes the dna of organisms to give them new traits
genetically modified crops can resist insects or drought
selective breeding is when humans breed organisms with desired traits
dog breeds were produced by selective breeding
farmers use selective breeding to produce cows that give more milk
genetic disorders such as sickle cell anemia and cystic fibrosis are inherited
variation is the differences in traits among individuals of a species
evolution is the change in species over long periods of time
charles darwin proposed the theory of evolution by natural selection
darwin studied finches on the galapagos islands with different beak shapes
natural selection organisms with traits better suited to their environment survive and reproduce more
individuals with helpful adaptations survive and pass the traits to their offspring
over many generations natural selection increases the frequency of helpful traits in a population
survival of the fittest means the best adapted organisms survive and reproduce
peppered moths dark colored moths survived better on soot covered trees during the industrial revolution
bacteria can evolve resistance to antibiotics through natural selection
insects can become resistant to pesticides over generations
evidence for evolution includes fossils similar body structures embryos and dna
homologous structures similar bones in different species show common ancestry
the arm of a human the wing of a bat and the flipper of a whale have similar bones
vestigial structures have little or no function such as the human appendix
species that share more similar dna are more closely related
fossils show that organisms changed over time
the horse evolved from small ancestors over millions of years
a new species can form when populations are separated geographically and change over time
extinct species no longer have living members
environmental changes can cause species to adapt migrate or become extinct
adaptations develop over many generations
a trait that helps an organism survive in one environment may not help in another
camouflage and mimicry are adaptations produced by natural selection
organisms produce more offspring than can survive
competition for limited resources leads to natural selection
offspring of the same parents can have different traits because of variation
identical twins have the same dna
fraternal twins come from two different eggs
the sex of a baby is determined by the x and y chromosomes from the father
females have two x chromosomes and males have an x and a y chromosome
genes are passed from generation to generation
puppies look like their parents because they inherit genes
a seedless plant can be grown from cuttings and is identical to the parent
the color of a flower is an inherited trait controlled by genes
a dog's ability to perform tricks is learned through training
the instructions for making proteins are stored in dna
proteins determine many traits of an organism
genetic information is copied when cells divide
