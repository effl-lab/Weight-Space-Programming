# Energy, heat, electricity, magnetism
energy is the ability to do work or cause change
energy cannot be created or destroyed only changed from one form to another
the law of conservation of energy says total energy stays the same
forms of energy include kinetic potential thermal heat chemical electrical light sound nuclear and mechanical
kinetic energy is the energy of motion
potential energy is stored energy because of position or condition
gravitational potential energy increases with height
a ball at the top of a hill has potential energy that changes to kinetic energy as it rolls down
a book on a high shelf has more potential energy than on a low shelf
a stretched rubber band or compressed spring has elastic potential energy
a roller coaster has the most potential energy at the top and most kinetic energy at the bottom
a swinging pendulum changes potential energy to kinetic energy and back
mechanical energy is kinetic plus potential energy
chemical energy is stored in the bonds of food fuel and batteries
food contains chemical energy that the body uses
batteries store chemical energy and change it to electrical energy
a flashlight changes chemical energy in batteries into electrical energy and then light energy
a light bulb changes electrical energy into light and heat energy
a toaster changes electrical energy into heat thermal energy
a fan or electric motor changes electrical energy into mechanical energy motion
a speaker changes electrical energy into sound energy
a generator changes mechanical energy motion into electrical energy
solar panels solar cells change light energy from the sun into electrical energy
plants change light energy into chemical energy through photosynthesis
burning fuel changes chemical energy into heat and light energy
a car engine changes chemical energy in gasoline into mechanical energy and heat
wind turbines change wind kinetic energy into electrical energy
hydroelectric dams change the energy of moving water into electricity
nuclear power plants use fission of uranium to produce heat and electricity
nuclear energy is stored in the nucleus of atoms
the sun produces energy by nuclear fusion
some energy is always changed into heat during energy transformations
energy transformations are never 100 percent efficient some energy is lost as heat
efficient machines waste less energy as heat
thermal energy is the total kinetic energy of the particles in a substance
heat is the transfer of thermal energy from a warmer object to a cooler object
heat always flows from hot to cold
temperature measures how hot or cold something is the average kinetic energy of particles
temperature is measured with a thermometer in degrees celsius or fahrenheit
the three ways heat moves are conduction convection and radiation
conduction is heat transfer by direct contact touching
a metal spoon in hot soup gets hot by conduction
a pan on a stove heats by conduction
convection is heat transfer by the movement of fluids liquids and gases currents
warm air rising and cool air sinking forms convection currents
boiling water circulates by convection
radiation is heat transfer by electromagnetic waves without matter
heat from the sun reaches earth by radiation through space
feeling the warmth of a campfire or heat lamp is radiation
metals are good conductors of heat
wood plastic cloth foam rubber and air are poor conductors insulators of heat
insulation keeps heat from moving such as in a thermos cooler or house walls
a thermos keeps drinks hot or cold by reducing heat transfer
wearing layers of clothing traps air and keeps the body warm
wool coats and blankets insulate and slow heat loss
pot handles are made of plastic or wood because they are insulators
dark colors absorb more light and heat than light colors
black clothing absorbs more sunlight and gets hotter
white and light colored surfaces reflect sunlight and stay cooler
shiny surfaces reflect heat
heating a substance makes it expand and cooling makes it contract
thermometers work because liquids expand when heated
bridges have expansion joints because materials expand in heat
sidewalks have cracks gaps to allow expansion in hot weather
specific heat is the energy needed to raise the temperature of a substance
water has a high specific heat and heats up slowly
sand heats up faster than water in the sun
electricity is the flow of electrons electric charges
electric current is the flow of electric charge through a conductor
a circuit is a closed path through which electricity flows
a complete closed circuit lets current flow and an open circuit stops it
a switch opens and closes a circuit
a circuit needs a power source such as a battery wires and a load such as a bulb
in a series circuit there is only one path for current and if one bulb goes out all go out
in a parallel circuit there are multiple paths so if one bulb goes out the others stay lit
homes are wired with parallel circuits
conductors such as copper metal wires let electricity flow easily
insulators such as rubber plastic glass and wood do not let electricity flow
wires are covered with plastic or rubber insulation for safety
voltage is the push that moves current measured in volts
current is measured in amperes amps
resistance opposes the flow of current measured in ohms
ohm's law voltage equals current times resistance
fuses and circuit breakers protect circuits from too much current
static electricity is the buildup of electric charge on an object
rubbing a balloon on hair transfers electrons and builds static electricity
like charges repel and opposite charges attract
positive and negative charges attract each other
lightning is a discharge of static electricity
electric power is measured in watts
energy use is measured in kilowatt hours
turning off lights saves electricity energy
magnets have a north pole and a south pole
opposite poles of magnets attract and like poles repel
north and south poles attract
two north poles push apart repel
a magnet attracts objects made of iron nickel and cobalt
magnets attract iron nails paper clips and steel
a magnetic field surrounds a magnet and is strongest at the poles
iron filings show the magnetic field lines around a magnet
earth acts like a giant magnet with a magnetic field
a compass needle is a magnet that points north toward earth's magnetic north pole
a compass is used to find direction
an electromagnet is made by wrapping wire around an iron core and running electric current through it
adding more coils of wire or more current makes an electromagnet stronger
an electromagnet can be turned on and off
electric motors use electromagnets to produce motion
moving a magnet through a coil of wire produces electric current
electricity and magnetism are related electromagnetism
cutting a magnet in half makes two smaller magnets each with two poles
magnets lose strength when heated or dropped
renewable energy resources can be replaced naturally such as solar wind water hydroelectric geothermal and biomass
nonrenewable energy resources such as coal oil natural gas and uranium cannot be replaced quickly
fossil fuels are nonrenewable
fossil fuels formed from remains of plants and animals that lived millions of years ago
coal formed from ancient swamp plants
oil and natural gas formed from ancient sea organisms
burning fossil fuels causes air pollution and releases carbon dioxide
solar energy is renewable and does not pollute the air
wind energy is renewable and does not pollute
solar energy does not work well at night or on cloudy days
wind energy does not work when the wind is not blowing
geothermal energy uses heat from inside the earth
biomass energy comes from burning plant material wood and animal waste
hydroelectric power uses flowing water at dams
tidal energy uses the motion of ocean tides
energy conservation means using less energy
insulating homes reduces energy needed for heating
using energy efficient light bulbs saves energy
a cell phone battery stores chemical energy
a microwave oven uses electromagnetic waves to heat food
a hair dryer changes electrical energy into heat and motion
an electric heater changes electrical energy to heat
sound energy is produced by vibrations
light energy travels as waves
the sun's energy is the source of energy in fossil fuels wind and food
wind is caused by the sun heating earth unevenly so wind energy comes from the sun
friction changes kinetic energy into thermal heat energy
when a ball bounces some energy changes to heat and sound so it bounces lower each time
a bicycle dynamo generator produces electricity from pedaling
power plants burn coal to boil water and make steam that turns turbines and generators
a turbine spins a generator to make electricity
electricity is carried from power plants through power lines
electrical safety never touch outlets with wet hands
water conducts electricity so electrical devices should be kept away from water
