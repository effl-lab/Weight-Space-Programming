# Natural resources, environment, human impact, technology
natural resources are materials from nature that people use such as water air soil trees minerals and fuels
renewable resources can be replaced naturally in a short time such as trees sunlight wind and water
nonrenewable resources cannot be replaced in a human lifetime such as coal oil natural gas and minerals
trees are a renewable resource if new trees are planted
reduce reuse and recycle help conserve natural resources
recycling turns used materials into new products
recycling paper saves trees
recycling aluminum cans saves energy and resources
paper glass plastic and aluminum can be recycled
reusing a plastic bag or bottle reduces waste
reducing means using less such as using less water or energy
composting food scraps and yard waste reduces garbage and makes fertilizer
landfills are where garbage trash is buried
littering and plastic trash harm wildlife and oceans
plastic lasts hundreds of years in landfills
biodegradable materials break down naturally by decomposers
pollution is harmful substances added to the environment
air pollution comes from burning fossil fuels in cars factories and power plants
water pollution comes from sewage oil spills fertilizers pesticides and factory waste
oil spills harm sea birds fish and ocean life
soil pollution comes from pesticides chemicals and garbage
noise pollution and light pollution affect people and animals
smog forms from car exhaust and sunlight
acid rain is caused by pollution from burning coal and fuels
carpooling walking riding bikes and using public transportation reduce air pollution
electric cars and hybrid cars reduce air pollution
cleaner energy sources such as solar and wind reduce pollution
conservation is protecting and wisely using natural resources
water conservation turning off the faucet while brushing teeth and fixing leaky pipes
energy conservation turning off lights and electronics when not in use
planting trees reduces erosion provides habitat and absorbs carbon dioxide
reforestation is planting trees to replace cut forests
deforestation is cutting down large areas of forest
deforestation causes habitat loss erosion and more carbon dioxide in the air
urban development building cities and roads destroys habitats
farming can cause soil erosion and pollution from fertilizer runoff
crop rotation keeps soil fertile
overgrazing by animals can cause erosion and desertification
irrigation brings water to crops
dams provide electricity and water but change river habitats and block fish migration
wetland destruction harms wildlife and increases flooding
humans can protect the environment by conserving resources and reducing pollution
endangered species laws protect plants and animals at risk of extinction
the ozone layer was damaged by cfcs chemicals once used in sprays and refrigerators
global warming climate change is caused by greenhouse gases from burning fossil fuels
rising temperatures melt glaciers and raise sea levels
scientists measure carbon dioxide levels in the atmosphere
renewable energy sources reduce the use of fossil fuels
natural gas is a fossil fuel used for heating and cooking
gasoline and diesel come from crude oil petroleum
coal is burned in power plants to produce electricity
uranium is a nonrenewable resource used in nuclear power
nuclear power produces radioactive waste that must be stored safely
solar panels on roofs produce electricity from sunlight
wind farms have many turbines
geothermal power plants are built where there is heat near the surface such as iceland
fresh water is a limited resource that must be conserved
clean drinking water is treated to remove germs and pollutants
sewage treatment plants clean wastewater
filters remove particles from water
people need clean air clean water and healthy soil
technology is the use of scientific knowledge to solve problems and make tools
inventions such as the light bulb telephone and computer changed people's lives
thomas edison invented a practical light bulb
alexander graham bell invented the telephone
the wright brothers built the first successful airplane
the printing press spread information
refrigerators keep food cold to slow the growth of bacteria and spoiling
refrigeration lets food be stored longer and shipped long distances
improved shipping and transportation increased the types variety of foods available in stores
canning freezing and drying preserve food
satellites provide communication gps navigation and weather information
computers process and store information
the internet lets people share information quickly around the world
robots do jobs that are dangerous or repetitive
medical technology such as x rays mri and vaccines improves health
new technologies can have benefits and drawbacks for society and the environment
cars changed transportation but cause air pollution
plastics are useful but create waste
a new invention can solve one problem but create another
materials are chosen for their properties such as strength flexibility and cost
building materials wood brick concrete steel glass
steel is strong and used to build bridges and buildings
rubber is flexible and waterproof
glass is transparent and used for windows
cotton and wool are natural fibers and nylon and polyester are synthetic fibers
synthetic materials are made by people from chemicals such as plastic and nylon
natural materials come from plants animals or the earth such as wood cotton wool and stone
earthquake resistant buildings are designed to sway without collapsing
levees and dams help prevent flooding
storm shelters protect people from tornadoes
weather warnings help people prepare for hurricanes and storms
during a tornado go to a basement or interior room away from windows
during a thunderstorm stay indoors away from water and tall objects
emergency kits contain water food flashlight batteries and first aid supplies
fire safety stop drop and roll
smoke detectors warn people of fire
human population growth increases demand for resources food water and energy
cities produce heat island effects
people use land for farming cities and industry
mining for coal and minerals can damage land and water
strip mining removes the top layers of soil and rock
oil drilling can harm habitats
reclaiming land after mining restores habitats
sustainable practices meet present needs without harming the future
organic farming uses natural methods
integrated pest management uses natural predators to control pests
ladybugs eat aphids and help gardens
buying local food reduces energy used in transportation
