# Plants
plants make their own food by photosynthesis
photosynthesis uses sunlight energy water and carbon dioxide to make sugar glucose and oxygen
during photosynthesis plants take in carbon dioxide and release oxygen
photosynthesis changes light energy into chemical energy stored in sugar food
photosynthesis happens mainly in the leaves of plants
chlorophyll in leaves absorbs sunlight for photosynthesis
chlorophyll makes leaves green
plants need sunlight water air carbon dioxide nutrients and space to grow
plants get carbon dioxide from the air through tiny openings in leaves called stomata
stomata are pores in leaves that let carbon dioxide in and oxygen and water vapor out
plants take in water and minerals nutrients from the soil through their roots
roots absorb water and nutrients and anchor hold the plant in the soil
the stem supports the plant and carries water and nutrients between roots and leaves
xylem tubes carry water up from the roots to the leaves
phloem tubes carry sugar food from the leaves to other parts of the plant
leaves are where the plant makes food by photosynthesis
flowers are the reproductive parts of flowering plants that make seeds
the flower attracts pollinators with bright colors scent and nectar
pollination is the transfer of pollen from the stamen to the pistil
bees butterflies birds and bats pollinate flowers while collecting nectar
wind also carries pollen to pollinate plants such as grasses and pine trees
the stamen is the male part of a flower that makes pollen
the anther makes pollen
the pistil is the female part of the flower with the stigma style and ovary
the ovary of a flower develops into the fruit and ovules become seeds
after fertilization the ovary becomes a fruit that contains seeds
fruits protect seeds and help disperse them
seeds contain an embryo young plant and stored food
the seed coat protects the embryo inside the seed
the cotyledon stores food for the growing seedling
a seed needs water warmth and oxygen to germinate sprout
germination is when a seed begins to sprout and grow into a seedling
seedlings need light to grow
seeds are dispersed by wind water animals and explosion
seeds with wings or fluffy parts like dandelions are dispersed by wind
seeds with hooks or burrs stick to animal fur to be dispersed
animals eat fruits and spread the seeds in their droppings
coconuts float and are dispersed by water
the plant life cycle is seed germination seedling adult plant flowering pollination fruit and seed
some plants reproduce asexually from cuttings bulbs tubers runners or stems
potatoes are tubers that can grow new plants
strawberry plants send out runners to make new plants
ferns and mosses reproduce with spores
conifers such as pine trees produce seeds in cones
deciduous trees lose their leaves in autumn fall
evergreen trees keep their needles leaves all year
leaves change color in autumn because chlorophyll breaks down showing other pigments
plants respond to their environment tropism
phototropism is when plants grow toward light
plants bend toward a window because they grow toward light
gravitropism geotropism is when roots grow down with gravity and stems grow up
thigmotropism is growth response to touch like vines climbing
plants grow toward light and roots grow toward water
a plant kept in the dark turns yellow pale and grows tall and thin
without sunlight a plant cannot make food and will die
too little water causes a plant to wilt
wilting happens when plant cells lose water
plants need nitrogen phosphorus and potassium from soil or fertilizer
fertilizer adds nutrients to soil to help plants grow
plants use the sugar they make for energy and growth
plants also carry out cellular respiration using oxygen
plants store extra food as starch in roots stems and seeds
carrots and beets are roots that store food
celery and asparagus are stems we eat
lettuce and spinach are leaves we eat
apples tomatoes cucumbers and peppers are fruits containing seeds
plants release water vapor through leaves in transpiration
cactus plants have thick stems that store water and spines that reduce water loss
desert plants have waxy coatings deep or wide roots and small leaves to save water
plants in rainforests have large leaves to capture light in shade
water lilies have air spaces so their leaves float on water
the waxy cuticle on leaves reduces water loss
thorns and spines protect plants from being eaten
some plants produce poisons or bad tastes to defend against animals
venus flytrap is a carnivorous plant that traps insects to get nitrogen
vines climb on other plants to reach sunlight
trees have woody stems trunks covered by bark
bark protects the tree trunk
tree rings show the age of a tree one ring each year
wide tree rings show years with good growing conditions lots of rain
plants are producers at the base of food chains
plants provide food oxygen shelter and materials for animals and people
plants remove carbon dioxide from the air
forests produce much of the oxygen in the air
algae and phytoplankton produce much of earth's oxygen
grass is eaten by grazing animals herbivores
plant roots prevent soil erosion
selective breeding produces plants with desired traits such as larger fruit
farmers grow crops such as corn wheat rice and soybeans
greenhouses keep plants warm so they can grow in cold weather
hydroponics grows plants in water with nutrients without soil
plants need space so overcrowded plants grow poorly competition
bulbs such as tulips and onions store food underground
the seed of a bean plant has two cotyledons
corn is a monocot with one cotyledon
pine needles are leaves adapted to cold dry climates
moss grows in moist shady places
plants in cold places may be small and grow close to the ground
some plants are annuals living one year and some are perennials living many years
maple trees produce sap that is used to make syrup
rubber cotton and wood are plant products
medicines can come from plants
sunflowers turn to follow the sun
the sugar made in photosynthesis is glucose
glucose is a simple sugar used for energy
starch is a complex carbohydrate stored by plants
a plant's leaves are flat and broad to absorb more sunlight
plants grow taller toward the light
plants grow from seeds and grow larger over time
