# cause and effect: earth and space
The tilt of Earth's axis causes the seasons; Earth's rotation causes day and night; Earth's revolution causes the year.
The Moon's revolution around Earth causes the phases of the Moon; the Moon's gravity causes the tides.
Earth's rotation causes the Sun, Moon and stars to appear to rise in the east and set in the west.
Earth's revolution around the Sun causes different constellations to be visible in different seasons.
If Earth's axis were not tilted, there would be no seasons.
If Earth rotated faster, days would be shorter.
If the Moon were closer to Earth, tides would be higher.
The uneven heating of Earth's surface causes air pressure differences, which cause wind.
Differences in temperature and density of water cause deep ocean currents.
Heat from Earth's core causes convection currents in the mantle that move tectonic plates.
The movement of tectonic plates causes earthquakes, volcanoes, mountain building and the formation of ocean trenches.
Water freezing and expanding in cracks causes rocks to break apart.
Acid rain is caused by pollution from burning fossil fuels such as coal in power plants and gasoline in cars.
Greenhouse gases trapping heat cause global warming and climate change.
Deforestation causes soil erosion, loss of habitat and more carbon dioxide in the air.
Heavy rain on bare soil causes erosion and mudslides.
Clouds form when warm moist air rises, expands and cools.
Rain forms when tiny cloud droplets join into larger drops that become too heavy to stay in the air.
Rising warm air causes low pressure; sinking cool air causes high pressure.
Mountains cause rain on their windward side and dry deserts on their leeward side.
Volcanic eruptions can cause short term global cooling by releasing ash and sulfur gases that block sunlight.
Large asteroid impacts can cause mass extinctions by throwing dust into the atmosphere and blocking sunlight.
Melting glaciers and ice sheets cause sea level to rise.
The gravity of the Sun causes the planets to orbit it; the gravity of Earth causes the Moon to orbit it.
Gravity causes objects to have weight and pulls rain, snow and rivers downhill.
Gravity holds the atmosphere and oceans to Earth.
Friction with the atmosphere causes meteoroids to burn up and glow as meteors.
# cause and effect: life
Lack of sunlight causes plants to stop photosynthesis and eventually die.
Lack of water causes plants to wilt.
Too little food causes populations to decrease; plentiful food causes populations to increase.
A decrease in predators causes an increase in prey; an increase in predators causes a decrease in prey.
Introducing a species with no natural predators can cause it to overpopulate and harm native species.
Destroying habitats causes species to become endangered or extinct.
Pollution of water can cause fish and other aquatic organisms to die.
Overfishing causes fish populations to decline.
Overhunting caused the extinction of the passenger pigeon and the dodo bird.
Pesticides such as DDT caused eagles and other birds to lay thin shelled eggs that broke.
Banning DDT helped bald eagle populations recover.
Germs such as bacteria and viruses cause infectious diseases.
Smoking causes lung disease and cancer.
Exercise causes the heart rate and breathing rate to increase.
A lack of vitamin C causes scurvy; a lack of vitamin D causes weak bones called rickets; a lack of iron causes anemia.
Eating too much sugar causes tooth decay.
Mutations in DNA can cause new traits to appear in a population.
Changes in the environment can cause natural selection of individuals with helpful traits.
Isolation of populations over long periods can cause new species to form.
Variation in traits within a species is caused by sexual reproduction and mutations.
Climate change can cause species to move to new areas, adapt or become extinct.
Warmer ocean water causes coral bleaching.
Increased carbon dioxide dissolving in seawater makes the ocean more acidic, harming shellfish and corals.
# cause and effect: physical
Heating causes matter to expand; cooling causes matter to contract.
Adding heat causes melting, evaporation and boiling; removing heat causes condensation and freezing.
Friction causes moving objects to slow down and produces heat.
An unbalanced force causes an object to accelerate, change speed or change direction.
Vibrations cause sound.
Light reflecting off objects into our eyes causes us to see them.
Refraction of light by raindrops causes rainbows.
Electric current flowing through a wire causes a magnetic field around the wire.
Moving a magnet near a coil of wire causes an electric current.
Rubbing two materials together can cause static charges to build up.
A break in a circuit causes the current to stop flowing.
Increasing voltage causes more current to flow; increasing resistance causes less current to flow.
Increasing the temperature causes chemical reactions to go faster and solids to dissolve faster.
Increasing surface area causes faster reactions, faster dissolving, faster evaporation and faster cooling.
Exposure to oxygen and water causes iron to rust.
Increasing the mass of an object causes it to need more force to accelerate.
Increasing the height of a ramp causes a ball to roll faster at the bottom.
Increasing the angle of a ramp increases the force needed to push an object up it.
Lowering a ramp's slope decreases the force needed but increases the distance.
A longer lever arm decreases the force needed to lift a load.
Increasing the tension on a string causes a higher pitch.
Increasing the amplitude of a sound wave causes a louder sound.
A greater distance from a light source causes the light to appear dimmer.
Dark colors absorbing light causes them to heat up more than light colors.
# comparisons that questions ask about
Light travels much faster than sound; light travels about 300,000 kilometers per second and sound about 340 meters per second in air.
Sound travels fastest in solids, slower in liquids and slowest in gases; it cannot travel in a vacuum.
Water heats up and cools down more slowly than land, sand or soil.
Metals conduct heat and electricity much better than wood, plastic, glass or rubber.
Gases are less dense than liquids, and liquids are usually less dense than solids, except that ice is less dense than water.
Warm air is less dense than cold air; warm water is less dense than cold water; fresh water is less dense than salt water.
Ice is less dense than liquid water, so ice floats.
The Sun is much larger than Earth, and Earth is larger than the Moon.
The Sun is a star much closer to Earth than any other star, so it appears larger and brighter.
Jupiter is the largest planet, and Mercury is the smallest planet.
Stars are much farther away than planets.
The Moon is closer to Earth than the Sun, and it is much smaller.
Mammals and birds are warm blooded; reptiles, amphibians and fish are cold blooded.
Plant cells have cell walls and chloroplasts; animal cells do not.
Bacteria are much smaller than plant and animal cells, and viruses are much smaller than bacteria.
Sexual reproduction produces variation; asexual reproduction produces identical offspring.
Weather changes daily; climate describes long term patterns.
Physical changes do not produce new substances; chemical changes produce new substances.
Mixtures can be separated by physical means; compounds can only be separated by chemical reactions.
Renewable resources can be replaced in a short time; nonrenewable resources take millions of years to form.
Kinetic energy is energy of motion; potential energy is stored energy.
Conductors allow electricity and heat to flow; insulators resist the flow.
Series circuits have one path; parallel circuits have more than one path.
Transparent objects let light through; translucent let some light through; opaque objects block light.
Igneous rocks form from cooled magma or lava; sedimentary rocks form from compacted sediments; metamorphic rocks form under heat and pressure.
Weathering breaks rocks down; erosion carries the pieces away; deposition drops them.
Mass is the amount of matter; weight is the pull of gravity on that matter.
Speed is distance over time; velocity is speed with direction; acceleration is change in velocity.
Evaporation happens at the surface of a liquid at any temperature; boiling happens throughout the liquid at the boiling point.
Herbivores eat plants, carnivores eat animals, omnivores eat both, and decomposers break down dead matter.
Inherited traits come from parents through genes; learned behaviors come from experience.
Vertebrates have backbones; invertebrates do not.
Arteries carry blood away from the heart; veins carry blood back to the heart.
Rotation takes one day; revolution takes one year.
A lunar eclipse happens at full moon; a solar eclipse happens at new moon.
Asteroids are rocky; comets are icy.
Planets reflect light; stars produce light.
