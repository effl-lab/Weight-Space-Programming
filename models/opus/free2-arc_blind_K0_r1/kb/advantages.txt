# advantages, benefits and disadvantages
An advantage of solar energy is that it is renewable and produces no air pollution; a disadvantage is that it does not work at night or on cloudy days and solar panels are expensive.
An advantage of wind energy is that it is renewable and clean; a disadvantage is that wind does not always blow, and turbines can be noisy and harm birds.
An advantage of hydroelectric power is that it is renewable and does not pollute the air; a disadvantage is that dams flood land and block fish migration.
An advantage of geothermal energy is that it is renewable and reliable; a disadvantage is that it is available only in certain places.
An advantage of nuclear power is that it produces a lot of energy without air pollution or carbon dioxide; a disadvantage is radioactive waste and the risk of accidents.
An advantage of fossil fuels is that they are reliable, store a lot of energy and are easy to transport; a disadvantage is that they are nonrenewable and cause pollution and global warming.
An advantage of burning biomass is that it is renewable; a disadvantage is that it produces smoke and air pollution.
An advantage of electric cars is that they produce no exhaust; a disadvantage is limited range and long charging time.
A benefit of recycling is that it conserves natural resources, saves energy and reduces landfill waste.
A benefit of carpooling and public transportation is less fuel use and less air pollution.
A benefit of planting trees is cleaner air, shade, habitat for animals and less erosion.
A benefit of wetlands is that they filter water, control floods and provide habitat.
A benefit of vaccines is that they prevent disease.
A benefit of antibiotics is that they cure bacterial infections; a disadvantage is that overuse leads to resistant bacteria.
A benefit of pesticides is that they protect crops from insects; a disadvantage is that they can harm helpful insects, birds, fish and people.
A benefit of fertilizer is bigger crop yields; a disadvantage is water pollution from runoff.
A benefit of dams is flood control, water storage and electricity; a disadvantage is the loss of river habitat.
A benefit of genetically modified crops is resistance to pests and drought; some people worry about effects on the environment.
A benefit of selective breeding is crops and animals with useful traits; a disadvantage is less genetic variety.
An advantage of sexual reproduction is genetic variation in offspring, which helps a species survive changes in the environment.
An advantage of asexual reproduction is that it is fast and needs only one parent; a disadvantage is that all offspring are identical and may all be harmed by the same disease or change.
An advantage of living in a group is protection from predators and help finding food; a disadvantage is competition for food and spread of disease.
An advantage of camouflage is that predators or prey cannot easily see the animal.
An advantage of being warm blooded is being active in cold weather; a disadvantage is needing much more food.
An advantage of an exoskeleton is protection; a disadvantage is that it must be shed, or molted, to grow.
An advantage of seeds is that they protect the embryo and provide stored food, and can survive harsh conditions.
An advantage of a parallel circuit is that if one bulb burns out the others stay lit.
A disadvantage of a series circuit is that if one bulb goes out, they all go out.
An advantage of an electromagnet is that it can be turned on and off and its strength can be changed.
An advantage of a pulley is changing the direction of the force; an advantage of a ramp is using less force.
An advantage of using a lever is that a small force can lift a heavy load.
An advantage of the metric system is that units are based on powers of ten and used worldwide.
An advantage of a computer model is that it can test ideas quickly, safely and cheaply; a disadvantage is that it may not include every real factor.
An advantage of a physical model is that it can be seen and handled; a disadvantage is that it is not exactly like the real thing in size or detail.
An advantage of a space telescope is a clear view above the atmosphere; a disadvantage is high cost and difficulty of repair.
An advantage of repeating trials is more reliable results.
An advantage of a large sample size is that results are more reliable.
An advantage of LED and fluorescent bulbs is lower energy use and longer life.
An advantage of insulation is lower heating and cooling costs.
An advantage of refrigeration is that food lasts longer without spoiling.
An advantage of satellites is that they can observe large areas of Earth, track weather and relay communications.
An advantage of a hand lens is that it is easy to carry into the field; a microscope magnifies much more.
A disadvantage of plastic is that it is made from oil and takes hundreds of years to break down.
A disadvantage of landfills is that they take up land and can leak pollution into groundwater.
A disadvantage of clearing forests is habitat loss, erosion and increased carbon dioxide.
A disadvantage of building on floodplains is the risk of flooding.
A disadvantage of mining is damage to land, water pollution and habitat loss.
A disadvantage of introduced invasive species is that they can outcompete native species.
A disadvantage of overgrazing is that it removes plants and causes soil erosion.
A disadvantage of monoculture farming, planting one crop, is that pests and disease can spread easily.
