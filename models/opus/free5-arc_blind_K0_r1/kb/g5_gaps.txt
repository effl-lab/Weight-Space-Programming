# germination
Seeds do not need soil or light to germinate; they need water, air and warmth, and can sprout on a wet paper towel.
Warmth, water and oxygen are the conditions most seeds need to germinate.
When a seed germinates, the root grows out first, then the shoot with the stem and first leaves.
# learned and inherited behaviour of pets
A dog that comes when its name is called has learned this behavior from training.
Tricks, coming when called and obeying commands are learned behaviors of dogs; panting, wagging the tail and sniffing are inherited.
# energy use of activities
Running uses much more energy than walking, and walking uses more energy than sitting, reading or sleeping.
The body uses the least energy while sleeping and the most energy during hard exercise such as running a race.
# transparent materials
Clear glass and clear plastic are transparent and let most light pass through; aluminum foil and wood are opaque and block light.
