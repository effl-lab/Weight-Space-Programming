# negative facts: what things are not or do not do (used by the negative-fact channel)
Rubber, plastic, wood, glass, cloth and paper do not conduct electricity well; they are insulators.
A rubber band, a plastic spoon, a wooden stick and a glass rod do not conduct electricity.
Metals such as copper, aluminum, iron and steel do not insulate well; they are conductors.
Magnets do not attract plastic, wood, glass, rubber, paper, copper, aluminum, gold or silver.
A magnet does not pick up a copper penny, an aluminum can, a plastic button or a wooden pencil.
Sharks, fish, frogs, snakes, lizards and birds are not mammals.
A shark is a fish, not a mammal; whales, dolphins and bats are mammals.
Coal, oil, natural gas and gasoline are not renewable resources.
Wind, sunlight, water and trees are not fossil fuels.
Spiders are not insects; they have eight legs and two body parts.
Insects do not have eight legs; they have six legs, three body parts and antennae.
Mushrooms and other fungi do not make their own food; they are not plants.
Animals do not make their own food; plants and algae do.
Melting, freezing, boiling, cutting, tearing, dissolving and crushing are not chemical changes.
Burning, rusting, cooking, baking and rotting are not physical changes.
A new substance is not formed in a physical change.
Rocks, water, air, sunlight and soil are not living things.
Viruses are not cells and cannot reproduce on their own.
The Moon does not produce its own light.
Planets do not produce their own light; they reflect light from the Sun.
Sound does not travel through empty space or a vacuum.
Light does not need a medium; it can travel through empty space.
Mars, Mercury, Venus and Earth do not have rings; Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus and Neptune have rings.
Geothermal energy and nuclear energy do not come from the Sun.
Tidal energy comes mostly from the Moon's gravity, not from the Sun.
Plants do not get their food from the soil; they make food by photosynthesis.
Plants do not take in oxygen to make food; they take in carbon dioxide.
Mass is not changed by moving an object to the Moon; only its weight changes.
The total mass does not change when ice melts, when water freezes or when sugar dissolves.
Mammals do not lay eggs, except the platypus and echidna.
Reptiles do not have fur or feathers; they have dry scales.
Fish do not breathe with lungs; they use gills.
Whales and dolphins do not breathe with gills; they breathe air with lungs.
Bats are not birds; they are mammals that fly.
Penguins and ostriches are birds that do not fly.
Humans do not make their own food; they are consumers.
Decomposers do not make food from sunlight; they break down dead matter.
Weathering does not move rock pieces; erosion moves them.
Breaking a rock into smaller pieces does not change the minerals in it.
Cutting a substance into pieces does not change its density or the kind of matter.
An insulator does not let heat or electricity pass through easily.
Opaque materials such as wood, metal and cardboard do not let light pass through.
Clear glass and clear plastic do not block most light.
Igneous rocks do not usually contain fossils; fossils are found in sedimentary rocks.
Earthquakes do not happen only at the equator; most happen along plate boundaries.
The seasons are not caused by Earth's distance from the Sun.
The phases of the Moon are not caused by Earth's shadow.
Inherited traits are not learned; learned behaviors are not passed on by genes.
A scar, a haircut, a tattoo and the ability to read are not inherited.
Students should not eat, drink or taste anything in a science laboratory.
Students should not smell chemicals directly or mix chemicals without permission.
Students should not touch broken glass with bare hands.
Bacteria and fungi are not producers; they are decomposers.
Producers do not need to eat other organisms.
Carnivores do not eat plants; herbivores do not eat meat.
A solid does not take the shape of its container; liquids and gases do.
A gas does not have a definite shape or a definite volume.
A liquid does not have a definite shape, but it has a definite volume.
Stars do not orbit planets; planets orbit stars.
The Sun is not a planet; it is a star.
Comets and asteroids are not stars.
Heat does not flow from a cold object to a hot object on its own.
Cold does not move into things; heat moves out of them.
Energy is not created or destroyed; it changes form.
# not part of a body system
The lungs, the heart, the brain and the kidneys are not part of the digestive system.
The stomach, the intestines and the liver are not part of the respiratory system.
The bones and the stomach are not part of the circulatory system; the heart, arteries, veins and blood are.
The heart and the lungs are not part of the nervous system; the brain, spinal cord and nerves are.
The kidneys and bladder are part of the excretory system, not the digestive system.
Muscles are not bones; tendons connect muscles to bones.
# organisms that do not need what others need
Some bacteria do not need oxygen to survive; they live where there is no air.
Plants do not need to eat food; they need light, water, air and nutrients.
Most seeds do not need light to sprout.
Fungi do not need sunlight to grow.
Animals in the deep ocean do not get sunlight; some depend on chemicals from vents.
# not found in a habitat
Polar bears do not live in rain forests or deserts; they live in the Arctic.
Cactuses do not grow in the Arctic tundra or rain forests; they grow in deserts.
Frogs and other amphibians do not usually live in dry deserts because their skin must stay moist.
Penguins do not live at the North Pole; they live in the Southern Hemisphere.
Fish do not live on land; whales do not live in fresh water ponds.
# not true about planets and space
Mars is not the largest planet; Jupiter is the largest planet.
Mercury is not the hottest planet; Venus is the hottest planet.
Pluto is not a planet; it is a dwarf planet.
The Moon is not a planet or a star; it is a natural satellite of Earth.
The Sun is not the largest star; it is a medium-sized star.
The Moon's light is not a source of energy for making electricity.
Comets are not made of fire; they are made of ice, dust and rock.
The Sun does not move around Earth; Earth rotates and revolves around the Sun.
# not a property or a change
Color, shape and size are not used alone to identify a mineral; hardness, streak and luster are better.
Changing the shape of clay does not change its mass.
Dissolving salt in water is not a chemical change.
Rusting is not a physical change; it forms a new substance.
Mixing sand and water does not make a new substance.
Heat is not a substance; it is energy moving from warmer to cooler matter.
# safety and conservation
Leaving lights and water running does not conserve resources.
Driving cars more often does not reduce air pollution; walking, biking and buses do.
Throwing batteries in the trash is not safe for the environment; they should be recycled.
Pouring chemicals down the drain is not a safe way to get rid of them.
