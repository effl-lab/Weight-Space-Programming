# Ordered sequences (arch.py: "order and extremes").
# name | words naming the low end | words naming the high end : item ; item ; ...
# Items are listed from first / smallest / closest / oldest to last / largest / farthest / youngest.
# An item may have several words separated by spaces (all mean the same item); a word that two items of
# the same sequence share ('core' in 'outer core', 'inner core') does not identify an item and is dropped.
# An optional fourth field lists topic words: the extremes use the sequence only when the question has one.
# A name ending in * is used for ranking only (its items are often named in either order).
# Used two ways: an option that lists at least three items in the sequence's order gains (in reverse order, it loses;
# "from largest to smallest" in the question reverses the order), and when the question asks for the
# low or high end ("Which is the smallest?"), options that are items of the sequence are ranked.
