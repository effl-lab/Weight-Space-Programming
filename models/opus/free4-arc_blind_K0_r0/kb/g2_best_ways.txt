# the best way to do things in science class and investigations
The best way to make results more reliable is to repeat the experiment several times and average the results.
The best way to find the volume of an irregular rock is to measure how much water it displaces in a graduated cylinder.
The best way to find the volume of a box is to measure its length, width and height with a ruler and multiply.
The best way to find the density of an object is to measure its mass with a balance and its volume, then divide mass by volume.
The best way to find out if a substance is an acid or a base is to test it with litmus paper or another indicator.
The best way to separate iron filings from sand is with a magnet.
The best way to separate sand from water is to pour the mixture through a filter.
The best way to separate salt from water is to let the water evaporate.
The best way to separate pebbles from sand is with a screen or sieve.
The best way to identify a mineral is to test several properties such as hardness, streak, luster and cleavage, not just color.
The best way to show change over time, such as temperature or plant growth, is a line graph.
The best way to compare amounts in different groups is a bar graph.
The best way to show parts of a whole is a circle graph.
The best way to observe tiny cells or microorganisms is with a microscope.
The best way to observe the Moon's craters or distant planets is with a telescope.
The best way to observe details of an insect or a leaf is with a hand lens or magnifying glass.
The best way to measure the speed of an object is to measure the distance it travels and the time it takes.
The best way to test a hypothesis is with a controlled experiment that changes only one variable.
The best way to know whether a result was caused by the variable being tested is to compare it with a control group.
The best way to share results with other scientists is to publish a detailed report of the procedure, data and conclusions.
The best way to reduce errors in measurement is to use the correct tool, read it carefully at eye level and repeat measurements.
The best way to keep data organized is a data table.
The best way to model the phases of the Moon is with a lamp as the Sun, a ball as the Moon and your head as Earth.
The best way to model the cause of day and night is to spin a globe in front of a lamp.
The best way to model the seasons is to move a tilted globe around a lamp.
The best way to model erosion is to pour water down a tray of soil or sand.
The best way to model how fossils form is to press a shell into clay.
The best way to model a volcanic eruption is with baking soda and vinegar in a model volcano.
The best way to study something too dangerous, large, small or slow is to use a model or computer simulation.
The best way to find out how many of an organism live in an area is to count them in sample plots and estimate.
The best way to learn about a plant's growth is to measure its height regularly with a ruler and record it.
The best way to protect your eyes in a lab is to wear safety goggles.
The best way to handle a hot beaker is with tongs or heat resistant gloves.
The best way to dispose of broken glass in a lab is to tell the teacher and put it in a special broken glass container.
The best way to smell a chemical is to wave the fumes toward your nose with your hand.
# the best way to solve everyday and environmental problems
The best way to reduce soil erosion on a hillside is to plant grass, trees or cover crops so roots hold the soil, and to build terraces.
The best way to reduce wind erosion on a farm is to plant windbreaks of trees and keep plants or crop stubble on the soil.
The best way to reduce erosion of a beach is to plant dune grass and build fences or jetties.
The best way to save energy at home is to turn off lights and electronics when not in use, use energy efficient bulbs and appliances, and insulate the house.
The best way to keep a house warm in winter while using less energy is to add insulation and seal gaps around windows and doors.
The best way to keep a house cool in summer is to use shade trees, light colored roofs, insulation and closed blinds on sunny windows.
The best way to save water is to fix leaks, take shorter showers and turn off the faucet while brushing teeth.
The best way to reduce air pollution from cars is to carpool, walk, bike, use public transportation or drive electric or hybrid cars.
The best way to reduce the amount of trash in landfills is to reduce, reuse and recycle, and to compost food scraps.
The best way to reduce water pollution from farms is to use less fertilizer and pesticide and keep plant buffers along streams.
The best way to protect an endangered species is to protect and restore its habitat.
The best way to prevent the spread of colds and flu is to wash hands often, cover coughs and sneezes and stay home when sick.
The best way to prevent food from spoiling is to keep it cold in a refrigerator or freezer, or to dry, can or salt it.
The best way to stay safe in a thunderstorm is to go indoors or into a car and stay away from water, tall trees and metal.
The best way to stay safe in a tornado is to go to a basement or an inside room with no windows on the lowest floor.
The best way to stay safe in an earthquake is to drop, cover under a sturdy table, and hold on.
The best way to prepare for a hurricane is to gather supplies, protect windows and evacuate if told to.
The best way to prevent forest fires is to put out campfires completely and not play with matches.
The best way to keep a drink cold is to put it in an insulated container such as a foam cooler or thermos.
The best way to keep warm outside in winter is to wear layers of clothing, a hat and gloves, because they trap air and slow heat loss.
The best way to melt ice on a sidewalk is to spread salt, which lowers the freezing point of water.
The best way to dissolve sugar quickly is to use hot water, stir, and use small grains instead of a sugar cube.
The best way to make a plant grow well is to give it sunlight, water, air, nutrients from good soil and the right temperature.
The best way to make an electromagnet stronger is to add more coils of wire, use more current or a stronger battery, and use an iron core.
The best way to light a bulb is to make a complete circuit with a battery and wires.
The best way to make a simple circuit bulb brighter is to add another battery in series.
The best way to make a sound louder is to make the object vibrate with more energy; the best way to raise its pitch is to make it vibrate faster.
The best way to move a heavy object up into a truck is to use a ramp, which needs less force over a longer distance.
The best way to lift a heavy rock with a lever is to put the fulcrum close to the rock.
The best way to reduce friction between moving parts is to use oil, grease or ball bearings.
The best way to increase friction on an icy road is to spread sand and use tires with deep treads.
The best way to keep iron from rusting is to paint it, oil it, or coat it with zinc to keep out water and oxygen.
The best way to tell if a liquid is pure water is to measure its boiling point and freezing point.
The best way to find out where an earthquake started is to compare seismograph readings from three or more stations.
The best way to predict the weather is to use data from weather stations, satellites, radar and computer models.
The best way to find north without a compass is to look for Polaris, the North Star, at night.
The best way to learn about the inside of Earth is to study seismic waves from earthquakes.
The best way to learn about Earth's past climate is to study ice cores, tree rings, fossils and layers of sediment.
The best way to learn what an extinct animal ate is to study its fossil teeth and fossil droppings.
The best way to tell the age of a tree is to count its growth rings.
The best way to tell the relative age of rock layers is the law of superposition: lower layers are older.
The best way to tell the absolute age of a rock is radiometric dating.
# most likely results and effects
The most likely result of removing all the plants from a hillside is increased erosion and landslides.
The most likely result of a long drought is that plants die, crops fail, water supplies shrink and wildfires increase.
The most likely result of a flood is erosion of riverbanks and deposition of new sediment on the floodplain.
The most likely result of an increase in greenhouse gases is an increase in Earth's average temperature.
The most likely result of melting glaciers and ice sheets is a rise in sea level.
The most likely result of building a city where there was a forest is more runoff, more flooding, less habitat and higher temperatures.
The most likely result of acid rain is damage to forests, lakes, fish, buildings and statues.
The most likely result of an oil spill is harm to birds, fish and other ocean life.
The most likely result of introducing an invasive species is a decrease in native species that compete with it or are eaten by it.
The most likely result of removing a top predator is an increase in its prey, followed by overgrazing of plants.
The most likely result of an increase in a prey population is an increase in its predator population.
The most likely result of a disease that kills a producer is a decrease in the consumers that depend on it.
The most likely result of overfishing is a decrease in fish populations.
The most likely result of using pesticides for many years is that pests become resistant to them.
The most likely result of cross pollination between two different flowers is offspring with traits from both parents.
The most likely result of exercise is an increase in heart rate, breathing rate and body temperature.
The most likely result of not getting enough calcium is weak bones and teeth.
The most likely result of eating too much sugar and fat without exercise is weight gain and health problems.
The most likely result of heating a solid is that it expands and may melt.
The most likely result of cooling a gas is that it contracts and may condense.
The most likely result of adding heat to water at 100 degrees Celsius is that it boils into steam while its temperature stays the same.
The most likely result of mixing an acid and a base is that they neutralize each other, forming water and a salt.
The most likely result of leaving iron outside in the rain is rust.
The most likely result of rubbing two objects together is that they get warmer.
The most likely result of pushing a cart with a greater force is a greater acceleration.
The most likely result of adding mass to a cart pushed with the same force is a smaller acceleration.
The most likely result of opening the switch in a series circuit is that all the bulbs go out.
The most likely result of bringing the north pole of one magnet near the north pole of another is that they repel.
The most likely result of the Moon passing between the Sun and Earth is a solar eclipse.
The most likely result of Earth passing between the Sun and the Moon is a lunar eclipse.
The most likely result of Earth's tilt is the change of seasons.
The most likely result of Earth's rotation is day and night.
The most likely result of a volcanic eruption is new rock from cooled lava, ash in the air, and possibly cooler temperatures.
The most likely result of two continental plates colliding is the formation of tall mountains.
The most likely result of plates sliding past each other is earthquakes.
The most likely result of plates moving apart under the ocean is a mid ocean ridge and new ocean floor.
