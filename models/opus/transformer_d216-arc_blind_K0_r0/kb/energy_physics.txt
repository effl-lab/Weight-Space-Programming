# forms of energy
energy is the ability to do work or cause change
kinetic energy is the energy of motion
potential energy is stored energy due to position or condition
gravitational potential energy increases with height and mass
a ball at the top of a hill has potential energy that changes to kinetic energy as it rolls down
elastic potential energy is stored in stretched or compressed springs and rubber bands
chemical energy is stored in food fuel and batteries
thermal energy heat is the energy of moving particles
electrical energy is the energy of moving electric charges electrons
light energy radiant energy travels as electromagnetic waves
sound energy is carried by vibrations
nuclear energy is stored in the nucleus of atoms
mechanical energy is the sum of kinetic and potential energy
faster objects have more kinetic energy
heavier objects moving at the same speed have more kinetic energy
= kinetic
= potential
= thermal
# energy transformation
energy can change from one form to another but cannot be created or destroyed
the law of conservation of energy says total energy stays the same
a battery changes chemical energy into electrical energy
a light bulb changes electrical energy into light and heat energy
a toaster changes electrical energy into heat energy
a fan motor changes electrical energy into mechanical energy motion
a radio speaker changes electrical energy into sound energy
solar panels solar cells change light energy into electrical energy
a generator changes mechanical energy into electrical energy
an electric motor changes electrical energy into mechanical energy
burning fuel changes chemical energy into heat and light energy
plants change light energy into chemical energy during photosynthesis
food provides chemical energy that the body changes into motion and heat
when you rub your hands together friction changes kinetic energy into heat
some energy is always lost as heat during energy transformations
a microphone changes sound energy into electrical energy
wind turbines change wind kinetic energy into electrical energy
hydroelectric dams change the energy of moving water into electricity

# heat transfer
heat is the transfer of thermal energy from a warmer object to a cooler object
heat always flows from hot to cold
conduction is heat transfer by direct contact between objects
a metal spoon in hot soup gets hot by conduction
convection is heat transfer by the movement of fluids liquids and gases
warm air rises and cool air sinks creating convection currents
boiling water in a pot circulates by convection
radiation is heat transfer by electromagnetic waves without matter
the sun warms earth by radiation
heat from a campfire or heat lamp reaches you by radiation
dark colors absorb more light and heat and light colors reflect more
black clothing absorbs more heat from sunlight than white clothing
temperature measures the average kinetic energy of particles
temperature is measured with a thermometer in degrees celsius or fahrenheit
a thermos keeps liquids hot or cold by reducing heat transfer
= conduction conduct conducts conducted
= convection
= radiation radiate radiates radiant
# conductors insulators
conductors allow heat or electricity to flow through them easily
metals such as copper aluminum iron and silver are good conductors
insulators slow or stop the flow of heat or electricity
wood plastic rubber glass cloth foam and air are good insulators
pot handles are made of plastic or wood because they are insulators
electric wires are made of copper covered with plastic or rubber insulation
wool coats and blankets keep you warm by trapping air and insulating
houses use insulation in walls and attics to keep heat in during winter
styrofoam cups keep drinks hot because foam is an insulator
= conductor conductors conductive conductivity
= insulator insulators insulate insulation insulating insulated
# electricity
electricity is the flow of electric charges electrons
an electric current is the flow of electrons through a conductor
a circuit is a closed path through which electric current flows
a circuit needs an energy source like a battery wires and a load like a bulb
a closed complete circuit lets current flow and an open circuit does not
a switch opens and closes a circuit
in a series circuit there is only one path and if one bulb goes out they all go out
in a parallel circuit there is more than one path and other bulbs stay lit if one goes out
houses are wired with parallel circuits
voltage is measured in volts current in amperes amps and resistance in ohms
resistance slows the flow of current
batteries store chemical energy and produce direct current
static electricity is a buildup of electric charge on an object
rubbing a balloon on hair gives it static charge
like charges repel and opposite charges attract
fuses and circuit breakers protect circuits from too much current
= electricity electric electrical electrically
= circuit circuits
= battery batteries

magnets have a north pole and a south pole
like poles repel and opposite poles attract
a magnetic field surrounds a magnet
magnetic force is strongest at the poles
earth acts like a giant magnet with a magnetic field
a compass needle is a magnet that points north toward earth's magnetic pole
an electromagnet is made by wrapping a wire coil around an iron core and running current through it
more coils of wire and more current make an electromagnet stronger
electromagnets can be turned on and off
magnets do not attract aluminum copper wood plastic or paper
= magnet magnets magnetic magnetism
= compass compasses
# forces
a force is a push or a pull
forces can change the speed direction or shape of an object
force is measured in newtons with a spring scale
balanced forces are equal and opposite and do not change motion
unbalanced forces cause an object to start move stop or change direction
friction is a force that opposes motion between two surfaces
friction produces heat
rough surfaces produce more friction than smooth surfaces
lubricants like oil reduce friction
air resistance is friction from air that slows falling objects
parachutes increase air resistance to slow a fall
gravity is a force that pulls objects toward each other and toward earth
magnetism is a force that acts at a distance
tension is a pulling force in ropes and strings

more force is needed to accelerate a more massive object
newton's third law for every action there is an equal and opposite reaction
a rocket moves forward because gas pushes out backward action and reaction

a wheel and axle such as a doorknob or steering wheel

# waves

waves transfer energy not matter
mechanical waves need a medium such as air water or solid to travel
transverse waves move the medium perpendicular at right angles to the direction of the wave
in a transverse wave particles move up and down perpendicular to the wave
longitudinal compressional waves move the medium parallel back and forth in the direction of the wave
sound waves are longitudinal waves with compressions and rarefactions
wavelength is the distance between two crests
amplitude is the height of a wave and relates to its energy
frequency is the number of waves that pass a point per second measured in hertz
higher frequency waves have shorter wavelengths
the crest is the top of a wave and the trough is the bottom

black objects absorb all colors and white objects reflect all colors
