# new york state geography
new york state has the adirondack mountains catskill mountains and the hudson river
long island and the finger lakes were shaped by glaciers during the ice age
niagara falls is on the border between new york and canada
lake erie and lake ontario border new york
the hudson river flows south into new york harbor and the atlantic ocean
new york has four seasons with cold snowy winters and warm summers
in new york the longest day is in june near the summer solstice and the shortest day is in december
in new york the sun is highest in the sky at noon in june
in new york the sun rises in the northeast in summer and the southeast in winter
lake effect snow falls east of lake erie and lake ontario near buffalo and syracuse
new york bedrock includes sedimentary rocks with fossils such as trilobites and brachiopods
the state fossil of new york is the eurypterid sea scorpion
# united states regions
the great plains have grasslands and farms
the rocky mountains are in the western united states
the appalachian mountains are in the eastern united states
the mississippi river is the longest river system in the united states
florida has a warm humid climate and hurricanes
california has earthquakes along the san andreas fault
alaska has glaciers and very cold winters
hawaii has active volcanoes formed over a hot spot
the gulf of mexico has warm water that feeds hurricanes
the great lakes hold a large part of the world's fresh surface water
tornadoes are common in the central plains in spring
deserts are found in the southwest such as arizona and nevada
# world geography science
the amazon rainforest in south america has the greatest biodiversity
the sahara desert is in northern africa
antarctica is covered by a thick ice sheet
the himalayas include mount everest the highest peak
the pacific ocean is the largest and deepest ocean
iceland sits on the mid atlantic ridge and has volcanoes and geysers
japan has many earthquakes and volcanoes because it is on plate boundaries
the dead sea is very salty so people float easily
# health safety
wear a helmet when riding a bike or skateboard
wear sunscreen and a hat to protect skin from sunburn
drink water when exercising to avoid dehydration
seat belts reduce injuries in car crashes
never swim alone and swim where a lifeguard is present
during a thunderstorm stay indoors away from water and tall objects
during a fire stay low under the smoke and get out
during an earthquake drop cover and hold on
during a tornado go to a basement or an inside room without windows
keep medicines and cleaning products away from young children
read labels on food and medicine
first aid treats minor injuries
# hygiene germs
germs are tiny organisms that cause illness
washing hands for twenty seconds with soap kills germs
sneezing into your elbow helps stop germs from spreading
brushing and flossing teeth removes plaque and bacteria
bathing removes dirt sweat and germs from skin
food should be stored at safe temperatures to stop bacteria
raw meat should be kept separate from other foods
# technology engineering problems
engineers designed seawalls to protect coasts from waves
engineers designed levees to protect cities from floods
engineers design cars with crumple zones to absorb energy in crashes
solar ovens use reflectors to concentrate sunlight to cook food
water filters remove dirt and germs to make water safe to drink
wind turbines are placed in windy areas like hills and coasts
greenhouses let sunlight in and trap heat so plants grow in cold weather
irrigation systems bring water to crops in dry areas
dams provide water storage flood control and hydroelectric power
bridges must be designed to carry heavy loads and resist wind and earthquakes
