# photosynthesis
photosynthesis is the process plants use to make food sugar glucose from sunlight water and carbon dioxide
plants use energy from sunlight to make their own food
photosynthesis takes place in the chloroplasts of leaves using chlorophyll
during photosynthesis plants take in carbon dioxide and release oxygen
photosynthesis changes light energy into chemical energy stored in sugar
plants are producers because they make their own food
plants need sunlight water air carbon dioxide and nutrients to grow
oxygen in the atmosphere comes from photosynthesis by plants and algae
plants also use cellular respiration to release energy from sugar
= photosynthesis photosynthesize photosynthetic
# plant parts
roots absorb water and minerals nutrients from the soil and anchor the plant
stems support the plant and carry water and nutrients between roots and leaves
leaves are where most photosynthesis happens and they capture sunlight
xylem carries water from the roots up to the leaves
phloem carries food sugar from the leaves to other parts of the plant
stomata are tiny openings in leaves that let carbon dioxide in and oxygen and water vapor out
guard cells open and close the stomata
flowers are the reproductive parts of many plants that make seeds
the stamen is the male part of a flower that makes pollen
the pistil is the female part of a flower that contains the ovary and eggs
petals are often colorful to attract pollinators
fruits contain and protect seeds and help spread them
seeds contain a plant embryo and stored food
the seed coat protects the seed
roots grow down because of gravity and stems grow up toward light
tree trunks are woody stems
thorns spines protect plants from animals that eat them
cactus has spines and a thick stem that stores water and a waxy coating to survive in the desert
= leaf leaves
= root roots
= stem stems
# plant reproduction
pollination is the transfer of pollen from the stamen to the pistil
bees butterflies birds and bats pollinate flowers while collecting nectar
wind pollinates some plants such as grasses and pine trees
after fertilization the ovary becomes a fruit and the ovules become seeds
seeds are dispersed spread by wind water animals and by sticking to fur
animals eat fruits and spread the seeds in their droppings
dandelion and maple seeds are carried by the wind
germination is when a seed begins to sprout and grow
seeds need water air oxygen and the right temperature to germinate
seeds do not need light to germinate
some plants reproduce asexually from cuttings runners bulbs tubers or spores
ferns and mosses reproduce with spores and do not have seeds or flowers
conifers like pine trees produce seeds in cones
flowering plants angiosperms produce seeds inside fruits
= pollination pollinate pollinates pollinated pollinator pollinators pollen
= germinate germination germinates sprout sprouts
= seed seeds
# plant life cycle
the life cycle of a flowering plant is seed germination seedling adult plant flower pollination fruit and seed
a seedling is a young plant that grows from a seed
annual plants live one growing season and perennial plants live many years
deciduous trees lose their leaves in fall autumn
evergreen trees keep their leaves needles all year
leaves change color in fall because chlorophyll breaks down
# plant responses
a tropism is a plant's growth response to a stimulus
phototropism is when plants grow toward light
gravitropism geotropism is when roots grow downward in response to gravity
plants bend toward a window to get sunlight
plants respond to light water gravity and touch
some plants close their leaves when touched
# plant types
vascular plants have tubes xylem and phloem to move water and food
nonvascular plants like mosses have no tubes and grow close to the ground in damp places
plants are classified as flowering plants and nonflowering plants
grasses trees shrubs and flowers are plants
crops such as corn wheat rice and soybeans are grown for food
farmers use fertilizer to add nutrients like nitrogen phosphorus and potassium to soil
plants wilt when they do not get enough water
plants grow poorly with too little light and become pale and spindly
greenhouses trap heat and light to help plants grow
