# circuits switches more
a switch can stop and start the flow of current in a circuit
when a switch is open the circuit is broken and current stops
when a switch is closed the circuit is complete and current flows
a light switch on a wall opens and closes a circuit
a battery has a positive terminal and a negative terminal
wires carry current from the battery to the bulb and back
a bulb has a filament that glows when current passes through it
a burned out bulb breaks a series circuit
current is the same everywhere in a series circuit
each branch of a parallel circuit gets the full voltage
electrical energy can be changed into light heat sound and motion
a buzzer changes electrical energy into sound
a motor uses magnets and electricity to spin
a solar powered calculator uses light energy
power is measured in watts
electric companies measure energy use in kilowatt hours
# conductors examples
a metal spoon a paper clip a key and aluminum foil conduct electricity
a rubber band a plastic straw a wooden stick and a glass marble do not conduct electricity
salt water conducts electricity but pure water is a poor conductor
graphite in pencils conducts electricity
the human body can conduct electricity
copper is the most common metal in electric wires
silver is the best conductor of electricity
# heat examples conduction convection radiation
touching a hot stove burns your hand by conduction
an ice pack cools your skin by conduction
a pan on a stove heats by conduction
warm air from a heater circulates around a room by convection
hot water rising and cold water sinking in a pot is convection
ocean currents and wind are convection currents
sunlight warming your face is radiation
feeling heat from a fire or a light bulb is radiation
a microwave oven heats food with radiation
heat travels through solids mostly by conduction
heat travels through liquids and gases mostly by convection
heat travels through empty space only by radiation

a match head stores chemical energy
fireworks change chemical energy into light sound and heat

the human body changes chemical energy from food into mechanical energy and heat
# motion examples

# sound light comparisons
light travels much faster than sound
light can travel through a vacuum but sound cannot
sound is a mechanical wave and light is an electromagnetic wave
both light and sound can be reflected
an echo is reflected sound and a mirror image is reflected light
thunder is heard after lightning is seen
fireworks are seen before they are heard
# weight mass gravity more
weight changes on different planets but mass stays the same
a person weighs less on the moon because the moon has less gravity
a person would weigh more on jupiter because jupiter has more gravity
mass is measured in kilograms and weight is measured in newtons
weight is the force of gravity on an object
astronauts appear weightless in orbit because they are falling around earth
gravity gives objects weight
the farther objects are apart the weaker the gravity between them

hydraulic systems use liquids to transmit force such as car brakes
a suction cup sticks because of air pressure
submarines change their buoyancy by filling tanks with water or air
a balloon pops when the air pressure inside is too great
