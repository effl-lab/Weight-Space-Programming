# earth rotation day night
earth rotates spins on its axis once every 24 hours
earth's rotation causes day and night
it is day on the side of earth facing the sun and night on the side facing away
the sun appears to rise in the east and set in the west because earth rotates from west to east
the sun rises in the east in the morning
the sun sets in the west in the evening
the sun is highest in the sky at noon midday
shadows are longest in the morning and evening and shortest at noon
shadows point away from the sun and change length and direction during the day
the stars appear to move across the night sky because earth rotates
the north star polaris stays in nearly the same place in the sky
= rotate rotation rotates rotating spin spins spinning
# earth revolution seasons
earth revolves orbits around the sun once every year about 365 days
one revolution of earth around the sun takes one year
a leap year has 366 days
earth's axis is tilted about 23.5 degrees
seasons are caused by the tilt of earth's axis as earth revolves around the sun
when the northern hemisphere is tilted toward the sun it is summer there
when the northern hemisphere is tilted away from the sun it is winter there
in summer days are longer and sunlight is more direct
in winter days are shorter and sunlight is less direct and spread out
when it is summer in the northern hemisphere it is winter in the southern hemisphere
the summer solstice has the longest day and the winter solstice has the shortest day
at the equinox in spring and fall day and night are about equal length
the seasons are spring summer autumn fall and winter
= revolve revolution revolves revolving orbit orbits orbiting
# moon
the moon orbits earth about once every 27 days and the phases repeat about every 29.5 days a month
the moon does not make its own light it reflects light from the sun
moon phases are caused by the changing positions of the moon earth and sun
we see different amounts of the lit half of the moon as it orbits earth
the phases of the moon are new moon crescent first quarter gibbous full moon and third quarter
during a new moon the moon is between earth and sun and we cannot see it
during a full moon earth is between the sun and moon and we see the whole lit side
waxing means the lit part is growing and waning means it is shrinking
the same side of the moon always faces earth because it rotates once each orbit
the moon has craters made by meteorite impacts
the moon has no atmosphere no liquid water and no weather
gravity on the moon is about one sixth of gravity on earth
astronauts weigh less on the moon but their mass stays the same
footprints on the moon last a long time because there is no wind or water erosion
the moon's gravity causes ocean tides on earth
# eclipses
a solar eclipse happens when the moon passes between the sun and earth and blocks sunlight
a lunar eclipse happens when earth passes between the sun and moon and earth's shadow falls on the moon
a lunar eclipse can only happen during a full moon
a solar eclipse can only happen during a new moon
# sun
the sun is a star at the center of our solar system
the sun is the closest star to earth
the sun is a medium sized yellow star
the sun produces energy by nuclear fusion of hydrogen into helium
the sun gives off light and heat energy radiation
the sun is the main source of energy for earth
the sun appears larger and brighter than other stars because it is much closer to earth
sunspots are dark cooler areas on the sun's surface
solar flares release bursts of energy from the sun
sunlight takes about 8 minutes to reach earth

the eight planets in order from the sun are mercury venus earth mars jupiter saturn uranus and neptune
the inner planets mercury venus earth and mars are small rocky terrestrial planets
the outer planets jupiter saturn uranus and neptune are large gas giants with rings and many moons
planets orbit the sun in elliptical paths
gravity of the sun keeps the planets in orbit
planets farther from the sun take longer to orbit and have longer years
planets closer to the sun are hotter
mercury is the closest planet to the sun and has no atmosphere and extreme temperatures
venus is the hottest planet because its thick carbon dioxide atmosphere traps heat greenhouse effect
earth is the third planet from the sun and the only planet known to support life with liquid water
mars is the red planet with iron oxide rust dust and polar ice caps
jupiter is the largest planet and has the great red spot storm
saturn has large bright rings made of ice and rock
uranus rotates on its side
neptune is the farthest planet from the sun and is very cold with strong winds
pluto is a dwarf planet in the kuiper belt
the asteroid belt is between mars and jupiter
asteroids are rocky objects that orbit the sun
comets are balls of ice and dust with tails that point away from the sun
meteors are streaks of light when rocks burn up in the atmosphere shooting stars
a meteorite is a rock from space that hits earth's surface
moons are natural satellites that orbit planets
the solar system formed from a cloud of gas and dust nebula
# stars
stars are huge balls of hot glowing gas mostly hydrogen and helium
stars produce light and heat by nuclear fusion
the color of a star shows its temperature blue stars are hottest red stars are coolest
the brightness of a star depends on its size temperature and distance from earth
apparent brightness is how bright a star looks from earth
stars are born in nebulas clouds of gas and dust
a star's life cycle depends on its mass
massive stars end as supernovas and become neutron stars or black holes
medium stars like the sun become red giants then white dwarfs
constellations are patterns of stars in the night sky
constellations appear to change position with the seasons as earth orbits the sun
stars are very far away and their light takes years to reach earth
a light year is the distance light travels in one year
other stars appear as points of light because they are so far away
stars are not visible during the day because sunlight is too bright
# galaxies universe
a galaxy is a huge group of billions of stars gas and dust held together by gravity
our solar system is in the milky way galaxy a spiral galaxy
galaxies can be spiral elliptical or irregular in shape
the universe contains all matter energy space and galaxies
the big bang theory explains the origin of the universe
the universe is expanding and galaxies are moving apart
# gravity space
gravity is the force of attraction between all objects with mass
the more mass an object has the stronger its gravity
gravity decreases as distance between objects increases
gravity keeps the moon in orbit around earth and earth in orbit around the sun
gravity pulls objects toward the center of earth
the sun's gravity is strongest in the solar system because it has the most mass
weight is the force of gravity on an object's mass
mass stays the same everywhere but weight changes with gravity on different planets
an object would weigh more on jupiter and less on the moon or mars

telescopes are used to see distant objects such as stars planets and galaxies
telescopes collect light and make faraway objects appear closer and larger
