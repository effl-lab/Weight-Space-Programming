# cell organelles functions
the nucleus is the control center of the cell
the nucleus directs cell activities such as growth and reproduction
the cell membrane is a thin flexible layer that surrounds the cell and lets nutrients in and wastes out
the cell membrane is selectively permeable
the cell wall made of cellulose supports and protects plant cells
mitochondria break down sugar to release energy for the cell
cells that need a lot of energy such as muscle cells have many mitochondria
chloroplasts are green organelles where light energy is used to make sugar
the large central vacuole in plant cells stores water and keeps the cell firm
when plant cells lose water from the vacuole the plant wilts
ribosomes are where proteins are made
fungi cells have cell walls made of chitin
bacteria have cell walls cell membranes cytoplasm and dna but no nucleus
red blood cells have no nucleus and carry oxygen
nerve cells are long and branched to carry messages
muscle cells contract to cause movement
skin cells protect the body
root hair cells increase surface area to absorb water
guard cells control the opening of stomata
sperm cells have tails to swim
egg cells are large and contain stored food
stem cells can develop into many kinds of cells
# genetics more
each parent passes one allele for each gene to its offspring
dominant traits are shown with capital letters and recessive traits with lowercase letters
two parents with brown eyes can have a child with blue eyes if both carry the recessive allele
in a punnett square a cross of bb and bb gives 50 percent heterozygous and 50 percent homozygous recessive
a cross of two homozygous dominant parents gives all dominant offspring
a cross of a homozygous dominant and homozygous recessive parent gives all heterozygous offspring that show the dominant trait
identical twins have the same dna
siblings share some but not all traits with each other
traits are determined by genes and influenced by the environment
a person's height is influenced by genes and nutrition
skin color hair texture and dimples are inherited traits
the ability to roll your tongue is an inherited trait
a dog learning tricks is a learned behavior not inherited
a plant growing toward light is a response not an inherited change
huntington's disease cystic fibrosis and sickle cell anemia are genetic disorders
down syndrome is caused by an extra chromosome
color blindness and hemophilia are sex linked traits carried on the x chromosome
females have two x chromosomes and males have an x and a y chromosome
the father's sperm determines whether a baby is a boy or girl
genetic variation comes from sexual reproduction and mutations
# disease prevention
germs spread by coughing sneezing touching and sharing cups
covering your mouth when you cough helps prevent the spread of disease
vaccines contain weakened or dead germs that cause the immune system to make antibodies
washing hands with soap removes germs
cooking meat thoroughly kills bacteria
refrigeration slows the growth of bacteria in food
pasteurization heats milk to kill bacteria
clean drinking water prevents diseases like cholera
mosquito nets prevent malaria
the flu influenza is caused by a virus
the common cold is caused by a virus
strep throat is caused by bacteria
athlete's foot and ringworm are caused by fungi
lyme disease is spread by ticks
rabies is spread by the bite of infected animals
diabetes is a disease where the body cannot control blood sugar
heart disease is linked to smoking high fat diets and lack of exercise
skin cancer is linked to too much ultraviolet light from the sun
allergies are immune responses to substances like pollen dust or peanuts
asthma makes it hard to breathe because airways swell
obesity is linked to eating too many calories and too little exercise
# drugs health
alcohol slows down the nervous system and damages the liver
tobacco smoke contains nicotine tar and carbon monoxide
nicotine is addictive
medicines should be taken only as directed by a doctor
drinking water keeps the body hydrated
eating fruits and vegetables provides vitamins minerals and fiber
vitamin c prevents scurvy and is found in citrus fruits
vitamin d is made in the skin with sunlight
protein is found in meat fish eggs beans and nuts
carbohydrates are found in bread pasta rice and potatoes
dairy foods milk cheese and yogurt provide calcium
the food pyramid my plate shows a balanced diet
# senses nervous details
the eye works like a camera with a lens that focuses light
the iris controls the size of the pupil
the retina contains rods and cones that detect light and color
the ear has outer middle and inner parts
the bones of the middle ear amplify vibrations
the inner ear helps with balance
the skin has nerve endings that sense heat cold pressure and pain
pulling your hand away from a hot stove is a reflex
the brain receives signals from the senses and sends signals to muscles
a stimulus is something that causes a response
# plant processes more
plants lose water through transpiration from stomata in leaves
transpiration pulls water up from the roots through the xylem
plants store extra sugar as starch in roots stems and seeds
plants release oxygen during the day through photosynthesis
plants use carbon dioxide from the air to make sugar
the green color of plants comes from chlorophyll
chlorophyll absorbs red and blue light and reflects green light
plants need nitrogen phosphorus and potassium from the soil
legumes like peas beans and clover have bacteria in their roots that fix nitrogen
plants can be grown without soil in water with nutrients hydroponics
grafting and cuttings produce plants identical to the parent
bulbs runners and tubers are ways plants reproduce asexually
strawberry plants send out runners
a potato can grow new plants from its eyes
spores are produced by ferns mosses mushrooms and molds
# ecosystems interactions more
plants provide food shelter and oxygen for animals
animals provide plants with carbon dioxide fertilizer from wastes and pollination and seed dispersal
when an animal dies decomposers break it down and return nutrients to the soil for plants
earthworms break down dead leaves and mix the soil
fungi decompose fallen logs in a forest
bacteria in the soil break down dead organisms
scavengers like crows and hyenas eat dead animals
vultures and crabs are scavengers
predators help keep prey populations healthy by removing sick and weak animals
a herbivore eating plants transfers energy from producers to consumers
energy is lost as heat at each step of a food chain so food chains rarely have more than five links
the top predator in a food chain has no natural predators
the sun is the original source of energy in almost all food chains
some bacteria near deep sea vents make food from chemicals instead of sunlight chemosynthesis
algae and phytoplankton produce much of earth's oxygen
