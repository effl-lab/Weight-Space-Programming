# heredity traits
heredity is the passing of traits from parents to offspring
inherited traits are passed from parents to offspring through genes
examples of inherited traits are eye color hair color height blood type and flower color
acquired traits are learned or developed during life and are not inherited such as scars or speaking a language
learned behaviors and skills like riding a bike are not inherited
offspring get half of their genes from each parent
offspring look similar but not identical to their parents
environment can affect traits such as plant height from sunlight and nutrients

# genes dna
genes are segments of dna that carry instructions for traits
dna is found in chromosomes in the nucleus of cells
dna is shaped like a double helix twisted ladder
dna is made of four bases adenine thymine guanine and cytosine
chromosomes are made of dna and carry genes
human body cells have 46 chromosomes 23 pairs
sex cells egg and sperm have half the number of chromosomes
a mutation is a change in dna that can be harmful helpful or neutral
mutations can be caused by radiation and chemicals
genetic engineering changes the dna of organisms
cloning makes a genetically identical copy of an organism
= gene genes genetic genetics
= chromosome chromosomes
= mutation mutations mutate mutated
# mendel dominant recessive
gregor mendel studied pea plants and discovered the basic laws of heredity
mendel is called the father of genetics
alleles are different forms of a gene
a dominant allele hides the recessive allele
a recessive trait shows only when two recessive alleles are present
a punnett square predicts the probability of traits in offspring
homozygous means two identical alleles and heterozygous means two different alleles
genotype is the genetic makeup and phenotype is the physical appearance
a cross between two heterozygous parents gives a 3 to 1 ratio of dominant to recessive traits
= dominant
= recessive
= allele alleles
# reproduction
sexual reproduction involves two parents and produces offspring with genetic variation
asexual reproduction involves one parent and produces offspring identical to the parent
bacteria reproduce asexually by binary fission dividing in two
budding is asexual reproduction in yeast and hydra
sexual reproduction increases genetic diversity variation
fertilization is the joining of a sperm and egg cell
= asexual asexually
= sexual sexually
= reproduce reproduction reproduces reproducing reproductive
# evolution natural selection
evolution is the change in species over long periods of time
charles darwin developed the theory of evolution by natural selection
darwin studied finches on the galapagos islands with different beaks for different foods
natural selection organisms with helpful traits survive and reproduce more and pass on those traits
individuals in a population have variations in their traits
organisms produce more offspring than can survive
over many generations favorable traits become more common in a population
selective breeding artificial selection is when humans breed plants and animals for desired traits
farmers use selective breeding to produce cows that give more milk and crops with larger fruit
dog breeds were developed by selective breeding
bacteria can evolve resistance to antibiotics
peppered moths changed color as pollution darkened trees
evidence for evolution includes fossils comparative anatomy embryos and dna similarities
homologous structures like the arms of humans and wings of bats show common ancestry
similar dna shows that species are closely related
the fossil record shows that life forms have changed over time
