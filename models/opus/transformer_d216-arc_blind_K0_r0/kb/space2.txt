# astronomy history
copernicus proposed the heliocentric model with the sun at the center of the solar system
ptolemy's geocentric model placed earth at the center of the universe
galileo used a telescope to discover four moons of jupiter phases of venus and craters on the moon
galileo's observations supported the sun centered heliocentric model
kepler discovered that planets move in elliptical orbits
newton explained that gravity keeps planets in orbit
edwin hubble discovered that the universe is expanding
neil armstrong was the first person to walk on the moon in 1969 apollo 11
yuri gagarin was the first human in space
# sun earth moon size distance
the sun is much larger than earth more than one million earths could fit inside
the sun's diameter is about 109 times earth's diameter
the moon is smaller than earth about one fourth its diameter
the sun is about 150 million kilometers 93 million miles from earth
the moon is about 384000 kilometers from earth
the sun and moon appear about the same size in the sky because the sun is much farther away
the moon is the brightest object in the night sky
venus is the brightest planet in the night sky and is called the morning star or evening star
the moon moves eastward in its orbit and rises about 50 minutes later each day
the moon rises in the east and sets in the west
# day length time
a day is the time earth takes to rotate once about 24 hours
a year is the time earth takes to revolve around the sun once about 365 days
a month is about the time for the moon to orbit earth
earth rotates counterclockwise when viewed from above the north pole
summer has the most hours of daylight and winter has the fewest
near the equator day length stays about 12 hours all year
near the poles there are months of daylight in summer and months of darkness in winter
the sun's path is higher in the sky in summer and lower in winter
the sun is never directly overhead in new york
# planets details
mercury has many craters like the moon
venus rotates backward and slowly so a day on venus is longer than its year
venus has clouds of sulfuric acid
mars has the largest volcano olympus mons and dry riverbeds that show water once flowed
mars has two small moons phobos and deimos
mars appears red because of iron oxide in its soil
jupiter has more than 79 moons including ganymede the largest moon in the solar system
jupiter is a gas giant made mostly of hydrogen and helium
saturn is the least dense planet and could float in water
saturn's moon titan has a thick atmosphere
uranus and neptune are ice giants that look blue because of methane
earth is the only planet with large amounts of liquid water on its surface
earth's atmosphere protects it from meteoroids and harmful radiation
earth's distance from the sun allows liquid water to exist
planets do not make their own light they reflect sunlight
planets appear to wander against the background stars
# stars details
the sun is an average medium sized star
stars differ in size color temperature brightness and age
a red giant is a large cool star near the end of its life
a white dwarf is a small hot dim star remnant
a supernova is the explosion of a massive star
a black hole has gravity so strong that light cannot escape
neutron stars are extremely dense remains of supernovas
the hertzsprung russell diagram plots star temperature against brightness luminosity
stars appear to twinkle because their light passes through earth's moving atmosphere
proxima centauri is the closest star to earth after the sun
sirius is the brightest star in the night sky
the big dipper is part of the constellation ursa major
orion is a constellation seen in winter
light from distant stars left them long ago so we see stars as they were in the past
# space technology
spacecraft use solar panels to make electricity
the space shuttle carried astronauts to orbit
astronauts float in the space station because they are in free fall orbit
astronauts need to bring oxygen water and food into space
space suits protect astronauts from extreme temperatures and lack of air pressure
rovers such as curiosity explore the surface of mars
voyager probes have traveled beyond the planets
radio telescopes detect radio waves from space
satellites in orbit take pictures of earth's weather clouds and storms
gps uses satellites to find locations
the hubble telescope is above the atmosphere so its images are not blurred
# meteors comets details
a meteoroid is a small rock in space
a meteor is the light streak when a meteoroid burns in the atmosphere
meteor showers happen when earth passes through debris left by a comet
halley's comet returns about every 76 years
comets have long elliptical orbits and develop tails when close to the sun
the kuiper belt and oort cloud contain icy objects and comets
impact craters on earth and the moon are caused by meteorites and asteroids
earth has fewer visible craters than the moon because of weathering erosion and plate tectonics
