# states of matter
matter is anything that has mass and takes up space
the three common states of matter are solid liquid and gas
solids have a definite shape and a definite volume
liquids have a definite volume but take the shape of their container
gases have no definite shape or volume and fill their container
particles in a solid are close together and vibrate in place
particles in a liquid are close together but can slide past each other
particles in a gas are far apart and move quickly and freely
plasma is a state of matter found in stars and lightning
adding heat energy makes particles move faster
removing heat makes particles move slower
gases can be compressed because their particles are far apart
= solid solids
= liquid liquids
= gas gases gaseous
# changes of state
melting is when a solid changes to a liquid by adding heat
freezing is when a liquid changes to a solid by removing heat
boiling is when a liquid changes rapidly to a gas at its boiling point
evaporation is when a liquid slowly changes to a gas at the surface
condensation is when a gas changes to a liquid by cooling
sublimation is when a solid changes directly to a gas like dry ice
deposition is when a gas changes directly to a solid like frost
water freezes at 0 degrees celsius 32 degrees fahrenheit
water boils at 100 degrees celsius 212 degrees fahrenheit at sea level
ice melts at 0 degrees celsius
the temperature stays the same while a substance is melting or boiling
ice cubes melt when they absorb heat from warmer surroundings
butter and chocolate melt in the sun
water expands when it freezes so ice is less dense than liquid water and floats
dry ice is frozen carbon dioxide
= melt melts melted melting
= freeze freezes freezing froze frozen
= boil boils boiled boiling
= sublimation sublimate sublimes
# physical properties
physical properties can be observed without changing the substance such as color shape size texture mass and volume
physical properties include hardness density melting point boiling point magnetism and state
texture is how something feels rough or smooth
luster is how shiny a material is
malleable means a material can be hammered into thin sheets like metals
ductile means a material can be pulled into wire
flexible materials bend without breaking
brittle materials break easily like glass
transparent materials let light pass through like clear glass
translucent materials let some light through like wax paper
opaque materials block light like wood and metal
solubility is the ability of a substance to dissolve in another
magnetic materials are attracted to magnets such as iron nickel and cobalt
conductivity is the ability to conduct heat or electricity
# density mass volume
mass is the amount of matter in an object
mass is measured with a balance in grams or kilograms
volume is the amount of space an object takes up
volume of a liquid is measured with a graduated cylinder in milliliters or liters
volume of a rectangular solid is length times width times height
volume of an irregular object can be found by water displacement
density is mass divided by volume
objects that are less dense than water float and objects more dense than water sink
density of water is 1 gram per milliliter
oil floats on water because oil is less dense
a heavy ship floats because its shape displaces a lot of water buoyancy
cutting an object in half does not change its density
= density dense denser densest
= mass masses
= volume volumes
# physical changes
a physical change changes the form size shape or state of a substance but not what it is made of
cutting tearing folding crushing melting freezing boiling and dissolving are physical changes
breaking glass and chopping wood are physical changes
mixing sand and water is a physical change
physical changes are often reversible
# chemical changes
a chemical change chemical reaction produces new substances with different properties
signs of a chemical change include gas bubbles color change light heat temperature change odor and a precipitate solid forming
burning wood rusting iron cooking an egg baking a cake and digesting food are chemical changes
rust forms when iron reacts with oxygen and water
burning combustion is a chemical reaction with oxygen that releases heat and light
fruit rotting and milk souring are chemical changes
mixing baking soda and vinegar produces carbon dioxide gas bubbles a chemical reaction
in a chemical reaction atoms are rearranged to form new substances
reactants are the starting substances and products are the new substances formed
mass is conserved in a chemical reaction the law of conservation of mass
the total mass of the products equals the total mass of the reactants
exothermic reactions release heat and endothermic reactions absorb heat
a catalyst speeds up a chemical reaction
chemical properties describe how a substance reacts such as flammability and reactivity
flammable materials burn easily
= chemical chemicals chemically
= reaction reactions react reacts reacted reacting
= rust rusts rusted rusting oxidation oxidize
= combustion combust burn burns burned burning burnt
# atoms
atoms are the smallest particles of an element
atoms are made of protons neutrons and electrons
protons have a positive charge
neutrons have no charge and are neutral
electrons have a negative charge and move around the nucleus in energy levels shells
the nucleus of an atom contains protons and neutrons and has most of the mass
the atomic number is the number of protons in an atom
the mass number is the number of protons plus neutrons
isotopes are atoms of the same element with different numbers of neutrons
ions are atoms that have gained or lost electrons and have a charge
valence electrons are in the outer shell and determine how atoms bond
= atom atoms atomic
= electron electrons
= proton protons
= neutron neutrons
# elements periodic table
an element is a pure substance made of only one kind of atom
the periodic table organizes elements by atomic number
elements in the same group column have similar properties
metals are on the left side of the periodic table and nonmetals on the right
metals are shiny good conductors of heat and electricity malleable and ductile
nonmetals are dull brittle and poor conductors
metalloids such as silicon have properties of both metals and nonmetals
noble gases such as helium neon and argon are unreactive
hydrogen is the lightest and most abundant element in the universe
oxygen is the most abundant element in earth's crust
carbon is found in all living things organic compounds
chemical symbols are abbreviations for elements such as o for oxygen h for hydrogen c for carbon na for sodium fe for iron
gold silver copper iron and aluminum are metals
helium is a light gas used in balloons
= element elements
= metal metals metallic
= nonmetal nonmetals
# compounds molecules
a compound is a substance made of two or more elements chemically combined
compounds have different properties than the elements that form them
water h2o is a compound made of two hydrogen atoms and one oxygen atom
carbon dioxide co2 is made of one carbon atom and two oxygen atoms
table salt sodium chloride nacl is made of sodium and chlorine
a molecule is two or more atoms bonded together
oxygen gas o2 is a molecule of two oxygen atoms sharing two covalent bonds
a chemical formula shows the elements and number of atoms in a compound
covalent bonds form when atoms share electrons
ionic bonds form when electrons are transferred between atoms
glucose c6h12o6 is a sugar
= compound compounds
= molecule molecules molecular
= bond bonds bonded bonding
# mixtures solutions
a mixture is two or more substances physically combined that keep their own properties
mixtures can be separated by physical means such as filtering evaporation magnets and sorting
a solution is a mixture where one substance dissolves evenly in another
salt water and sugar water are solutions
the solute dissolves in the solvent
water is called the universal solvent
dissolving happens faster with stirring heating and smaller particles
more sugar dissolves in hot water than in cold water
a magnet can separate iron filings from sand
a filter separates solids from liquids such as sand from water
evaporating salt water leaves the salt behind
distillation separates liquids by boiling and condensing
sand and water is a mixture that can be separated by filtering
a suspension is a mixture where particles settle out
= mixture mixtures mix mixes mixed mixing
= solution solutions
= dissolve dissolves dissolved dissolving soluble
= filter filters filtering filtration
# acids bases
acids have a ph less than 7 and taste sour like lemon juice and vinegar
bases have a ph greater than 7 and feel slippery like soap and baking soda
pure water is neutral with a ph of 7
litmus paper turns red in acid and blue in base
acids and bases neutralize each other
= acid acids acidic
= base bases basic alkaline
# conservation of matter
matter cannot be created or destroyed only changed in form
when water evaporates the water particles still exist in the air
when ice melts the mass stays the same
