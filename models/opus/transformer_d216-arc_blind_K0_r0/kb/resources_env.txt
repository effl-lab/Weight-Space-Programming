# renewable resources
renewable resources can be replaced naturally in a short time
solar energy from the sun is a renewable energy source
wind energy is renewable and uses wind turbines to make electricity
hydroelectric power from moving water is renewable
geothermal energy from heat inside earth is renewable
biomass such as wood and plant material is a renewable energy source
trees are a renewable resource if new trees are planted
water air and soil are renewable resources if managed carefully
renewable energy sources produce little pollution
= renewable
# nonrenewable resources
nonrenewable resources cannot be replaced in a human lifetime and have limited supplies
fossil fuels coal oil petroleum and natural gas are nonrenewable resources
fossil fuels formed from the remains of plants and animals that lived millions of years ago
coal formed from ancient swamp plants buried and compressed
oil and natural gas formed from tiny ocean organisms buried under sediment
gasoline is made from oil petroleum a fossil fuel
burning fossil fuels causes air pollution acid rain and releases carbon dioxide
minerals and metals ores are nonrenewable resources
nuclear power uses uranium a nonrenewable resource and produces radioactive waste
= nonrenewable
= fossil fossils
= petroleum oil

cotton from plants is used to make clothing
wool comes from sheep

# conservation
conservation is protecting and saving natural resources
reduce reuse and recycle help conserve resources and reduce waste
recycling paper saves trees
recycling aluminum cans saves energy and metal resources
turning off lights and unplugging electronics conserves electricity energy
turning off water while brushing teeth conserves water
taking shorter showers fixing leaks conserves water
carpooling riding bikes walking and using public transportation saves fuel and reduces pollution
composting food scraps returns nutrients to the soil and reduces garbage
reusing bags bottles and containers reduces waste
using both sides of paper reduces waste
energy efficient appliances and light bulbs use less electricity
national parks and wildlife refuges protect habitats and endangered species
= conserve conservation conserves conserving
= recycle recycling recycles recycled
= reuse reusing reused reusable
# pollution
pollution is harmful substances added to the environment
air pollution comes from burning fossil fuels in cars factories and power plants
smog is air pollution from car exhaust and factories
acid rain forms when pollution from burning fossil fuels mixes with water vapor in the air
acid rain harms forests lakes fish and buildings
water pollution comes from sewage fertilizer pesticides oil spills and factory waste
fertilizer runoff causes algae to grow too much in lakes and use up oxygen killing fish
oil spills harm ocean birds fish and marine animals
landfills hold garbage trash and can leak pollution into groundwater
plastic waste pollutes oceans and harms animals
pesticides kill insects but can harm other organisms in the food chain
chlorofluorocarbons cfcs damage the ozone layer
noise pollution and light pollution affect animals
= pollution pollute pollutes polluted pollutant pollutants
# human impact
humans affect the environment by clearing land for farms cities roads and buildings
deforestation is cutting down forests and destroys habitats
urbanization building cities destroys habitats
overfishing and overhunting reduce populations
building dams changes river ecosystems
humans can help the environment by planting trees cleaning up litter and protecting habitats
the human population growth increases the need for resources food water and land

water treatment plants clean water for drinking by filtering and adding chlorine
only a small amount of earth's water is fresh water available for people to use
irrigation brings water to crops
wastewater sewage is treated before returning to rivers

genetically modified crops resist insects or drought

refrigerators keep food cold to slow spoiling by bacteria
shipping and transportation improvements increased the variety of foods available all year
