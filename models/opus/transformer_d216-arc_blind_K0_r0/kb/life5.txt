# digestive organs more
the gallbladder stores bile made by the liver
bile breaks fats into small droplets
saliva contains enzymes that begin breaking down starch in the mouth
the tongue moves food and helps swallowing
the appendix is a small organ attached to the large intestine
the rectum stores solid waste feces before it leaves the body
the stomach churns food and mixes it with gastric juice
digested nutrients are carried by the blood to all cells
undigested food leaves the body as waste
# blood heart more
blood is made of red blood cells white blood cells platelets and plasma
blood carries oxygen nutrients hormones and wastes
the right side of the heart pumps blood to the lungs and the left side pumps blood to the body
oxygen rich blood is bright red and oxygen poor blood is dark red
the aorta is the largest artery
valves in the heart and veins keep blood flowing in one direction
blood vessels include arteries veins and capillaries
a pulse can be felt where arteries are near the skin like the wrist and neck
blood types are a b ab and o
bone marrow makes blood cells
anemia is a lack of red blood cells or iron
# respiration breathing more
the nose filters warms and moistens air
cilia and mucus in the airways trap dust and germs
the larynx voice box contains the vocal cords
when the diaphragm contracts air is pulled into the lungs
oxygen passes from the alveoli into the capillaries and carbon dioxide passes out
breathing rate increases during exercise because muscles need more oxygen
cells use oxygen to release energy from glucose
# body support movement more
the skeleton has 206 bones in an adult
cartilage is flexible tissue at the ends of bones and in the nose and ears
the knee and elbow are hinge joints
the shoulder and hip are ball and socket joints
the skull has immovable joints
biceps and triceps muscles work in pairs to bend and straighten the arm
exercise makes muscles and bones stronger
the heart is made of cardiac muscle that never tires
# nervous more
the brain uses about 20 percent of the body's energy
nerves carry signals as electrical impulses
sensory neurons carry messages from senses to the brain and motor neurons carry messages from the brain to muscles
the brain processes information from the senses
memory and learning happen in the brain
a concussion is a brain injury
helmets protect the brain during sports and bike riding
# reproduction development more
a fertilized egg is called a zygote
an embryo develops into a fetus in the uterus
the placenta and umbilical cord provide nutrients and oxygen to the fetus
human pregnancy lasts about nine months
the ovaries release an egg about once a month
twins can be identical from one egg or fraternal from two eggs
# immune more
the body has natural defenses like skin mucus tears and stomach acid
fever helps the body fight infection
inflammation swelling and redness help healing
the lymph nodes filter germs
memory cells help the body respond faster to a disease the second time
allergies happen when the immune system reacts to harmless substances
an epidemic is a disease outbreak that spreads quickly through a population
# homeostasis responses
the body keeps a stable internal environment called homeostasis
sweating and shivering help keep body temperature stable
the kidneys help balance water and salt in the body
insulin from the pancreas lowers blood sugar
thirst signals the body needs water
hunger signals the body needs food
when you exercise your heart rate breathing rate and body temperature increase
after exercise your heart rate returns to normal
# plants and humans
plants provide food oxygen medicine wood and fiber for clothing
many medicines like aspirin and penicillin come from plants and fungi
cotton and linen come from plants
paper and lumber come from trees
rubber comes from the sap of rubber trees
maple syrup comes from the sap of maple trees
# animal kingdom details
sponges are simple animals that filter food from water
cnidarians like jellyfish and corals have stinging cells
flatworms roundworms and segmented worms are types of worms
echinoderms like sea stars and sea urchins have spiny skin
arthropods are the largest group of animals and include insects spiders and crustaceans
chordates have a notochord or backbone
primates include monkeys apes and humans
rodents like mice rats and squirrels have long front teeth that keep growing
marsupials like kangaroos and opossums carry young in pouches
monotremes like the platypus lay eggs
# behavior more
courtship behaviors help animals find mates
territorial animals defend an area
imprinting is when young animals like ducks follow the first moving object they see
conditioning is learning by reward or punishment
a dog salivating at a bell is a conditioned response
trial and error learning happens when animals learn from mistakes
chimpanzees use sticks as tools to catch termites
some birds learn songs from their parents
circadian rhythms are daily cycles of behavior like sleeping and waking
nocturnal animals like owls bats and raccoons are active at night
diurnal animals are active during the day
schooling fish swim together for protection
playing dead protects some animals like opossums
# microorganisms more
bacteria can be shaped like rods spheres or spirals
bacteria reproduce quickly by dividing in two
some bacteria form spores to survive harsh conditions
bacteria are used to make yogurt cheese sauerkraut and vinegar
bacteria help clean up oil spills bioremediation
bacteria in the intestines help digest food and make vitamin k
cyanobacteria make food by photosynthesis
viruses infect bacteria plants and animals
a virus has genetic material inside a protein coat
viruses cannot be killed with antibiotics
protozoa like amoebas move with pseudopods and paramecia move with cilia
euglena have a flagellum and chloroplasts
diatoms are algae with glass like shells
yeast cells reproduce by budding and are used in baking and brewing
mold is a fungus that grows from spores on bread and fruit
penicillin is an antibiotic made by a mold

in a lichen the algae make food by photosynthesis and the fungus provides water minerals and protection
bees and flowers have a mutualistic relationship
clownfish and sea anemones help each other
oxpecker birds eat ticks off zebras and rhinos
remora fish ride on sharks and eat their scraps commensalism
barnacles on whales is commensalism
tapeworms living in intestines are parasites
mistletoe is a parasitic plant
cleaner fish eat parasites off larger fish
nitrogen fixing bacteria and legumes have a mutualistic relationship
