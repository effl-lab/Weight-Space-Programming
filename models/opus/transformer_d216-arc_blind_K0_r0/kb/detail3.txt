# cells microscope observations
onion skin cells are rectangular with a cell wall and nucleus
cheek cells are round animal cells without a cell wall
elodea leaf cells show green chloroplasts
a stain like iodine makes cell parts easier to see
cells were discovered after the invention of the microscope
plant and animal cells both have a nucleus cell membrane cytoplasm and mitochondria
only plant cells have chloroplasts and a cell wall
animal cells have small vacuoles and plant cells have one large vacuole
the cell membrane is like a gatekeeper
the nucleus is like the brain of the cell
mitochondria are like power plants
chloroplasts are like solar panels
the cell wall is like a fence or wall that gives support
# heredity examples more
puppies in a litter can look different because each gets a different mix of genes
children often look like both parents
a plant with purple flowers can have offspring with white flowers if both parents carry a recessive allele
genes come in pairs one from each parent
if a trait skips a generation it is probably recessive
traits that are controlled by many genes like height show a range
identical twins raised apart still share inherited traits
environmental factors like diet sunlight and exercise can change how traits appear
a hydrangea's flower color depends on soil acidity
a person's skin may darken from sun exposure but this is not inherited
# evolution adaptation more
organisms with traits better suited to the environment survive and reproduce
over time a population changes as helpful traits become more common
giraffes with longer necks could reach more food and survived to have offspring
moths with dark color survived better when trees were darkened by soot
insects become resistant to pesticides over many generations
bacteria become resistant to antibiotics when antibiotics are overused
whales evolved from land mammals
birds evolved from dinosaurs
species that cannot adapt to environmental change may become extinct
the variety of beak shapes in galapagos finches came from adapting to different foods
# ecology examples more
in a pond ecosystem algae are producers and fish frogs and insects are consumers
in a desert food chain cactus is eaten by a mouse which is eaten by a snake which is eaten by a hawk
in an arctic food chain algae are eaten by krill which are eaten by fish which are eaten by seals which are eaten by polar bears
in a forest food chain acorns are eaten by squirrels which are eaten by foxes or owls
in the ocean krill are eaten by whales
sea otters eat sea urchins which eat kelp so otters protect kelp forests
wolves returned to yellowstone reduced elk and helped trees and beavers
bees are important pollinators for many crops
if bees disappeared many plants would not be pollinated
bats eat insects and pollinate some plants
owls control mouse populations
ladybugs control aphid populations
# human body more organs
the epidermis is the outer layer of skin
melanin gives skin its color and protects from ultraviolet light
hair and nails are made of keratin
the tongue has taste buds
the uvula hangs at the back of the throat
the pupil is the black opening in the center of the eye
tears protect and clean the eyes
the thyroid gland controls metabolism
the adrenal glands release adrenaline
the pituitary gland controls growth and other glands
the spleen filters blood
the bladder holds urine
the ureters carry urine from the kidneys to the bladder
# nutrition digestion more
nutrients are substances in food that the body needs for energy growth and repair
the six nutrients are carbohydrates proteins fats vitamins minerals and water
sugar and starch are carbohydrates
meat fish eggs beans and nuts are high in protein
butter oil and fried foods are high in fat
fruits and vegetables are good sources of vitamins and fiber
milk and cheese are good sources of calcium
whole grains give fiber and energy
eating too much sugar can cause tooth decay
food labels list calories fat protein carbohydrates and vitamins
# plants types more
trees have woody trunks and can live for hundreds of years
shrubs are woody plants smaller than trees with several stems
herbs and grasses have soft green stems
mosses are small plants without roots stems or leaves that soak up water like sponges
ferns have roots stems and leaves but reproduce with spores
conifers have needles and cones and stay green all year
deciduous trees like oaks and maples lose leaves in fall
fruits like apples oranges and peaches grow on trees
vegetables like carrots lettuce and potatoes come from different plant parts
seeds like corn wheat and rice are important foods
flowering plants are the largest group of plants
