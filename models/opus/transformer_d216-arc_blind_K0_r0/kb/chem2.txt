# reaction types
synthesis reactions combine simple substances into a more complex substance
decomposition reactions break a compound into simpler substances
electrolysis uses electricity to split water into hydrogen and oxygen
combustion reactions burn fuels in oxygen and produce carbon dioxide water heat and light
neutralization is a reaction between an acid and a base that forms water and a salt
oxidation is a reaction with oxygen such as rusting and burning
fermentation by yeast changes sugar into alcohol and carbon dioxide
photosynthesis stores energy and respiration releases energy
# energy in reactions
exothermic reactions give off heat and feel warm such as burning and hand warmers
endothermic reactions take in heat and feel cold such as cold packs
activation energy is the energy needed to start a reaction
a match provides activation energy to light a fire
enzymes are biological catalysts that speed up reactions in living things
catalytic converters in cars reduce harmful exhaust gases
# reaction rates
reactions go faster at higher temperatures
reactions go faster when particles are smaller with more surface area
reactions go faster with higher concentration of reactants
stirring speeds up reactions and dissolving
refrigeration slows the chemical reactions that spoil food
# acids bases more
stomach acid is hydrochloric acid that helps digest food
acid rain has a ph lower than 5.6
lemon juice has a ph of about 2 and is acidic
soap and bleach are basic
baking soda is a weak base
milk of magnesia is a base used to treat upset stomach
the ph scale goes from 0 to 14
strong acids and bases can burn skin and should be handled with gloves and goggles
red cabbage juice is an indicator that changes color in acids and bases
phenolphthalein turns pink in bases
# solutions more
a saturated solution cannot dissolve any more solute
a concentrated solution has a lot of solute and a dilute solution has a little
more solute dissolves in a warm solvent than in a cold solvent for most solids
gases like carbon dioxide dissolve better in cold liquids so soda goes flat when warm
oil and water do not mix because oil is not soluble in water
soap helps oil and water mix
sugar is soluble in water and sand is insoluble
# properties used to identify substances
boiling point melting point density and solubility identify pure substances
color and shape alone are not good ways to identify a substance
a substance with a melting point of 0 degrees celsius is probably water
the density of gold is much higher than the density of aluminum
each element has a unique set of properties
# conservation of mass examples
when wood burns the mass of the ash is less because gases escape into the air
in a closed container the mass stays the same during a reaction
when a candle burns the wax combines with oxygen to form carbon dioxide and water vapor
when ice melts in a closed bag the mass of the water equals the mass of the ice
when salt dissolves in water the mass of the solution equals the mass of salt plus water
