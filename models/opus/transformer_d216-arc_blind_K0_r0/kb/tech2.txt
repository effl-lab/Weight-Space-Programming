# assistive devices technology examples
assistive devices help people with disabilities such as wheelchairs hearing aids glasses and contact lenses
contact lenses and eyeglasses correct vision
hearing aids amplify sounds for people with hearing loss
prosthetic limbs replace missing arms or legs
crutches canes and walkers help people walk
pacemakers help a heart beat regularly
artificial hearts and kidneys dialysis machines help patients
x ray machines mri and ct scans let doctors see inside the body
thermometers stethoscopes and blood pressure cuffs are medical tools
# communication technology
telephones change sound into electrical signals
cell phones send signals using radio waves
satellites relay television and phone signals around the world
fiber optic cables carry information as pulses of light
computers store and process information
the internet connects computers around the world
radios receive radio waves and change them into sound
televisions receive signals and change them into pictures and sound
# transportation technology
the wheel made transportation easier
steam engines powered trains and ships
gasoline engines power cars and trucks
jet engines power airplanes
electric cars run on batteries and produce no exhaust
bicycles are human powered transportation
hybrid vehicles combine a gasoline engine and electric motor
improvements in transportation allow food to be shipped around the world
# design process details
the first step in solving a problem is to identify the problem
engineers brainstorm many possible solutions
engineers build a model or prototype and test it
testing shows the strengths and weaknesses of a design
engineers redesign and retest to improve a product
a design must meet criteria and work within constraints like cost and materials
engineers choose materials based on their properties such as strength weight and cost
a bridge must be strong enough to hold its load
a good design is safe reliable efficient and affordable
# building structures
triangles make structures strong and stable
steel beams and concrete are used in tall buildings
bridges can be beam arch suspension or truss bridges
dams store water and generate electricity
levees protect land from floods
buildings in earthquake zones are designed to sway without breaking
insulation and double windows make buildings energy efficient
solar panels on roofs generate electricity
