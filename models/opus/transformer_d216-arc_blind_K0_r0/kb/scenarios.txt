# experiment scenarios
to find out which paper towel absorbs the most water use the same amount of water for each brand
to test which ball bounces highest drop each ball from the same height
to test how temperature affects dissolving use the same amount of sugar and water at different temperatures
to test the effect of light color on plant growth keep the same type of plant water and soil
to compare how fast ice melts in different places use ice cubes of the same size
to test which material is the best insulator wrap identical cups of hot water and measure temperature over time
to test whether a magnet attracts objects test objects made of different materials
to test how ramp height affects a car's speed change only the height of the ramp
to test if fertilizer helps plants use two groups one with fertilizer and one without as a control
a control group in a plant experiment gets no fertilizer or treatment
the dependent variable in a plant growth experiment is the height of the plants
the independent variable is what the scientist changes on purpose
repeating the experiment many times gives more reliable results
measuring the temperature every minute gives data for a line graph
students should record observations in a science notebook
students should wear goggles when mixing liquids
students should wash their hands after handling animals or soil
students should report a broken thermometer to the teacher
# observation inference examples
seeing that the ground is wet is an observation and inferring that it rained is an inference
an observation uses the senses such as seeing smelling hearing feeling or tasting
an inference explains an observation using past experience
measurements are quantitative observations
descriptions of color and texture are qualitative observations
# data interpretation
if plant height increases each week the data show growth over time
if the temperature rises as the day goes on a line graph will slope upward
if more fertilizer gives taller plants there is a positive relationship
when a graph line is flat nothing is changing
the highest bar on a bar graph shows the greatest amount
an average is used to summarize many trials
outliers are data values far from the others
# problem solving everyday science
to keep ice from melting wrap it in an insulating material like foam or cloth
to dry wet clothes faster hang them in the sun and wind
to make sugar dissolve faster stir it and use hot water
to separate salt from water let the water evaporate
to separate sand from water pour it through a filter
to separate iron from sand use a magnet
to make a louder sound hit the drum harder
to make a higher pitch shorten or tighten the string
to make an electromagnet stronger add more coils or more batteries
to make a bulb brighter add more batteries
to reduce friction use oil wheels or smooth surfaces
to increase friction use rough surfaces sand or rubber
to lift a heavy object with less force use a lever or pulley
to move a heavy box up to a truck use a ramp
to see a distant planet use a telescope
to see tiny cells use a microscope
to find north use a compass
to stay warm in winter wear layers and wool
to stay cool in summer wear light colored loose clothing
to prevent the spread of germs wash hands and cover coughs
to reduce pollution walk ride bikes carpool or use public transportation
to save energy turn off lights and unplug devices
to conserve water fix leaks and take shorter showers
to protect soil from erosion plant grass and trees
to protect a beach from erosion build jetties or plant dune grass
to prevent flooding build levees and protect wetlands
# cause effect pairs
heating causes expansion and cooling causes contraction
adding heat causes melting and evaporation
removing heat causes condensation and freezing
friction causes heat and wear
gravity causes objects to fall and causes tides
earth's rotation causes day and night
earth's tilt causes seasons
the moon's revolution causes moon phases
uneven heating causes wind
moving plates cause earthquakes volcanoes and mountains
weathering and erosion cause landforms to change
pollution causes acid rain smog and harm to living things
cutting forests causes habitat loss and erosion
burning fossil fuels causes air pollution and global warming
bacteria and viruses cause infectious diseases
lack of water causes plants to wilt
too little sunlight causes plants to grow weak and pale
overhunting causes populations to decline
introducing predators causes prey populations to decrease
drought causes crops to fail
