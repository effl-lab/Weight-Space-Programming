# moon phases sequence
the moon phases in order are new moon waxing crescent first quarter waxing gibbous full moon waning gibbous third quarter waning crescent
it takes about two weeks to go from new moon to full moon
a full moon rises at sunset and sets at sunrise
a first quarter moon looks like a half circle lit on the right
the lit part of the moon always faces the sun
from the moon earth would also show phases
the moon's surface has dark flat areas called maria made of old lava
the moon has mountains and highlands
the far side of the moon is never seen from earth
the moon has very little gravity to hold an atmosphere
temperatures on the moon are very hot in sunlight and very cold in darkness
# sun more
the sun is made mostly of hydrogen and helium gas
the sun's core is the hottest part where fusion happens
the sun's surface is called the photosphere
the corona is the sun's outer atmosphere seen during a total solar eclipse
the sun rotates and sunspots move across its surface
solar wind is a stream of charged particles from the sun
the sun will become a red giant in billions of years
never look directly at the sun because it damages the eyes
# seasons more
earth is actually closest to the sun in january during northern winter
seasons are not caused by the distance between earth and the sun
the angle of sunlight determines how much energy an area receives
direct vertical rays heat the ground more than slanted rays
in june the north pole is tilted toward the sun
in december the south pole is tilted toward the sun
the tropic of cancer and tropic of capricorn mark where the sun can be directly overhead
the arctic circle has at least one day of 24 hour daylight
if earth's axis were not tilted there would be no seasons
# planets comparisons
the four inner planets are rocky and the four outer planets are made mostly of gas
the outer planets have many moons and rings
the inner planets are closer together and the outer planets are far apart
a year on mercury is 88 days
a year on neptune is about 165 earth years
venus and uranus rotate in the opposite direction from most planets
jupiter rotates fastest and has the shortest day
mars has seasons because its axis is tilted like earth's
earth is the densest planet
only earth has plate tectonics and liquid water oceans on its surface
# star patterns navigation
sailors used the north star and constellations to navigate
the stars rise in the east and set in the west because of earth's rotation
different constellations are seen in different seasons because earth orbits the sun
the zodiac constellations lie along the path of the sun
star charts show the positions of stars and constellations
the stars near polaris appear to circle around it during the night
# space distances scale
the solar system is very large and the planets are very far apart
an astronomical unit is the average distance from earth to the sun
light years measure distances between stars
the milky way galaxy is about 100000 light years across
the andromeda galaxy is the nearest large galaxy
there are billions of galaxies in the universe
# space exploration more
apollo astronauts brought moon rocks back to earth
the space station is a laboratory where astronauts live for months
astronauts exercise in space to keep their muscles and bones strong
in microgravity plants grow in different directions
space probes have visited every planet in the solar system
telescopes on satellites can see infrared ultraviolet and x ray light that the atmosphere blocks
rockets need fuel and an oxidizer to burn in space
launching rockets near the equator uses earth's rotation for extra speed
