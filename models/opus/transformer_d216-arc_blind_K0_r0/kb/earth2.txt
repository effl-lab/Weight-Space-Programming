# glaciers evidence
glaciers form where more snow falls in winter than melts in summer and the snow is compressed into ice
continental glaciers ice sheets cover large areas such as antarctica and greenland
valley alpine glaciers form in mountains and flow downhill
glaciers leave behind scratches striations grooves and polished bedrock
glacial erratics are large boulders carried far by glaciers and left behind
moraines are ridges of unsorted sediment deposited by glaciers
glacial till is unsorted rock and sediment deposited by a glacier
kettle lakes formed when blocks of glacial ice melted
the great lakes and finger lakes were carved by glaciers
long island formed from moraines deposited by glaciers
sediment deposited by glaciers is unsorted with many sizes mixed together
sediment deposited by rivers is sorted and rounded
# rivers streams
streams and rivers flow downhill from higher to lower elevation
a river's source is where it begins and its mouth is where it ends
a watershed drainage basin is the land area that drains into a river
faster moving water can carry larger particles and more sediment
when a river slows down it deposits sediment
rivers flow faster on steep slopes
meanders are curves in a river
the outside of a river bend erodes and the inside deposits sediment
rocks in rivers become rounded and smooth as they tumble and bump into each other
v shaped valleys are carved by rivers
a dam blocks a river and forms a reservoir lake behind it
levees are walls that keep rivers from flooding
sediment particles sorted by size boulders cobbles pebbles gravel sand silt clay
# beach coast
beaches are made of sand from weathered rock and shells
waves and longshore currents move sand along the beach
jetties and seawalls protect beaches from erosion
barrier islands protect the coast from storms
hurricanes cause beach erosion and flooding along coasts
sand dunes on beaches are held in place by grasses
# mountains formation
the appalachian mountains are old and worn down by weathering and erosion
the rocky mountains and himalayas are young high mountains
folded mountains form when rock layers are squeezed by colliding plates
fault block mountains form when blocks of crust move along faults
volcanic mountains form from layers of lava and ash
mount everest in the himalayas is the highest mountain on earth
the himalayas are still rising because india pushes into asia
# earthquake safety natural disasters
during an earthquake people should drop cover and hold on under a table
buildings with flexible frames survive earthquakes better
scientists monitor volcanoes to predict eruptions using seismographs and gas measurements
tornado warnings tell people to go to a basement or interior room
hurricane warnings tell people to evacuate coastal areas
floods can be reduced by levees dams and wetlands
forest fires wildfires are more likely during droughts
lightning can start forest fires
landslides are more likely on steep slopes after heavy rain
# earth history
earth formed about 4.6 billion years ago
the first living things were single celled organisms in the ocean
dinosaurs lived during the mesozoic era and became extinct about 65 million years ago
an asteroid impact may have caused the extinction of the dinosaurs
mass extinctions are when many species die out at the same time
humans appeared very recently in earth's history
the precambrian is the longest part of earth's history
early earth had no oxygen in the atmosphere until photosynthesis by cyanobacteria
# maps directions location
latitude lines run east west and measure distance north or south of the equator
longitude lines run north south and measure distance east or west of the prime meridian
the equator divides earth into the northern and southern hemispheres
the prime meridian passes through greenwich england
a compass rose on a map shows north south east and west
north is the direction toward the north pole
the scale of a map shows distance
a globe is the most accurate model of earth's shape
earth is shaped like a sphere slightly flattened at the poles
new york state is in the northern hemisphere
the north pole is in the arctic and the south pole is in antarctica
time zones exist because earth rotates
# wind local
sea breezes blow from the ocean to the land during the day because land heats faster
land breezes blow from the land to the sea at night because land cools faster
land heats up and cools down faster than water
water has a high specific heat and changes temperature slowly
the coriolis effect caused by earth's rotation curves winds and ocean currents
global winds such as trade winds and westerlies are caused by uneven heating and earth's rotation
the jet stream is a fast wind high in the atmosphere that moves weather systems
in the united states weather usually moves from west to east
monsoons are seasonal winds that bring heavy rain
# uneven heating
the sun heats earth's surface unevenly
dark surfaces like asphalt and soil heat up faster than light surfaces like snow and sand
the equator receives more direct solar energy than the poles
cities are warmer than the countryside because pavement and buildings absorb heat
# water properties earth
water is the only substance found naturally on earth as a solid liquid and gas
lakes freeze from the top down because ice floats
ice floating on lakes insulates the water below so fish survive the winter
water dissolves many substances
earth is called the blue planet because water covers most of its surface
most of earth's water about 97 percent is in the oceans
about 3 percent of earth's water is fresh water
icebergs are large pieces of fresh water ice floating in the ocean
