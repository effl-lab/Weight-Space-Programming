# weather patterns more
clouds and precipitation form when warm moist air rises and cools
air rises over mountains cools and drops rain on the windward side
warm air holds more water vapor than cold air
relative humidity is high when the air is nearly saturated with water vapor
the dew point is the temperature at which water vapor condenses
fog forms on cool mornings when air near the ground cools to its dew point
frost forms when water vapor turns directly into ice on cold surfaces
a sunny clear night is usually colder than a cloudy night because clouds trap heat
cloudy days are usually cooler than sunny days because clouds block sunlight
summer thunderstorms happen in the afternoon when the ground is heated
hurricanes get energy from warm ocean water
tornadoes form from severe thunderstorms
tornado alley is in the central united states
lake effect snow forms when cold air passes over warm lake water
weather balloons carry instruments that measure conditions high in the atmosphere
doppler radar tracks precipitation and storms
weather satellites take pictures of clouds from space
isobars on a weather map connect places with equal air pressure
a weather station records temperature pressure humidity wind and precipitation
# seasons weather
spring brings warmer temperatures melting snow and new plant growth
summer is the warmest season with the longest days
fall autumn brings cooler temperatures and leaves changing color and falling
winter is the coldest season with the shortest days and often snow in northern places
in new york summer is warm and humid and winter is cold and snowy
animals grow thicker fur in fall to prepare for winter
many birds migrate south in fall and return north in spring
plants grow and flowers bloom in spring
farmers plant crops in spring and harvest in fall
# soil more
soil erosion is reduced by plant roots windbreaks terracing and contour plowing
soil contains living things such as bacteria fungi insects and worms
soil texture depends on the amounts of sand silt and clay
sandy soil drains quickly and holds little water
clay soil holds a lot of water and drains slowly
rich dark soil has a lot of humus and nutrients
farmers add compost and manure to soil to add nutrients
soil can be polluted by chemicals and trash
soil forms faster in warm wet climates
# water quality
water pollution can be measured by testing ph oxygen and bacteria levels
fish need dissolved oxygen in water to survive
warm water holds less dissolved oxygen than cold water
thermal pollution from power plants warms rivers and harms fish
wetlands filter pollutants and reduce flooding
a water filter of sand gravel and charcoal cleans water
boiling water kills germs
desalination removes salt from ocean water to make fresh water
# rocks minerals more
granite is used for buildings and countertops because it is hard and durable
marble is used for statues and buildings
slate is used for roofs and chalkboards
limestone is used to make cement
coal is burned for energy
salt is mined from rock salt deposits
graphite is a soft mineral used in pencils
gypsum is used in drywall and plaster
quartz is used in glass watches and electronics
iron ore is used to make steel
copper ore is mined to make wire
aluminum comes from bauxite ore
gems like diamonds rubies and sapphires are cut and polished minerals
mineral crystals grow larger when they cool slowly
the color of a mineral is not a reliable way to identify it
a scratch test compares the hardness of minerals
a fingernail can scratch talc and gypsum and a steel nail can scratch calcite
# geologic processes more
uplift raises rock layers to the surface where they can be weathered and eroded
folding bends rock layers and faulting breaks them
tilted rock layers show that forces moved them after they formed
an unconformity is a gap in the rock record caused by erosion
rock layers can be matched across distances using index fossils
hot springs and geysers are heated by magma underground
old faithful in yellowstone is a geyser
magma that cools underground forms intrusive rock such as granite
lava that cools quickly on the surface forms extrusive rock such as basalt and obsidian
pressure from overlying layers compacts sediment
minerals dissolved in water cement sediment grains together
heat from magma can change nearby rock into metamorphic rock contact metamorphism
the rock cycle has no beginning or end
# human impact earth
mining removes rocks and minerals and can damage land
strip mining removes the top layers of land
reclamation restores land after mining
building roads and parking lots increases runoff and flooding
overgrazing by cattle can cause soil erosion and desertification
irrigation can make soil salty
cutting forests on hillsides causes landslides and erosion
