# properties classification of matter
substances can be classified as elements compounds or mixtures
pure substances have a fixed composition such as water and salt
a heterogeneous mixture has visible different parts like trail mix or salad
a homogeneous mixture looks the same throughout like salt water or air
alloys such as brass bronze and steel are mixtures of metals
brass is copper and zinc and bronze is copper and tin
the properties of a mixture are the properties of its parts
separating a mixture of iron and sulfur can be done with a magnet
a compound of iron and sulfur cannot be separated with a magnet
salt can be separated from water by evaporation
chromatography separates colors in ink
a sieve or screen separates particles by size
# temperature effects
most substances expand when heated and contract when cooled
when air is heated it becomes less dense and rises
when a gas is cooled its particles slow down and move closer together
when a liquid is heated enough it boils and becomes a gas
cooling water vapor turns it into liquid water droplets
heating ice turns it into liquid water
heat makes chocolate butter wax and ice melt
lowering the temperature of water to 0 degrees celsius turns it to ice
water evaporates faster when it is warm windy and dry
wet clothes dry faster on a sunny windy day
a wider container lets water evaporate faster because of more surface area
# light color more
the primary colors of light are red green and blue
mixing all colors of light makes white light
a red apple reflects red light and absorbs the other colors
a white shirt reflects all colors of light
a black shirt absorbs all colors of light and gets hot in the sun
leaves look green because they reflect green light
a filter lets only some colors of light pass through
a convex lens can focus sunlight to a point and start a fire
a concave mirror can focus light like in a flashlight or telescope
a convex mirror spreads light and gives a wide view like car side mirrors
light travels slower in water and glass than in air
a pencil in a glass of water looks broken because of refraction
diamonds sparkle because they refract and reflect light
the moon shines because it reflects sunlight
light from the sun is made of many colors
glow in the dark materials absorb light and give it off slowly
# waves more
waves can be reflected refracted and absorbed
water waves are surface waves where particles move in circles
the energy of a wave increases with its amplitude
radio and television signals are carried by electromagnetic waves
earthquakes produce seismic waves that travel through earth
a slinky can model both transverse and longitudinal waves
microwaves are used in cooking and cell phone communication
infrared cameras detect heat given off by warm objects
ultraviolet light can kill bacteria and cause sunburn
gamma rays are used to kill cancer cells
visible light is the only part of the electromagnetic spectrum that we can see
# electricity safety
never touch electrical outlets with wet hands
do not fly kites near power lines
frayed cords can cause shocks and fires
electricity can flow through the human body causing a shock
rubber gloves and boots insulate electricians from shocks
a circuit breaker or fuse stops current when it gets too high
grounding wires carry extra current safely into the earth
# energy resources compare
coal is burned in power plants to produce electricity and causes air pollution
natural gas burns cleaner than coal but is still a fossil fuel
nuclear power produces no air pollution but makes radioactive waste
solar power works only when the sun shines and needs batteries for night
wind power works only when the wind blows
hydroelectric power needs a river and a dam and can harm fish habitats
geothermal power works best near volcanoes and hot springs
biofuels like ethanol are made from corn and other plants
burning biomass like wood releases carbon dioxide
tidal power uses the energy of ocean tides
hydrogen fuel cells produce electricity and water
# forces more
a spring scale measures the pull of gravity on an object weight in newtons
the force of gravity on earth pulls objects down at 9.8 meters per second squared
pushing a cart with more force makes it accelerate faster
a heavier cart needs more force to accelerate at the same rate
when two objects collide they push on each other with equal and opposite forces
a book resting on a table has balanced forces gravity pulling down and the table pushing up
a tug of war with equal pulling forces does not move
friction helps us walk and keeps shoes from slipping
ice is slippery because it has little friction
snow tires and chains increase friction on icy roads
sandpaper is rough and has high friction
air resistance makes a flat sheet of paper fall slower than a crumpled ball of paper
water resistance slows swimmers and boats
buoyancy is the upward force of a fluid that makes objects float
an object floats when the buoyant force equals its weight
magnets can push or pull without touching
# motion more
an object at rest stays at rest unless a force acts on it
a ball kicked on grass slows down and stops because of friction
a car turning a corner is accelerating because its direction changes
a passenger moves forward when a car stops suddenly because of inertia
objects with more mass have more inertia
the speed of an object can be measured with a stopwatch and a meter stick
speed equals distance traveled divided by the time it takes
a cheetah can run about 110 kilometers per hour
the motion of an object can be shown on a graph of distance and time
a steeper slope on a speed graph shows greater acceleration
