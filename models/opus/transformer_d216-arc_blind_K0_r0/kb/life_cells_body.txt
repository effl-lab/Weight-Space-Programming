# cells
cells are the basic unit of structure and function in all living things
all living things are made of one or more cells
cell theory states all organisms are made of cells and cells come from existing cells
unicellular single celled organisms are made of one cell such as bacteria amoeba and paramecium
multicellular organisms are made of many cells such as plants and animals
a microscope is used to see cells and microorganisms
robert hooke first observed and named cells in cork using a microscope
cells divide to make new cells for growth and repair
= cell cells cellular
= unicellular
= multicellular
# cell parts organelles
the cell membrane controls what enters and leaves the cell
the nucleus controls the cell's activities and contains dna chromosomes genetic information
mitochondria release energy from food sugar through cellular respiration powerhouse of the cell
chloroplasts in plant cells capture sunlight and make food by photosynthesis
chloroplasts contain chlorophyll a green pigment
the cell wall is a rigid layer outside the membrane of plant cells that gives support and shape
plant cells have a cell wall chloroplasts and a large vacuole and animal cells do not
the vacuole stores water food and wastes
cytoplasm is the gel like fluid inside the cell where organelles are found
ribosomes make proteins
the endoplasmic reticulum transports materials within the cell
the golgi apparatus packages and ships proteins
lysosomes digest wastes and old cell parts
bacteria cells have no nucleus prokaryotic
cells with a nucleus are eukaryotic
= mitochondria mitochondrion
= chloroplast chloroplasts
= nucleus nuclei
# cell processes
diffusion is the movement of particles from high concentration to low concentration
osmosis is the diffusion of water across a cell membrane
active transport uses energy to move materials across the membrane
mitosis is cell division that produces two identical cells for growth and repair
meiosis is cell division that produces sex cells gametes with half the chromosomes
cellular respiration uses oxygen and glucose to release energy and produces carbon dioxide and water
cells need energy water and nutrients to carry out life processes
cells get rid of wastes
cancer is uncontrolled cell division
specialized cells perform specific functions such as nerve cells muscle cells and blood cells
inside cells special molecules carry messages from the membrane to the nucleus
# levels of organization
cells form tissues tissues form organs organs form organ systems and systems form organisms
a tissue is a group of similar cells that work together
an organ is a group of tissues that work together to perform a function
an organ system is a group of organs that work together
# digestive system
the digestive system breaks down food into nutrients the body can absorb
digestion begins in the mouth where teeth chew food and saliva breaks down starch
the esophagus carries food from the mouth to the stomach
the stomach uses acid and enzymes to break down food
most nutrients are absorbed into the blood in the small intestine
villi in the small intestine increase surface area for absorption
the large intestine absorbs water and forms solid waste
the liver produces bile which helps digest fats
the pancreas makes digestive enzymes and insulin
enzymes speed up the breakdown of food chemical digestion
chewing is mechanical digestion
= digest digestion digestive digests digested digesting
# circulatory system
the circulatory system transports blood oxygen nutrients and wastes throughout the body
the heart is a muscle that pumps blood through the body
arteries carry blood away from the heart
veins carry blood back to the heart
capillaries are tiny blood vessels where oxygen and nutrients pass to cells
red blood cells carry oxygen using hemoglobin
white blood cells fight infection and disease germs
platelets help blood clot to stop bleeding
plasma is the liquid part of blood
the heart has four chambers two atria and two ventricles
blood pressure and pulse heart rate can be measured
exercise increases heart rate and breathing rate
= circulatory circulation circulate
# respiratory system
the respiratory system takes in oxygen and removes carbon dioxide
the lungs are the main organs of the respiratory system
air enters through the nose and mouth travels down the trachea windpipe to the bronchi and lungs
alveoli are tiny air sacs in the lungs where oxygen enters the blood and carbon dioxide leaves
the diaphragm is a muscle that helps you breathe
breathing in inhale brings oxygen and breathing out exhale removes carbon dioxide
the respiratory and circulatory systems work together to deliver oxygen to cells
fish use gills to take oxygen from water
= respiratory respiration breathe breathing breath
= lung lungs
# nervous system
the nervous system controls and coordinates the body and responds to stimuli
the brain spinal cord and nerves make up the nervous system
neurons nerve cells carry electrical messages signals impulses
the brain is the control center of the body
the five senses are sight hearing touch taste and smell
eyes detect light ears detect sound skin detects touch and temperature nose detects smell tongue detects taste
reflexes are automatic quick responses that do not involve thinking
the spinal cord carries signals between the brain and the body
= nervous nerve nerves neuron neurons
# endocrine system
the endocrine system uses glands that release hormones chemical messages into the blood
hormones are chemical messengers that travel through the blood to target cells
glands include the pituitary thyroid adrenal glands and pancreas
the endocrine system controls growth development metabolism and puberty
insulin is a hormone that controls blood sugar
adrenaline is a hormone released when frightened fight or flight
= hormone hormones hormonal
= endocrine glands gland
# skeletal muscular system
the skeletal system supports the body protects organs and allows movement
bones store calcium and make blood cells in the marrow
the skull protects the brain and ribs protect the heart and lungs
joints are where bones meet and allow movement
ligaments connect bone to bone and tendons connect muscle to bone
the muscular system moves the body
muscles work in pairs by contracting and relaxing to move bones
skeletal muscles are voluntary and heart muscle is involuntary
smooth muscle lines the stomach intestines and blood vessels
calcium and vitamin d keep bones strong
= skeletal skeleton bone bones
= muscle muscles muscular
# excretory system
the excretory system removes wastes from the body
the kidneys filter wastes from the blood and make urine
the bladder stores urine
the skin removes wastes through sweat
the lungs remove carbon dioxide waste
= excretory excretion excrete
# immune system
the immune system protects the body against disease germs pathogens
white blood cells attack and destroy pathogens
antibodies help fight specific germs
vaccines help the body build immunity to a disease
skin is the first barrier against germs
the lymphatic system carries lymph and helps fight infection
= immune immunity
# integumentary system
the integumentary system includes skin hair and nails
skin protects the body keeps out germs and helps control temperature
sweating cools the body when sweat evaporates
# reproductive system
the reproductive system produces offspring
sperm cells from the male and egg cells from the female join in fertilization
the female reproductive system includes ovaries that produce eggs and the uterus
the male reproductive system includes testes that produce sperm
# health nutrition
a balanced diet includes proteins carbohydrates fats vitamins minerals and water
carbohydrates sugars and starches provide energy
proteins are used to build and repair muscles and tissues
fats store energy
vitamins and minerals keep the body healthy
calcium builds strong bones and teeth
iron is needed for healthy blood
fiber helps digestion
water is needed by all cells
exercise strengthens muscles heart and lungs
sleep helps the body rest and repair
smoking damages the lungs and causes lung cancer
washing hands helps prevent the spread of germs and disease
calories measure the energy in food
# disease
infectious diseases are caused by pathogens such as bacteria viruses fungi and parasites
bacteria cause diseases such as strep throat and tuberculosis and are treated with antibiotics
antibiotics kill bacteria but do not work against viruses
viruses cause colds flu measles chickenpox and aids
viruses are not cells and reproduce only inside living host cells
diseases spread through air water food contact and insects like mosquitoes
mosquitoes spread malaria
noninfectious diseases such as diabetes heart disease and cancer are not spread by germs
genetic disorders are inherited from parents
louis pasteur showed germs cause disease and developed pasteurization
alexander fleming discovered penicillin an antibiotic from mold
edward jenner developed the first vaccine for smallpox
# microorganisms
microorganisms microbes are tiny living things seen only with a microscope
bacteria are single celled microorganisms that live almost everywhere
some bacteria are helpful in digestion making yogurt cheese and decomposing dead matter
some bacteria cause disease
bacteria in the soil decompose dead organisms and recycle nutrients for plants
nitrogen fixing bacteria in plant roots change nitrogen into a form plants can use
yeast is a fungus used to make bread rise by producing carbon dioxide gas
fungi include mushrooms mold and yeast and they absorb food from dead matter
mold grows on old bread and food
protists include amoeba paramecium euglena and algae
algae are plant like protists that make food by photosynthesis
= microorganism microorganisms microbe microbes microscopic
