# scientific method
the scientific method includes asking a question forming a hypothesis experimenting collecting data analyzing and drawing conclusions
a hypothesis is a testable prediction or possible explanation
a prediction is a statement about what will happen in an experiment
an experiment tests a hypothesis
an observation is information gathered using the senses
an inference is a conclusion based on observations and prior knowledge
a conclusion is a statement that explains the results of an experiment and whether the hypothesis was supported
a theory is a well tested explanation supported by much evidence
a scientific law describes what happens in nature such as gravity
scientists communicate results so others can repeat the experiment
scientists publish their results and other scientists review them
evidence is data and observations that support a conclusion
= hypothesis hypotheses hypothesize
= observe observation observations observed observing
= infer inference inferences
= conclude conclusion conclusions
= predict prediction predictions predicted
= experiment experiments experimental experimenting
# variables
a variable is a factor that can change in an experiment
the independent variable is the one factor the scientist changes on purpose manipulated variable
the dependent variable is what is measured or observed responding variable
controlled variables constants are kept the same in all trials
a fair test changes only one variable at a time
a control group does not receive the treatment and is used for comparison
repeating trials many times makes results more reliable and accurate
a large sample size makes results more reliable
= variable variables
= control controls controlled
= trial trials repeat repeated repeating
# data
data are facts measurements and observations collected during an investigation
qualitative data describe qualities such as color and smell
quantitative data are numbers and measurements
data tables organize data
a bar graph compares amounts in different categories
a line graph shows how data change over time continuous change
a circle pie graph shows parts of a whole percentages
the mean average is the sum of values divided by the number of values
scientists record data in a notebook or journal accurately
= data
= graph graphs
# measurement tools
a ruler or meter stick measures length in centimeters or meters
a balance measures mass in grams
a spring scale measures force and weight in newtons
a graduated cylinder measures the volume of a liquid in milliliters
a beaker holds and measures approximate volumes of liquids
a thermometer measures temperature in degrees celsius
a stopwatch measures time in seconds
a magnifying glass hand lens makes small objects appear larger
a microscope magnifies very small objects like cells
a telescope makes distant objects like stars and planets appear closer
a compass shows direction north south east and west
binoculars magnify distant objects like birds
= ruler rulers
= balance balances
= thermometer thermometers
= microscope microscopes
= telescope telescopes

liquid volume is measured in milliliters and liters

energy is measured in joules and calories

# safety
safety goggles protect the eyes during experiments with chemicals heat or glass
wear goggles gloves and aprons when handling chemicals
never eat or drink in the laboratory
bringing food and drinks into the laboratory is unsafe
wash hands after experiments and before leaving the laboratory
tie back long hair near flames
report spills broken glass and accidents to the teacher immediately
follow directions and never mix chemicals without permission
point test tubes away from people when heating
use tongs or heat resistant gloves to handle hot objects
never smell or taste chemicals directly waft odors
dispose of chemicals properly as directed
= goggles
= laboratory laboratories lab labs
= safety safe safely unsafe
# models
models represent objects or systems that are too big too small or too complex to observe directly
a globe is a model of earth
maps are models of areas of earth's surface
computer models simulate weather and climate
models help scientists explain and predict
# engineering technology
engineers design solve problems and build solutions using science and math
the engineering design process includes identifying a problem brainstorming designing building a prototype testing and improving
a prototype is an early model used for testing a design
technology is the use of science to solve problems and meet needs
criteria are requirements a design must meet and constraints are limits such as cost time and materials
designs are tested and improved many times
= engineer engineers engineering
= design designs designed designing
= prototype prototypes
= technology technologies technological
