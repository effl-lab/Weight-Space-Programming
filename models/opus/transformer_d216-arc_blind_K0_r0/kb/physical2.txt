# energy everyday examples
a stretched rubber band has elastic potential energy
a wound up spring toy stores potential energy
a roller coaster has the most potential energy at the top of the hill and the most kinetic energy at the bottom
a pendulum swings changing potential energy to kinetic energy and back
water stored behind a dam has potential energy
a falling object loses potential energy and gains kinetic energy
food is chemical energy that the body uses
gasoline stores chemical energy
wood stores chemical energy that is released as heat and light when it burns
a campfire gives off heat and light
the sun's energy is stored in fossil fuels
electric energy can be carried long distances by power lines
power plants generate electricity by burning coal or natural gas to boil water to steam that spins turbines
nuclear power plants use fission to heat water into steam
wind turbines and water turbines spin generators
solar cells photovoltaic cells change sunlight into electricity
geothermal power uses heat from inside earth
an electric current produces heat in a wire
energy efficiency means less energy is wasted as heat
fluorescent and led bulbs are more efficient than incandescent bulbs
incandescent bulbs waste most energy as heat
# heat temperature details
heat moves from the warmer object to the cooler object until they are the same temperature
a hot drink cools as it loses heat to the air
an ice cube melts in your hand because heat moves from your hand to the ice
metals feel cold to the touch because they conduct heat away from your hand quickly
thermal expansion objects expand when heated and contract when cooled
sidewalks have gaps cracks so the concrete can expand in summer heat
bridges have expansion joints
railroad tracks expand on hot days
a thermometer works because liquid in it expands when heated
a balloon shrinks in cold weather and expands in warm weather
hot air balloons rise because hot air is less dense than cool air
insulated walls keep a house warm in winter and cool in summer
double paned windows reduce heat loss
specific heat is the amount of heat needed to raise the temperature of a substance
sand on a beach heats up faster than ocean water
friction between moving parts produces heat
rubbing hands together makes them warm
# electricity details
electrons flow from the negative terminal of a battery through the circuit to the positive terminal
a light bulb lights only when the circuit is complete closed
a broken wire makes an open circuit and the bulb goes out
adding more batteries in series increases the voltage and makes bulbs brighter
adding more bulbs in series makes each bulb dimmer
conductors such as metal paper clips coins and aluminum foil complete a circuit
insulators such as rubber plastic glass and wood do not complete a circuit
water conducts electricity so electrical devices should be kept away from water
lightning rods carry lightning safely into the ground
an ammeter measures current and a voltmeter measures voltage
electric charge positive and negative
static electricity can make hair stand up and cause a shock when touching a doorknob
generators use magnets and coils of wire to produce electric current electromagnetic induction
michael faraday discovered electromagnetic induction
benjamin franklin flew a kite in a storm to show lightning is electricity
thomas edison invented a practical light bulb
alternating current ac is used in homes and direct current dc comes from batteries
# magnetism details
a magnet can be made by rubbing a steel needle with a magnet
breaking a magnet in half makes two smaller magnets each with north and south poles
iron filings show the magnetic field lines around a magnet
magnetic force can pass through paper glass and water
an electric current creates a magnetic field
electromagnets are used in junkyard cranes motors speakers and doorbells
earth's magnetic field protects earth from solar wind and causes auroras northern lights
# force motion details
a push or pull can make an object start moving stop or change direction
the greater the force the greater the change in motion
it takes more force to move a heavy object than a light object
a moving object stops because of friction
objects fall because gravity pulls them toward earth
in a vacuum all objects fall at the same rate
a feather falls slower than a rock because of air resistance
when you jump on a trampoline the trampoline pushes back up on you
when you push on a wall the wall pushes back on you
a ball rolling on a level floor eventually stops because of friction
wheels and ball bearings reduce friction
oil and grease on gears reduce friction and wear
streamlined shapes reduce air and water resistance drag
the net force is the combination of all forces acting on an object
an object moving in a circle needs a force toward the center centripetal force
weight equals mass times gravitational acceleration
terminal velocity is the constant speed of a falling object when air resistance equals gravity
# speed calculations
average speed equals total distance divided by total time
if a car travels 100 kilometers in 2 hours its average speed is 50 kilometers per hour
a runner who runs 100 meters in 10 seconds has a speed of 10 meters per second
acceleration is change in velocity divided by time
a horizontal line on a distance time graph means the object is not moving at rest
# simple machines examples
scissors are two levers joined together
a hammer pulling a nail is a lever
a bottle opener is a lever
a broom and a fishing pole are levers
a crane uses pulleys to lift heavy loads
a flagpole uses a pulley to raise a flag
blinds and window shades use pulleys
a ramp for wheelchairs is an inclined plane
a longer ramp needs less force but more distance
stairs and slides are inclined planes
an axe knife and doorstop are wedges
teeth are wedges that cut food
a jar lid light bulb base and bolt are screws
a doorknob steering wheel and screwdriver are wheels and axles
gears are wheels with teeth that change speed or direction of motion
compound machines like bicycles use more than one simple machine
# sound details
sound needs a medium so it cannot travel in outer space
sound travels about 340 meters per second in air
sound travels faster in water than in air and fastest in solids like steel
a drum vibrates when hit
plucking a guitar string makes it vibrate and produce sound
vocal cords vibrate to make the human voice
a thicker or longer string vibrates slower and makes a lower pitch
a shorter tighter string vibrates faster and makes a higher pitch
blowing harder on a whistle makes a louder sound
a tuning fork vibrates at one frequency
sound waves spread out and get quieter with distance
soft materials like carpet curtains and foam absorb sound
hard smooth surfaces reflect sound and make echoes
earplugs and earmuffs protect ears from loud noise
dogs can hear higher pitches than humans ultrasound
sonar uses sound waves to find objects underwater and map the ocean floor
the doppler effect changes the pitch of a siren as it moves past
# light details
light travels in straight lines until it hits an object
light can be reflected absorbed or transmitted
a plane mirror forms an image that is reversed left to right
a periscope uses mirrors to see around corners
a magnifying glass is a convex lens
eyeglasses use lenses to correct vision
nearsighted people use concave lenses and farsighted people use convex lenses
a camera uses a lens to focus light on film or a sensor
plants appear green because they reflect green light
the sky appears blue because air scatters blue light from the sun
sunsets appear red and orange
a shadow is longer when the light source is low
light travels faster than anything else
light from the sun takes about 8 minutes to reach earth
lasers produce a narrow beam of light of one color
fiber optics carry light signals through glass fibers
the speed of light is about 300000 kilometers per second
# chemistry details
the periodic table was organized by dmitri mendeleev
the rows of the periodic table are called periods and the columns are called groups or families
alkali metals like sodium and potassium are very reactive with water
halogens like chlorine and fluorine are reactive nonmetals
noble gases have full outer electron shells and rarely react
an element's chemical symbol is one or two letters such as he for helium and au for gold and ag for silver
carbon atoms form chains and rings in organic molecules
hydrogen and oxygen combine to form water
sodium and chlorine combine to form table salt
iron and oxygen combine to form rust iron oxide
photosynthesis equation carbon dioxide plus water with light energy produces glucose and oxygen
respiration equation glucose plus oxygen produces carbon dioxide water and energy
a balanced chemical equation has the same number of each kind of atom on both sides
heating a substance speeds up a chemical reaction
increasing surface area by crushing a solid speeds up a reaction
a precipitate is a solid that forms from two liquids reacting
fireworks produce light and colors from chemical reactions with metals
batteries produce electricity from chemical reactions
antacids neutralize stomach acid
vinegar is acetic acid
lemon juice and orange juice contain citric acid
ammonia and bleach are bases and should never be mixed
indicators change color in acids or bases
# matter details
all matter is made of tiny particles called atoms
air is matter because it has mass and takes up space
a balloon filled with air has more mass than an empty balloon
gas particles spread out to fill a container
when a gas is heated its particles move faster and it expands
smells spread through a room because gas particles move and diffuse
food coloring spreads in water by diffusion faster in hot water
the mass of a substance stays the same when it changes state
dissolving sugar in water does not change the total mass
a substance's melting point and freezing point are the same temperature
different substances have different boiling points and melting points
mercury is a metal that is liquid at room temperature
alcohol freezes at a lower temperature than water
salt water freezes at a lower temperature than fresh water
adding salt to water raises its boiling point
