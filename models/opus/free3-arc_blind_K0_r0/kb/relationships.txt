# directional relationships: as X changes, Y changes
As altitude increases, air pressure decreases and air temperature usually decreases.
As depth in the ocean increases, water pressure increases, temperature decreases and light decreases.
As depth inside Earth increases, temperature and pressure increase.
As temperature increases, the particles of a substance move faster and the substance usually expands.
As temperature decreases, particles move slower and the substance usually contracts.
As the temperature of water increases, evaporation increases.
As the temperature of water increases, more sugar or salt can dissolve, but less gas such as oxygen can dissolve.
As air temperature increases, the amount of water vapor the air can hold increases.
As humidity increases, evaporation of sweat decreases and it feels hotter.
As wind speed increases, evaporation increases and wind chill makes it feel colder.
As the mass of an object increases, its weight increases and more force is needed to move it.
As the mass of objects increases, the gravitational force between them increases.
As the distance between objects increases, the gravitational force between them decreases.
As the force on an object increases, its acceleration increases.
As the mass of an object increases, the acceleration from the same force decreases.
As speed increases, kinetic energy increases.
As height increases, gravitational potential energy increases.
As a ball falls, its potential energy decreases and its kinetic energy increases.
As friction increases, an object slows down faster and more heat is produced.
As the steepness of a ramp increases, the force needed to push an object up increases.
As the length of a ramp increases for the same height, the force needed decreases.
As the number of coils in an electromagnet increases, its strength increases.
As the current in an electromagnet increases, its strength increases.
As the distance from a magnet increases, its magnetic force decreases.
As voltage increases, current increases; as resistance increases, current decreases.
As more bulbs are added in series, each bulb gets dimmer.
As frequency increases, pitch gets higher and wavelength gets shorter.
As amplitude increases, sound gets louder and waves carry more energy.
As the length of a vibrating string increases, the pitch gets lower.
As the tension of a string increases, the pitch gets higher.
As distance from a sound source increases, the sound gets quieter.
As distance from a light source increases, light gets dimmer.
As an object moves closer to a light source, its shadow gets larger.
As the Sun gets higher in the sky, shadows get shorter.
As latitude increases, away from the equator, average temperatures decrease.
As distance from the Sun increases, a planet's year gets longer and its temperature usually decreases.
As the Moon moves from new moon to full moon, the lit part we see gets larger, which is waxing.
As the Moon moves from full moon to new moon, the lit part we see gets smaller, which is waning.
As days get longer in spring, temperatures increase.
As days get shorter in fall, temperatures decrease.
As carbon dioxide in the atmosphere increases, Earth's average temperature increases.
As global temperature increases, sea level rises and ice melts.
As the slope of land increases, runoff and erosion increase.
As plant cover increases, erosion decreases.
As the speed of water in a river increases, it can carry larger sediment and erosion increases.
As a river slows down, it deposits sediment.
As rock layers get deeper, they are usually older.
As time passes, a radioactive element decays and less of it remains.
As the surface area of a solid increases, it dissolves, reacts and melts faster.
As the concentration of reactants increases, reactions go faster.
As the population of prey increases, the population of predators usually increases.
As the population of predators increases, the population of prey usually decreases.
As the number of producers decreases, the number of consumers decreases.
As food becomes scarce, competition increases and populations decrease.
As a population grows beyond the carrying capacity, deaths increase.
As biodiversity increases, an ecosystem becomes more stable.
As pollution increases, the health of organisms in an ecosystem decreases.
As light increases, the rate of photosynthesis increases up to a point.
As carbon dioxide increases, the rate of photosynthesis increases up to a point.
As exercise increases, heart rate, breathing rate and body temperature increase.
As a person ages from child to adult, height increases until growth stops.
As the number of trials increases, results become more reliable.
As the voltage of a battery increases, a bulb gets brighter.
As water freezes, its volume increases and its density decreases.
As water vapor cools, it condenses into liquid.
As a gas is compressed, its pressure increases.
As a gas is heated in a closed container, its pressure increases.
As air is heated, it becomes less dense and rises.
As air cools, it becomes denser and sinks.
As the angle of sunlight becomes more direct, the ground heats more.
As cloud cover increases at night, the temperature drops less.
As ocean water temperature increases, hurricanes become stronger.
As elevation increases on a mountain, the types of plants change from forest to grasses to bare rock.
